\begin{solution}{exo:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:1}
The blackout rule: the firm traded the name within the two days before the request. It stops employees trading on knowledge of the firm's own
orders, ahead of them or alongside them.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:2}
$100/11\,600=0.86\%$.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:3}
The \omterm{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:lists}{restricted list} bars trading and is known to staff; the \omterm{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:lists}{watch list} is for compliance only and records names where some part of the firm holds
\omterm{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:abuse}{inside information}. Publishing it would itself pass on that information.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:4}
The 580 alerts exceed the capacity of 100, and analysts read them in arrival order: on average 17\% of the 89 true alerts are read,
$89\times100/580=15.3$.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:5}
Legitimate market makers cancel almost all their orders, and more than spoofers do, so a high ratio ranks market makers first; the spoofers'
signature is the gap between their large and small orders' fill rates and the timing of their cancellations.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:6}
Days 40 to 74: from the crossing until the cleansing on day 75. \omterm{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:pad}{Pre-clearance} only covers the crossed person's own account; the information could
reach the firm's trading or other people, which only surveillance of all trading in the name would detect.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:7}
In arrival order the best rate is 2.5\% and finds 81; the default 5\% finds 46. In score order the default rate also finds 81: reviewing the
highest scores first makes a low threshold harmless.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:8}
More alerts with the same analysts means a smaller share read: past the capacity rate, lowering the threshold reduces the true cases found in
arrival order (from 65 at 0.8\% to 15 at 5\% in the chapter). Strength is true cases found, not alerts generated.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item See \cref{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:abuse}.
\item Effective arrangements, systems and procedures to detect and report suspicious orders and transactions, and notification without delay on
reasonable suspicion.
\item See \cref{dat:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:us}.
\item It failed to implement the 2016 requirement properly, took eighteen months to assess its risks, and could not effectively monitor certain
types of abuse: a \pounds12.6 million fine.
\item See \cref{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:stor}.
\item See \cref{prop:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:queue}.
\item 0.8\% and 65 found with 50 hours; 2.5\% and 81 with 150.
\item 580 alerts, 89 true, 15 found and 85 missed.
\item At the default rate it finds 66 with 50 hours and 81 with 150: the order, not the threshold, decides what is read.
\item Alerts not read are not found: the function's recall is the detector's times the share of true alerts reviewed.
\item See \cref{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:pad,def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:lists}.
\item 964 approved; refused: 202 firm traded recently, 23 restricted, 15 insiders, 6 holding period, 5 \omterm{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:lists}{watch list}.
\item See \cref{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:barrier}.
\item From the crossing until the cleansing; every personal trade in the issuer's instruments is refused meanwhile.
\item An alert closed unread is a case not found, and a record that the firm chose not to look.
\item So that each detector's alerts fill its share of the analysts' hours, reviewed in score order, and adjusted to the measured true-alert rate.
\item Map the product to the abuse it allows, check that a detector covers it before the first trade, and test with planted cases.
\item Plant known cases in the data each year and measure how many the whole chain, detectors and queue, finds.
\item The alert rate whose alerts just fill the capacity, $q^*=C/N$ (0.86\%, best on the grid 0.8\%, 65 found); at the default 5\% the queue misses
85\% of the cases.
\item Size thresholds to the team and review alerts in score order, with coverage checked for every new business and tested with planted cases;
pre-clear every personal trade against dated lists and record every \omterm{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:barrier}{wall-crossing}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:1}
Precise, non-public, price-sensitive information about an issuer or instrument; do not trade or pass it on, and tell compliance at once, who will
restrict or watch the name and record you as an insider.
\iqlookfor{the definition and escalation to compliance.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:2}
To stop trading on the firm's orders or on \omterm{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:abuse}{inside information} held elsewhere in the firm, and to leave a record that the firm checked.
\iqlookfor{front-running, \omterm{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:abuse}{inside information} and the record.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:3}
Entries with the instrument, list, start and end timestamps and the reason, append-only, with corrections as new entries; queries as of a time.
\iqlookfor{bitemporal, append-only records.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:4}
It depends on capacity: at 5\% of a large population the alerts may far exceed what analysts can read, and the cases found are recall times the
share read. Ask for the precision and the queue.
\iqlookfor{recall is not the function's result.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:5}
The controlled passing of \omterm{def:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:abuse}{inside information} across a barrier with consent; the record of who, when, what, the consent, the insider-list entry,
and the cleansing date.
\iqlookfor{consent, records and cleansing.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:compliance-and-market-abuse:6}
Give hours where the next hour finds the most true cases: equalise the precision of the marginal alert across detectors, reviewing each in score
order.
\iqlookfor{marginal precision equalised.}
\end{solution}
