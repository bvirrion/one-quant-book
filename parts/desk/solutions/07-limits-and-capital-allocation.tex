\begin{solution}{iq:fm:limits-and-capital-allocation:1}
A \omterm{def:fm:limits-and-capital-allocation:breach}{soft limit} warns and triggers a review (a VaR at 75\% of its limit); a \omterm{def:fm:limits-and-capital-allocation:breach}{hard limit} must be cured or escalated at once (a loss limit that stops
trading).
\iqlookfor{the difference in required action.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:limits-and-capital-allocation:2}
Each measure misses something: VaR depends on its model and history, sensitivities on small moves, notionals on nothing; stress, loss and
\omterm{def:fm:limits-and-capital-allocation:conc}{concentration limits} cover what they miss.
\iqlookfor{complementary blind spots.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:limits-and-capital-allocation:3}
By Euler contributions, each strategy's expected loss in the firm's tail; they add up to 90. Fifty each ignores diversification and double counts.
\iqlookfor{additivity to the firm's measure.}
\end{solution}
