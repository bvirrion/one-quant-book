\begin{solution}{exo:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:1}
$1.5\%+20\%\times(10\%-1.5\%)=3.2\%$. Without the share, $2.2/(0.032-0.0005)=\$69.8$ million; with 20\%, $2.2/(0.8\times0.032-0.0005)=\$87.6$ million.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:2}
$(2/1)^2=4$ years; $(2/0.5)^2=16$ years.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:3}
The people primarily responsible manage accounts at the new adviser; the accounts are sufficiently similar; all substantially similar accounts are
included unless excluding them would not materially raise performance; and the advertisement discloses that the results were achieved at another
entity.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:4}
Inflows are proportional to assets already managed and scaled by the record's $t$-statistic: a \$15 million fund with the same record attracts less
than a third of the seeded fund's inflows, and its record needs years to convince.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:5}
$7.52-(-2.85)=\$10.37$ million over five years; the revenue share costs \$4.21 million of it.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:6}
Investors cannot recover from operational failures by diversifying: a single signatory on cash, marks set by the trader without independent
verification, an administrator that does not check the manager's records.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:7}
The break-even falls to \$77.7 million; the manager's value rises to \$9.62 million, and the seeder's share to \$2.10 million; the probability of
reaching \$100 million is unchanged (43.2\%), since fees do not affect assets in the model.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:8}
Two years at 1.5 is $t=2.12$, barely significant and weaker if several strategies were tried; investors who wait for evidence wait years, and the
fund must survive until then.
\end{solution}

\begin{enumerate}
\item See \cref{def:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:emerging}.
\item See \cref{prop:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:be}.
\item \$69.8 million and \$87.6 million.
\item See \cref{def:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:seed,def:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:founders}.
\item About $1/\sqrt T$; $1.5\sqrt2=2.12$.
\item Four years.
\item See \cref{def:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:port} and \cref{dat:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:marketing}.
\item Each strategy tried is a chance of a lucky record; the deflated Sharpe ratio (Book 4) discounts for them.
\item Monthly inflows up to 4\% of assets scaled by a logistic function of the $t$-statistic; redemptions of 10\% a month after a 10\% drawdown.
\item 43.2\% and 0.25\%.
\item Seeded: \$30.7, 88.9 and 186.0 million at the 10th, 50th and 90th percentiles; 32.3\% below break-even after five years (64.5\% unseeded).
\item Manager \$7.52 million seeded, $-\$2.85$ million unseeded; seeder \$4.21 million.
\item Serial correlation of returns, capacity limits, the seeder's return on its capital, the buy-back of the share, and investor behaviour beyond a
single inflow rule.
\item See \cref{def:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:odd}.
\item The share's size, its duration and a buy-back, the lock-up, \omterm{def:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:seed}{capacity rights} and information rights.
\item In the model yes: it is worth \$10.37 million to the manager and costs \$4.21 million.
\item Its network, family offices, funds of \omterm{def:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:emerging}{emerging managers} and platforms, each with its own terms.
\item Policies, service providers, controls and a completed questionnaire, and an honest statement of what the record proves.
\item \$87.6 million, reached in a median 32 months on the paths that reach \$100 million; four years for a Sharpe ratio of 1.
\item Accept a seed with a revenue share that falls away after a buy-back, launch with \omterm{def:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:odd}{operational due diligence} ready, and offer a founders'
class; plan costs to survive four years, the time a Sharpe ratio of 1 needs to convince.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:4}
Segregation of duties over cash, independent valuation, a reputable administrator and auditor, compliance policies, and business continuity.
\iqlookfor{controls a small team tends to skip.}
\end{solution}

records.
\iqlookfor{portability conditions.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:launching-a-fund-and-raising-capital:6}
Simulate the fund's assets and fees and discount the share of fees paid away; trade against it the seed's size, a buy-back, a sunset, and the lock-up
the seeder accepts.
\iqlookfor{simulation and negotiable terms.}
\end{solution}
