\begin{solution}{pb:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item See \cref{def:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:flash,def:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:walk}.
\item Each step is the difference of two totals of books, and consecutive steps share a total, so the sum telescopes from flash to final.
\item See \cref{tab:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:walk}.
\item Only the valuation-adjustment rule: \$149\,787 against \$100\,000; the total, \$95\,967, is inside tolerance.
\item 73\,000 shares closed at \$123.49, 1.2\% above a consensus of \$122.01, moved to the edge of the 0.5\% band at \$122.63.
\item A reporting line outside the desks, ownership of the official marks, and adjustments the trader cannot reverse.
\item That the desk's marks are optimistic, its trade capture late, or its fees forgotten.
\item Who signed, when, on what evidence, every exception and its reason; entries are only appended.
\item See \cref{def:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:drag}.
\item See \cref{prop:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:half}.
\item 40, 20 and 8 times a year.
\item See \cref{tab:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:tax}: statistical arbitrage 11.85, $-8.00$ and 3.85\%; momentum 8.77, 6.00 and 7.58\%; value
6.48, 6.60 and 6.31\%.
\item See \cref{dat:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:tax}.
\item See \cref{def:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:cumex}.
\item 4\,170 claims paid from mid-2012 to mid-2015, just under DKK 12.1 billion, none valid; the contested civil claims failed because the
authority was not shown to have been misled.
\item Obtain the approval of its tax and legal functions, and audit the profit as a tax position.
\item See \cref{def:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:tp}; research fees and execution fees.
\item Transfer prices must follow where the work and the decisions are; if the charts disagree, the pricing has no basis.
\item $T_{1/2}=r/(2t)$; a difference beyond the total tolerance, or any category beyond its own tolerance, or anything unexplained.
\item Sign daily only walks that add up and pass the total and category thresholds, escalating every exception on the record; trade no strategy whose
return depends on a tax position without prior tax and legal approval.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:1}
The flash is the desk's early estimate from its own marks and the trades captured by a cut-off; the final includes late trades, fees and
independently verified marks and is signed off.
\iqlookfor{cut-off, fees and verification.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:2}
Build each step as the difference of two revaluations of the same book, in exact units, and report any remainder as unexplained.
\iqlookfor{telescoping revaluations.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:3}
The tax costs $25\times0.20\%=5\%$ a year, leaving 1\%: five-sixths of the return goes to tax. Not in that instrument; trade where the tax does
not apply, if the rules allow, or slow the strategy down.
\iqlookfor{turnover times rate against the return.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:4}
Track it as a pattern, tighten the bands or require observable prices, apply reserves, and raise it with the desk head and risk: the marks are
the trader's estimate of their own pay.
\iqlookfor{independence and patterns over days.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:5}
Section 1256 taxes futures gains 60\% long-term and 40\% short-term whatever the holding period; the same gains on stocks held for weeks are all
short-term.
\iqlookfor{the 60/40 rule.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:finance-product-control-and-tax:6}
Thresholds on the total and each category scaled to each desk's volatility and size, a zero tolerance on unexplained P\&L, trend alerts on
categories, and escalation routes with deadlines.
\iqlookfor{scaled, per-category and trended.}
\end{solution}
