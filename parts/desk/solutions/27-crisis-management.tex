\begin{solution}{pb:fm:crisis-management:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item See \cref{def:fm:crisis-management:plan}.
\item The business decisions around the technical fix: resume or not, what to flatten, whom to tell, what it has cost.
\item See \cref{def:fm:crisis-management:drill}.
\item See \cref{def:fm:crisis-management:trapped}.
\item See \cref{dat:fm:crisis-management:record}.
\item It bought long-dated gilts temporarily, from 28 September to 14 October, to restore orderly market conditions, after its Financial Policy
Committee noted the risks from the market's dysfunction.
\item Customer asset segregation; it protects only if the broker obeys it and its regulator can see that it does.
\item Liquidity risk management for margin and collateral calls, stress tests of them, and \omterm{def:fm:operations:collateral}{collateral management} with enough cash and liquid assets.
\item A 60\% rise in \omterm{def:fm:treasury-and-funding:house}{house margin} over the first day, clearing margin up 30\% and then 50\% at the daily cycles, broker B failing at hour 6 with 60\% of
its collateral trapped, the rest back at hour 48.
\item Hour 6; hour 23 without the failure.
\item Money-market funds (hour 9, \$0.02 million), repo (hour 15, \$0.12 million cumulative), line (hour 24, \$0.42 million), a 10\% cut (past 72 hours,
\$1.42 million).
\item See \cref{prop:fm:crisis-management:monotone}.
\item Hours are not additive and deadlines constrain order; an exhaustive search over small action sets is exact.
\item Approvals that cannot be obtained, cut-off times missed, systems down, counterparties that do not answer.
\item More than one prime broker with lock-ups, segregation and rehypothecation limits, and excess collateral moved out daily.
\item At least the drill's largest shortfall plus the cost of the actions that are slow or uncertain.
\item Yearly and after major changes; the committee should not know the scenario in advance.
\item The horizon achieved, the decisions and their times, what broke, and the fixes with owners and dates.
\item Six hours with no action; \$1.42 million for the cheapest set that reaches 72 hours.
\item Hold money-market funds and a committed line that can be drawn within hours, spread collateral across brokers and move excess out daily;
run the drill yearly and fix what it exposes before the next.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:crisis-management:1}
Hit the kill switch or mass cancel, stop the strategy, confirm the orders are gone, check the position and the P\&L, and call the incident commander.
\iqlookfor{stop first, then assess.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:crisis-management:2}
What it holds of ours and whether it is segregated or rehypothecated, our positions and their margin elsewhere, where to move positions, and our cash
for the calls that follow.
\iqlookfor{\omterm{def:fm:crisis-management:trapped}{trapped assets} and replacement margin.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:crisis-management:3}
Liquid assets that can be sold the same day, committed lines, collateral transfers from accounts with excess, repo, and cutting positions, in
order of cost per hour of survival gained, checking each delay against the call's deadline.
\iqlookfor{delays and costs, not only amounts.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:crisis-management:4}
Run it without warning on real contact lists and authorities, with injected failures (a signatory absent, a system down), and record decisions and
times against the plan.
\iqlookfor{surprise and realism.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:crisis-management:5}
Selling into a stressed market has a large impact, and the losses realised can exceed the margin released; others selling the same positions make it
worse.
\iqlookfor{impact and crowding.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:crisis-management:6}
Choose a subset of actions with delays, costs and deadlines to minimise cost subject to cash being non-negative every hour; a greedy rule ignores
interactions (an action that is useless alone but decisive after others), so a small exhaustive or integer search is better.
\iqlookfor{constraints on every hour and interactions.}
\end{solution}
