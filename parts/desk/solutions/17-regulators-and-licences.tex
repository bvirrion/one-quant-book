\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:fm:regulators-and-licences} --- Regulators and Licences}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:regulators-and-licences:1}
On the text of article 2(1)(d), yes, if it is not a market maker, provides no other investment service and does not deal on own account when
executing client orders. Membership of the exchange is one of the listed exceptions: it would then need \omterm{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:perimeter}{authorisation}.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:regulators-and-licences:2}
Latency-minimising infrastructure (co-location, proximity hosting or high-speed direct electronic access); system-determined order initiation,
generation, routing or execution without human intervention; high message intraday rates.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:regulators-and-licences:3}
45 days to grant or open proceedings; proceedings concluded within 120 days of filing, extendable by up to 90: 210 days in all.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:regulators-and-licences:4}
The exemption from securities-association membership now requires trading solely on exchanges of which the firm is a member; off-exchange
trading, beyond the narrow exceptions, brings association membership and its rules.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:regulators-and-licences:5}
\omterm{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:cftc}{Commodity pool operator} for the fund and commodity trading advisor for the advice; registration with the CFTC through the NFA, with NFA
membership.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:regulators-and-licences:6}
The general prohibition and the licensing requirement apply to regulated activities carried on as a business; whether own-account trading is one
depends on definitions and exemptions the map does not restate, so the row points to the rule and says it must be checked.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:regulators-and-licences:7}
Three rows (\omterm{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:perimeter}{authorisation} as a market maker, \omterm{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:perimeter}{authorisation} for the high-frequency technique, and the article 17 obligations), two regimes, and a
six-month decision period.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:regulators-and-licences:8}
Regulation follows activities, not clients: in the EU exchange membership, direct electronic access or a high-frequency technique removes the
own-account exemption; in the US off-exchange trading brings association membership; elsewhere the definitions must be checked.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:fm:regulators-and-licences:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item See \cref{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:perimeter}.
\item See \cref{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:own}.
\item See \cref{dat:fm:regulators-and-licences:eu}.
\item See \cref{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:passport}; the right to provide its authorised services in other member states.
\item See \cref{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:sro}; a registered securities association and the NFA.
\item See \cref{dat:fm:regulators-and-licences:us}: 45 days, up to 120 plus 90 with proceedings.
\item See \cref{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:cftc}.
\item Pre-trade risk controls and supervisory procedures on the broker's market access.
\item Jurisdiction, activity, status, regime, category, reference, decision period, as-of month and source: so that each statement can be checked
and dated.
\item 2, 5 and 6 rows; 1, 4 and 5 regimes.
\item Six months in the UK (twelve if incomplete), six months in the EU, 45 to 210 days for a US broker-dealer.
\item Every row stale, since each was checked in September 2026.
\item Whether the firm is inside a perimeter, what its application must contain, and what the regulator will ask: questions for its lawyers.
\item Systems and risk controls, business continuity, testing, notification to the home and venue authorities, and the technical standards.
\item The programme of operations, the governance and approved persons, the capital, and the systems and controls the application describes.
\item It controls the preparation; it does not control the regulator's period, which starts from a complete application.
\item An owner per jurisdiction, a review date per row, and a stale-row report read by compliance.
\item Licensing depends on carrying on a business in a regulated activity, and the map does not restate the ordinance's definitions.
\item One regime in London, four with Chicago, five with Hong Kong; the longest statutory decision is six months (UK, complete application).
\item Prepare complete applications in each jurisdiction, UK first since its decision period is longest, with algorithmic controls documented for
all three; keep the map dated and reviewed, with legal advice on every check row.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:regulators-and-licences:1}
Because the exemption for own-account dealing is lost by exchange members, firms with direct electronic access and firms using a high-frequency
technique, which covers almost every electronic trading firm.
\iqlookfor{the exceptions to the exemption.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:regulators-and-licences:2}
The firm must have effective systems and risk controls, business continuity and tested systems, and must notify its home and venue authorities,
who may ask for descriptions of its strategies and controls.
\iqlookfor{article 17.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:regulators-and-licences:3}
Whether its swap activity makes it a \omterm{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:cftc}{swap dealer} (holding out, making a market, regularly entering swaps in the ordinary course of business), and
if so CFTC registration through the NFA.
\iqlookfor{the dealer definition.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:regulators-and-licences:4}
Rows with jurisdiction, activity, status, regime, reference, decision period, as-of date and source; append-only versions with a diff; a schema
check and a stale report.
\iqlookfor{sources and dates on every row.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:regulators-and-licences:5}
The right of an authorised firm to serve other member states on its home \omterm{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:perimeter}{authorisation}; it rests on the directive, so a firm authorised only in a
state outside the single market no longer has it and needs \omterm{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:perimeter}{authorisation} within it.
\iqlookfor{the passport's legal basis.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:regulators-and-licences:6}
US: investment adviser registration, and CPO or CTA registration for futures; EU: \omterm{def:fm:regulators-and-licences:perimeter}{authorisation} for portfolio management or a fund manager's
regime; both with timetables, capital and compliance functions, and legal advice on each.
\iqlookfor{activities mapped to regimes.}
\end{solution}
