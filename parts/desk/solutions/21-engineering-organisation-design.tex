\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:fm:engineering-organisation-design} --- Engineering Organisation Design}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:engineering-organisation-design:1}
Organisations that design systems are constrained to produce designs that copy their communication structures; a design effort should therefore
be organised according to the need for communication.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:engineering-organisation-design:2}
$6.2/15=0.41$ pages a week in both designs of the twenty services, and $4.3/15=0.29$ after consolidation; by
\cref{prop:fm:engineering-organisation-design:load} reassignment cannot change the mean.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:engineering-organisation-design:3}
$0.9\times6=5.4$ hours a week each: 13.5\% of a forty-hour week, just over half of the 25\% cap.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:engineering-organisation-design:4}
Each desk's strategy now depends on gateways, market data and pricing inputs owned by other teams: the edges from strategies to gateways and
market data, and from gateways to risk checks, all cross boundaries, and they carry frequent changes.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:engineering-organisation-design:5}
Embedded: it stays 3 (the equities desk keeps three people). Central: it falls to 1, and the three gateways each depend on one person.
Consolidated: it falls to 2, for the one gateway, risk check and market-data handler.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:engineering-organisation-design:6}
Embedded: on the desks, where the models change. Central: between the desks and the platform, as the owners of the handoff specifications, since
the \omterm{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:coord}{coordination cost} falls on them.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:engineering-organisation-design:7}
A \omterm{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:owner}{bus factor} of 2 for the gateway, the risk check and the market-data handler, and a load of 1.0 page a week on each of the two remaining platform
engineers.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:fm:engineering-organisation-design:8}
Moving the same services between teams leaves the mean load unchanged and concentrates it on small function teams; only consolidating or
fixing the noisy services lowers it.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:fm:engineering-organisation-design:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item See \cref{exo:fm:engineering-organisation-design:1}.
\item See \cref{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:teams}: closeness and speed against duplication, and one good copy against boundaries.
\item See \cref{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:qd}; the handoff from research to production is where models break.
\item See \cref{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:coord,def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:owner}.
\item Three desks with five services each, five shared services, 33 dependencies, 34.5 changes and 6.2 pages a week.
\item See \cref{prop:fm:engineering-organisation-design:load}.
\item Embedded: 22.5 changes a week, 0.43 pages per engineer at most, \omterm{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:owner}{bus factor} 3. Central: 33.0, 0.90, 2.
\item 29.0, 0.67 and 3, with a mean load of 0.29; one copy of each function and a free engineer per desk, at the cost of every desk depending on one
team.
\item At most 25\% of time on-call, two incidents per twelve-hour shift; the busiest \omterm{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:teams}{central team} uses 13.5\% of a week, the embedded desks about
6\%.
\item See \cref{exo:fm:engineering-organisation-design:5}.
\item The services at the minimum \omterm{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:owner}{bus factor}: the gateways in the central design.
\item From who has changed, deployed or handled incidents on each service in the last year.
\item Embed strategies and pricing; consolidate and centralise gateways, risk checks and market data; keep shared services central.
\item Consolidate one function at a time, with its team formed first and the old copies retired only after the new one has run in parallel.
\item Deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate, time to restore service.
\item One failure or one departure stops every desk; keep its \omterm{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:owner}{bus factor} at three or more, test changes with canaries, and hold a tested fallback.
\item At each new desk, venue or major service, and yearly.
\item It adds boundaries and concentrates load without removing any service.
\item Embedded: 22.5 changes a week, 0.43 pages per engineer a week, \omterm{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:owner}{bus factor} 3; central: 33.0, 0.90, 2.
\item Keep strategy work embedded and consolidate the duplicated gateways, risk checks and market-data handlers under one \omterm{def:fm:technology-strategy:teams}{platform team} of at least
three; measure coordination, on-call load and \omterm{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:owner}{bus factor} before and after every step.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:engineering-organisation-design:1}
Systems copy the communication structure of the organisations that build them; for example one order gateway per desk where desks do not share
engineers.
\iqlookfor{the thesis and a concrete instance.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:engineering-organisation-design:2}
Turns models into production code, owns their performance and correctness, and understands the model well enough to change it safely.
\iqlookfor{the handoff.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:engineering-organisation-design:3}
For each file or service, count the people who have changed it meaningfully in a recent window; the \omterm{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:owner}{bus factor} is the smallest set of people whose
removal leaves some part with nobody, approximated by the minimum count.
\iqlookfor{history-based knowledge.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:engineering-organisation-design:4}
Measure which services page and why, fix or silence the noisiest, add runbooks and automation, and rebalance the rotation; two a night is above a
sustainable budget.
\iqlookfor{fix the source, then the rota.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:engineering-organisation-design:5}
For what makes the desk different, yes; for what every desk needs in the same form, no: shared platforms avoid three copies.
\iqlookfor{the embedded-central split by service.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:fm:engineering-organisation-design:6}
Choose an owner for each service and a team for each engineer to minimise a weighted sum of coordination (cross-team edges weighted by change
frequency), on-call load above a budget and a penalty for \omterm{def:fm:engineering-organisation-design:owner}{bus factors} below a floor; an integer programme.
\iqlookfor{the three measures as objective and constraints.}
\end{solution}
