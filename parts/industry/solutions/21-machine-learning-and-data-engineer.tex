\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:in:machine-learning-and-data-engineer:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item As in the chapter's definition.
\item The problem: low signal-to-noise, few independent observations, non-stationarity and a monetary cost of error.
\item The systems that train, serve and monitor models; the contract of what a model package must contain between builders
and runners (Book~12).
\item The path from vendors' and venues' files to the researchers' queries: capture, storage, point-in-time versions,
feature stores, checks.
\item It has no data-engineer occupation; O*NET lists the title under database architects, and most are filed as software
developers.
\item 136 and 1\,051.
\item Poisson counts with log-linear mean; $g=\log(n_1/n_0)/4$, standard error $\sqrt{1/n_0+1/n_1}/4$.
\item 66.7\% (59.4\% to 74.3\%); 21.6\% (20.2\% to 23.0\%); 0.2\% ($-1.7\%$ to 2.1\%).
\item It assumes independent filings; batches from a few employers widen the true uncertainty.
\item Banks: 920 of 1\,051.
\item $-\$17\,000$ overall ($-\$20\,000$ to $-\$15\,000$); $-\$20\,500$, $-\$18\,300$, $-\$19\,000$ at levels I--III;
$+\$2\,220$ at IV.
\item Medians near those firms' researchers and engineers: \$175\,000 to \$193\,000, from ten to twelve filings each.
\item Professional and technical services, finance and insurance, and information; in finance mostly banking and
insurance.
\item The filings do not say; the job may serve business lines paid less than engineering, or be filed at the analyst end
of the title.
\item Bonus and equity.
\item 66.7\% a year (59.4\% to 74.3\%); about \$19\,000 below software developers at levels I--III, level at IV.
\item Mostly not: most of it is at banks.
\item The statistics master's prepares for data science and research-embedded machine learning; the financial mathematics
master's for bank quant and research roles (chapter~29).
\item Which business the role serves, what it ships, who owns the models, and how its pay compares with engineers'.
\item Hiring of machine-learning and data titles in finance grew about two-thirds a year to fiscal 2025, mostly at banks
and at pay below the engineers'. Choose the master's by the job you want: trading research still hires by research
title, and uses machine learning as a method.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:in:machine-learning-and-data-engineer:1}
Shuffling puts future observations in the training folds of past test points and lets overlapping labels leak across
folds; use walk-forward or purged and embargoed splits (Book~12, chapter~3).
\iqlookfor{leakage through time and overlapping labels.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:in:machine-learning-and-data-engineer:2}
A feature computed differently in production; look-ahead in the training data; a change in the data vendor or the market;
overfitting to the validation set; and a population drift. Check the cheap and certain first: features, then data, then
the model.
\iqlookfor{training-serving skew before drift.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:in:machine-learning-and-data-engineer:3}
One definition in a feature store used by both, point-in-time joins for training, and a live comparison of served values
against recomputed ones.
\iqlookfor{one definition and a check.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:in:machine-learning-and-data-engineer:4}
Schema and distribution checks on arrival (types, ranges, null rates, quantiles against history), reconciliation with a
second source where one exists, and alerts that stop downstream jobs.
\iqlookfor{data contracts and distribution monitoring.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:in:machine-learning-and-data-engineer:5}
Only if the gain survives costs and time: test it out of sample over several periods, measure its value in money after
costs, and weigh the maintenance and risk of the more complex model; a 0.3\% gain in accuracy may be noise.
\iqlookfor{economic value and stability, not accuracy.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:in:machine-learning-and-data-engineer:6}
Partition by date and symbol in a columnar format with compression, keep an index of time ranges per file, store raw and
normalised versions, and serve queries through an engine that prunes partitions (Book~15, chapters~3 and~4).
\iqlookfor{partitioning for the access pattern and columnar storage.}
\end{solution}
