\begin{solution}{exo:in:pay-regulation-and-tax-by-location:7}

\begin{solution}{exo:in:pay-regulation-and-tax-by-location:8}

role exist in both places is a separate question.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:in:pay-regulation-and-tax-by-location:1}

\item A legal limit on variable over fixed pay; in the EU, variable at most 100\% of fixed, 200\% with the owners' approval.
\item EU credit institutions' material risk takers; not EU investment firms since 2021, nor UK firms since 31 October 2023.
\item More of pay becomes fixed; the level need not fall, since base can rise.
\item The share of the next unit of pay taken; the share of all pay taken.
\item No: both are taxed as employment income in the year received; withholding may differ during the year.
\item 20\%, 40\%, 45\% above \pounds125\,140; allowance \pounds12\,570 withdrawn above \pounds100\,000; National Insurance 8\% to
\pounds50\,270 and 2\% above.
\item Federal 10\% to 37\% above \$640\,600 after a \$16\,100 deduction; state 4\% to 10.9\% with recapture above \$107\,650;
city 3.078\% to 3.876\%; Social Security 6.2\% to \$184\,500; Medicare 1.45\% plus 0.9\% above \$200\,000.
\item 35.75\%, 37.56\%, 49.5\% above \euro78\,426; up to 30\% of wages tax-free for five years (27\% from 2027), capped at
\euro262\,000 of wages.
\item Federal tax to 11.5\% of all income; cantonal simple tax to 13\% times 2.14; social insurance 5.3\% and 1.1\% to CHF
148\,200.
\item Singapore 0\% to 24\%; Hong Kong the lower of 2\%--17\% after allowances and 15\%--16\% of income, plus MPF; Dubai no tax.
\item GBP 758\,235; USD 1\,000\,000 (New York, Chicago, Dubai); EUR 884\,969; CHF 829\,243; SGD 1\,305\,833; HKD 7\,796\,944.
\item Dubai \$1\,000\,000 (0\%, 0\%); Hong Kong \$844\,104 (15.6\%, 16.0\%); Singapore \$791\,283 (20.9\%, 24.0\%); Zurich
\$599\,515 (40.0\%, 44.4\%); Chicago \$597\,361 (40.3\%, 44.3\%); London \$545\,545 (45.4\%, 47.0\%); New York \$540\,584

\item \$43\,964: net \$560\,341.

\item When it vests or is received, at its value then; the vesting year's rate applies, the value at vesting is taxed, and

\begin{solution}{iq:in:pay-regulation-and-tax-by-location:1}
Walk the brackets, taxing the part of income inside each at its rate; stop when income is below a bracket's start. Test
at zero, at each edge, inside each band, far above the top, and against a published example.
\iqlookfor{edge cases and an external oracle.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:in:pay-regulation-and-tax-by-location:2}
The regulators argued that it raised fixed pay and so reduced the part of pay that can absorb losses; the EU kept the cap
for credit institutions.
\iqlookfor{the fixed-pay effect, and who is still covered.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:in:pay-regulation-and-tax-by-location:3}
A curve flattens when a cap on the average binds (Hong Kong's standard rate) or the top rate is reached early; it keeps
rising where a high top bracket starts late or where a recapture moves the whole of income to a higher rate (New York).
\iqlookfor{the shape of the schedule, not just its top rate.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:in:pay-regulation-and-tax-by-location:4}
Taxed at the year with the lower marginal rate; with deferral, the vesting year's rate applies, which is worse if income
rises and better if it falls (for example after leaving, when vesting continues as a good leaver).
\iqlookfor{timing arbitrage and its dependence on future income.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:in:pay-regulation-and-tax-by-location:5}
As dated rows keyed by jurisdiction, parameter and effective period, with the source for each; never overwrite, add new
periods; the calculation takes a date and reads the rows in force.
\iqlookfor{effective-dated, sourced, append-only parameters.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:in:pay-regulation-and-tax-by-location:6}
For each omitted item, bound its size (the largest pension deduction allowed, the largest allowance) and its rate (the
marginal rate), and add the effects; the error is largest where deductions are large and rates high, and where family
status changes brackets.
\iqlookfor{bounding by the largest allowed item times the marginal rate.}
\end{solution}
