\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology} --- Surveillance and Compliance Technology}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:1}
Sixteen: two analysts reviewing eight each. The queue stays empty at 0.2\% (4.8 alerts a day) and 0.5\% (10.7); at 1\% (21.2) it grows.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:2}
Because the report itself is well formed: it describes the trade as it was first booked, with valid identifiers, a positive quantity and
price. It is wrong only against the trade as it now stands, which only \omterm{def:pl:post-trade-systems:recon}{reconciliation} compares.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:3}
Each of the 41 amended trades is reported three times --- as first booked, cancelled, and as amended --- so 2\,500 trades need 2\,582 reports;
netting the cancellations leaves one live report per trade.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:4}
Planted episodes score far above most false positives, so a queue sorted by score reviews them the day they appear while the backlog fills with
low-scoring alerts. The cost is that those low-scoring alerts may never be reviewed; a firm must still close them, by rule or by sampling,
and watch that the backlog does not hide a new pattern with modest scores.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:5}
Because every pointer both find is a phrase that moves a conversation (\textquotedbl{}take this to whatsapp\textquotedbl{}), which the narrow
lexicon matches exactly; the broad one also matches every ordinary message that happens to contain the word --- 454 false hits for the same
ten pointers.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:6}
Because a message that was never captured cannot be found by any search: fifteen of the forty conversations left no trace at all. Search
statistics measure what is in the archive; only capture measures what is not.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:7}
With twenty-four reviews a day against 21.2 alerts, the queue empties every day: no backlog at the quarter's end, and all 38 alerted episodes
reviewed within five days, even oldest first.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:8}
The lexicon found the conversations that left a pointer phrased as it expects; it cannot find those that left a pointer phrased otherwise or
no pointer at all. In the chapter's quarter it found ten of forty. The claim confuses recall within the archive with coverage of the
conversations.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Orders and trades into account-day features, scores, alerts and cases; messages into lexicon hits; trades into validated, reconciled
transaction reports.
\item On a separate calibration quarter, at a false-positive rate of legitimate account-days: 0.2\%, 0.5\% or 1\%.
\item 45 planted; 32, 36 and 38 alerted.
\item 4.8, 10.7 and 21.2.
\item Sixteen alerts a day.
\item Empty every day at 0.2\% and 0.5\%; at 1\% it grows to 330.
\item 28 reviewed by the quarter's end, 11 within five days.
\item 37 of 38, all within five days.
\item Seven days.
\item Each alert's state, owner, evidence, decision with reason, and age.
\item Forty; twenty-five leave a pointer.
\item Both find the same ten pointers; the broad one flags 464 messages, the narrow one 10.
\item Missing client LEIs, mistyped ISINs, unreported amendments, and batches sent twice.
\item 41 reports fail validation, 6 are duplicates, and 40 misstate an amended trade: 81 of 2\,500 trades unreported or misreported.
\item Amendments become cancellations and new reports, identifiers come from reference data: nothing fails.
\item \textbf{Named result.} With two analysts reviewing sixteen alerts a day, thresholds at false-positive rates of 0.2\%, 0.5\% and 1\% leave
backlogs of 0, 0 and 330 alerts after a quarter and alert 32, 36 and 38 of 45 planted episodes; at 1\%, 11 are reviewed within five days oldest
first and 37 highest score first. The legacy report generator leaves 3.2\% of trades unreported or misreported (41 failing validation, 40 failing
\omterm{def:pl:post-trade-systems:recon}{reconciliation}) and 6 duplicates; the event-sourced generator, none.
\item 0.5\% with the queue ordered by score, or 1\% with a third analyst.
\item That no search can cover channels the firm does not capture, and what capture and attestation cover.
\item Plant episodes, off-channel pointers and report faults on a schedule and check that each comes out of the pipeline where it should.
\item Coverage, capacity and quality measured, not assumed.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:1}
Trade reporting publishes trades to the market after execution (prices and sizes, for transparency); \omterm{def:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:reporting}{transaction reporting} sends the
regulator a confidential record of every transaction with the parties' identities, for surveillance.
\iqlookfor{Public transparency against confidential surveillance.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:2}
Measure alerts a day against capacity per detector; raise thresholds or improve detectors where false positives dominate; order the queue by
risk; close low-risk alerts by documented rules or sampling; staff to the chosen threshold.
\iqlookfor{Threshold as a capacity decision; risk-ordered queue.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:3}
Generate reports from the event-sourced trade store, so an amendment is a cancellation and a new report; validate before sending; reconcile
the net live reports with the trades every day.
\iqlookfor{Events and daily \omterm{def:pl:post-trade-systems:recon}{reconciliation}.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:4}
True-positive rate at a fixed false-positive rate on planted or labelled episodes, the share of each legitimate account type it flags, and
precision at the real base rate; then end-to-end, whether its alerts are reviewed in time.
\iqlookfor{Detection at a stated false-alarm rate, then in the queue.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:5}
Feature extraction from orders and trades; detectors with thresholds set against capacity; a \omterm{def:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:case}{case management system} with risk-ordered queues
and ageing; a \omterm{def:pl:surveillance-and-compliance-technology:archive}{communications archive} capturing all approved channels with holds and lexicon or model search; transaction reports from the
event-sourced trade store with validation and daily \omterm{def:pl:post-trade-systems:recon}{reconciliation}; planted \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:tests}{end-to-end tests} and published measures.
\iqlookfor{Pipeline, people, archive, reports, measures.}
\end{solution}
