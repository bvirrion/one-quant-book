\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:pl:position-and-pnl-services} --- Position and P\&L Services}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:position-and-pnl-services:1}
The execution counted once: the service keys executions by their identifier, so the second and third reports only record that the drop copy
has seen it (and the status becomes agreed).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:position-and-pnl-services:2}
Short 200 shares. Realised on the 100 closed: $100 \times \$0.02 = \$2$; the 200 opened short at \$100.02 are marked at \$100.00: unrealised
$+\$4$. Total \$6, which is also cash ($-\$10\,000 + \$30\,006$) plus quantity times mark ($-\$20\,000$).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:position-and-pnl-services:3}
So that the position before the notice can still be computed as it was known then --- what risk and the strategy saw --- and the audit shows
who removed what and when; deleting it would rewrite history and make every earlier report unreproducible.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:position-and-pnl-services:4}
In four seconds a quoting strategy's resting orders trade only if an aggressive order arrives at their price: in six sessions none did. Longer
outages, larger or more aggressive orders, or a busier market (the open, news) would lose more.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:position-and-pnl-services:5}
On Friday evening the trade-date view includes it and the settlement-date view does not; it settles on Monday (the next business day), from
when both include it.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:position-and-pnl-services:6}
Because it is the only signal that the session lost messages: without it the session's view is wrong and nobody knows. Pending prompts the
replay, tells risk to use the union of the sources, and marks the P\&L as provisional.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:position-and-pnl-services:7}
With cancel on disconnect the strategy's orders are cancelled when the session drops, so nothing trades during the outage: no session loses an
execution in any of the ten sessions, and the session-only service's error is the bust's alone, 220 shares on average, like the merged
service's. Cancel on disconnect removes the problem at its source, at the price of leaving the book for a few seconds.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:position-and-pnl-services:8}
Adding fills as they arrive counts duplicates twice and misses lost ones, and the error persists all day, when risk and limits use it; the
morning reset hides the error instead of finding it. Key executions by identifier, merge the drop copy, keep busts as events, reconcile, and
explain every break before resetting anything.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:pl:position-and-pnl-services:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Executions keyed by identifier, busts and corrections as events; a position is the replay of the executions in force at a time through
the exact keeper.
\item The \omterm{def:pl:position-and-pnl-services:execid}{execution identifier}.
\item Agreed when every source has reported the same executions; pending otherwise.
\item As events with their own times: removed or replaced from their time on, the earlier position still computable.
\item Integer quantity and cash in \texttt{firm.pnl}; the mark applied only at the end.
\item It logs back in asking only for new messages; the reports generated while it was down are skipped unless a replay is requested.
\item Every execution on a second path, 50~milliseconds late; one more connection to run and reconcile.
\item A request to the venue to resend the session's messages from a sequence number; it brings the session back into agreement.
\item The executions the clearing firm cleared, busts removed: the day's truth.
\item The merged service with the bust from the bust notice on; the others never before the statement.
\item In four of the ten: one, four, three and one executions.
\item 60 to 63~seconds, until the replay.
\item Statement $-3\,300$ shares and $-\$3\,493$; session only 600 shares and \$252 off; merged 100 and \$47 off; merged with the bust exact.
\item 290 shares and \$52.4 without the drop copy; 220 and \$28.4 with it; 0 with the bust.
\item The lost executions; not the bust, which only the venue's notice removes.
\item \textbf{Named result.} At the close, averaged over ten sessions: 290 shares and \$52.4 off the clearing statement from the session alone,
220 and \$28.4 with the drop copy, none with the drop copy and the bust applied; the complete service agrees with the statement from the bust
notice on, twenty minutes after the busted trade, and was pending for about a minute after each outage that lost executions.
\item Use the union of the sources --- the larger exposure --- and flag it until it agrees.
\item Reconcile with the clearing statement, explain every break, then roll the end-of-day positions into the start-of-day.
\item Pending time per account, breaks at reconciliation, lag between sources, and executions seen by one source only.
\item That it is computed from every execution, once, and agrees with an independent record.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:position-and-pnl-services:1}
Key executions by the venue's \omterm{def:pl:position-and-pnl-services:execid}{execution identifier} and make applying one idempotent: a second report of a known identifier changes nothing
but the record of which sources have seen it.
\iqlookfor{Idempotency on a venue identifier.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:position-and-pnl-services:2}
Compare the session's executions with the drop copy's: any execution in one and not the other is a gap; request a replay from the last
sequence number received; until the sources agree, treat the position as pending and use the union.
\iqlookfor{A second source and a replay.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:position-and-pnl-services:3}
Positions are a function of executions; storing executions makes arrival order and duplicates harmless, keeps busts and corrections as
history, and lets any past position be recomputed and explained. A stored position can only be patched.
\iqlookfor{Event sourcing.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:position-and-pnl-services:4}
Trade-date counts an execution when it trades (risk, P\&L, limits); settlement-date when it settles (cash, custody, financing, some regulatory
reports). They differ by the settlement cycle.
\iqlookfor{Who uses which view.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:position-and-pnl-services:5}
Executions from every session and drop copy into an event store keyed by \omterm{def:pl:position-and-pnl-services:execid}{execution identifier}, with busts, corrections and allocations as
events; positions and exact P\&L computed by replay, by account and instrument, trade and settlement date, intraday and \omterm{def:pl:position-and-pnl-services:marks}{end-of-day marks} from
the valuation policy; statuses and alerts when sources disagree; reconciliation with the clearing statement before the start-of-day roll.
\iqlookfor{Events, idempotency, two sources, reconciliation.}
\end{solution}
