\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:pl:real-time-risk} --- Real-Time Risk}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:real-time-risk:1}
$-0.4 \times 1\,000 \times 5\,000 = -\$2$~million of delta. Under 90\% of \$50~million (\$45~million), 22 such trades fit, before gamma and the
market move the deltas.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:real-time-risk:2}
The library computes delta and gamma by central differences with bumps of $\pm1\%$ of the spot, so the quadratic $\Delta\,\mathrm{d}S +
\frac12\Gamma\,\mathrm{d}S^2$ passes exactly through the two repriced points: at $\pm1\%$ it is the full revaluation.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:real-time-risk:3}
That its desk would stay at $-\$45.2$~million, inside its limit, and the firm would go to $-\$92.8$~million, breaching the firm's. Desk~B's
system knows only desk~B's trades; the firm's number needs desk~A's too.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:real-time-risk:4}
The quadratic keeps gamma constant. A long put's gamma falls as the index rises and the put goes out of the money, and its value flattens
towards zero, so the quadratic, still curving, misstates the loss of value more and more on the upside; on the downside the puts go into the
money and behave more linearly, closer to the quadratic.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:real-time-risk:5}
Acceptable while the spot stays within the refresh threshold of the cache's spot (then the carried delta is accurate to the threshold's
error) and nothing else the sensitivities depend on has moved; not acceptable after a volatility shock, a large move in a correlated factor, a
change of the book the cache has not seen, or time passing near expiry.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:real-time-risk:6}
Because the aggregator never recomputes the tree, an error in one difference --- a contribution not updated, an old value subtracted twice,
an ancestor missed --- persists and accumulates silently. Comparing with a rebuild after fills, ticks and refreshes catches any such drift.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:real-time-risk:7}
At 0.1\% the cache is repriced 105 times, at 2\% once; the firm's exposure at the close is $-\$90.837$~million and $-\$90.832$~million, and the
breach time is the same, 14:23:50. For this book and afternoon the threshold changes the cost, not the answer; a larger move or a shorter-dated
book would change that.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:real-time-risk:8}
Desk limits bound each desk at the moment of each trade; exposures drift with the market after trades (gamma), limits are often set so that
their sum exceeds the firm's appetite, and even when the sum is below it, desks' exposures in correlated underlyings can compound while
netting in the limit arithmetic does not. Only an aggregate computed across desks, continuously, shows the firm's number.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:pl:real-time-risk:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Seconds and the whole firm, between the nightly engine (hours, full revaluation) and the gate (microseconds, one order).
\item Trade delta-dollars summed up the hierarchy; desks are not expected to use their limits together, so the firm's appetite is less than
their sum.
\item A fill adds its new contribution minus its old to every ancestor; a tick adds, per trade, the change of its carried delta-dollars.
\item When the spot has moved more than 0.5\% since the cache was filled; by repricing every instrument with the pricing library.
\item Exposures, the market time, the cache's spot and age, and the breached limits.
\item Desk~A bought puts, 1\,000 at a time; desk~B sold futures, 400 at a time; each checked that its own exposure stayed under 90\% of its limit.
\item $-\$49.3$~million and $-\$43.6$~million.
\item At 14:23:50.
\item $-\$90.8$~million, 1.82 times.
\item Desk~B inside at $-\$45.2$~million, the firm at $-\$92.8$~million, breaching.
\item Delta-gamma P\&L from Greeks computed at the close against the change of full values, for moves of $-10\%$ to $+10\%$.
\item $-6.75\%$ and $+3.5\%$.
\item 3.1\% and 23.6\%.
\item The Greeks were computed from bumps of $\pm1\%$.
\item They come from full revaluation.
\item \textbf{Named result.} The firm's limit was breached at 14:23:50 while neither desk exceeded its own (largest $-\$49.3$~million and
$-\$43.6$~million against \$50~million); at the close the firm was $-\$90.8$~million, 1.82 times its appetite; the cache's P\&L error exceeds
1\% of full revaluation beyond a move of $+3.5\%$ (or $-6.75\%$).
\item A risk manager with authority over both desks, able to block risk-increasing orders at the gate and cut a limit through the \omterm{def:pl:signal-serving-and-parameter-management:store}{parameter
store}.
\item From the approximation error: small enough that the error stays well under the precision limits are set with (here, under 1\% up to 3.5\%),
and large enough that repricing costs are bounded.
\item Vega, rates and credit sensitivities, gross and net notional, P\&L against loss limits, and concentrations by issuer.
\item Because a limit breached across desks is created while they trade and is only useful to know while it can still be stopped.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:real-time-risk:1}
Desk limits are set on each desk alone and usually add up to more than the firm's appetite; exposures in the same direction and underlying
add, and gamma moves exposures after the trades.
\iqlookfor{Aggregation across desks.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:real-time-risk:2}
\omterm{def:pl:real-time-risk:incremental}{Incremental aggregation} up a hierarchy: each event adds its difference to its ancestors; sensitivities cached and refreshed on thresholds;
limits checked on the updated nodes only; snapshots with timestamps; a periodic rebuild as a check.
\iqlookfor{Differences, not recomputation; caches with invalidation.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:real-time-risk:3}
For moves within the range where its error is small against the decisions made with it --- here under 1\% up to 3.5\% --- and for books without
strong higher-order terms; revalue for large moves, stress, barriers and short-dated options near the strike.
\iqlookfor{Measure the error against the move size.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:real-time-risk:4}
The market time it reflects, the time and market point of the sensitivities used and their age, which positions are pending, and which limits
are breached.
\iqlookfor{Timestamps and staleness.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:real-time-risk:5}
Executions from the \omterm{def:pl:position-and-pnl-services:service}{position service} and market data from the feed into an aggregator over the risk hierarchy; sensitivities from the pricing
library in a cache with invalidation; incremental updates; limits from a \omterm{def:pl:signal-serving-and-parameter-management:store}{parameter store} checked on every update with alerts to people who can
act; \omterm{def:pl:real-time-risk:whatif}{what-if checks} for the pre-trade gate; snapshots with staleness; full revaluation periodically and for stress.
\iqlookfor{Hierarchy, incrementality, cache, limits in the loop.}
\end{solution}
