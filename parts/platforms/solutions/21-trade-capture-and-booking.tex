\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:pl:trade-capture-and-booking} --- Trade Capture and Booking}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:1}
Ten lots, exact shares of $3\tfrac13$ lots each. Largest remainder: 400, 300 and 300 (the tie broken by the funds' names). Odd lots to the
largest funds: the same, since the weights are equal. Each fund rounded: 300 each, 900 shares, leaving 100 unallocated.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:2}
It copies every trade every evening, so it misses only what happened since Thursday at 18:00; the other two copy on some kinds of event only,
and miss every event of the other kinds, whenever it happened in the week.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:3}
The firm's LEI followed by a value unique for the firm: \texttt{5493001KJTIIGC8Y1R12} and \texttt{000\dots0007} padded to 52 characters,
upper-case letters and digits only.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:4}
Because otherwise the allocation can favour a fund after the fact --- the better fills, the odd lot in a rising market --- which is unfair to the
other clients and, for a manager, a conflict that regulators look for. A rule fixed in advance and applied mechanically removes the choice.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:5}
Every novation and every partial termination: it was sent copies on new trades and amendments only. It needs every event that changes what it
confirms --- in practice, the whole stream, filtered by itself rather than by the sender.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:6}
As an event (a book transfer) with its time and reason, not by editing the book field: the risk and P\&L of the previous day were reported in the
old book and must remain reproducible, and the transfer itself is information for product control.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:7}
Two trades: copying at noon as well catches the morning's events, leaving only the events between noon and 15:00. More frequent copies shrink the
window; they never close it.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:8}
Reading on demand from the trading system avoids stale copies only if the trading system holds every event and every system reads the same
version at the same moment; in practice each reads at a different time, keeps what it read, and misses what the trading system does not own
(novations, terminations processed elsewhere). Agreement needs one event stream and versions as of a stated time.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Identifiers, parties, book, dates, status, version, and the instrument, quantity and price; the library's form lets every system price and
risk-manage the trade from the same record.
\item The generating entity's LEI followed by a value unique for it, at most 52 characters, A--Z and 0--9.
\item Identifier formats, distinct parties, settlement not before trade date, a positive quantity, an instrument type.
\item Where a trade is booked, from its instrument type and desk.
\item So that every version stays reproducible and every system can rebuild the same one.
\item 69 amendments, 26 novations and 41 partial terminations among 200 swaps.
\item Amended Monday, novated Wednesday, cut by 40\% Friday; risk held version~3, confirmation version~2, settlement version~4.
\item Risk the events since the last evening; confirmation novations and terminations; settlement amendments and novations.
\item 25, 60 and 71 trades.
\item 109 of 200; none.
\item Ten child orders taking the offer every 20~seconds, 57 executions, average \$100.0783.
\item 4\,950, 2\,970 and 1\,980 shares.
\item Largest remainder 4\,900/3\,000/2\,000, residual 0; odd lots to the largest 5\,000/3\,000/1\,900, residual 0; each rounded
5\,000/3\,000/2\,000, residual $-100$.
\item 50, 80 and 50 shares.
\item The block's average price, \$100.0783.
\item \textbf{Named result.} After a week of lifecycle events, copy-based distribution leaves 25, 60 and 71 wrong trades in risk, confirmation and
settlement and 109 of 200 swaps on which they disagree; event-based distribution, none. The block of 9\,900 shares splits with residuals of 0, 0
and $-100$ shares under largest-remainder, odd-lots-to-the-largest and round-each rules, and deviations from target of at most 50, 80 and 50.
\item Largest remainder: it allocates the whole block and keeps every fund closest to its target.
\item That it consumes the event stream and can say which version it holds as of when.
\item The share of trades that go from execution to settlement without manual intervention.
\item The result of its events.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:1}
Rebuild the trade from its events as of now and compare each system's version with it; then replace copies by subscriptions to the event stream,
with each system reporting the version it holds.
\iqlookfor{Event sourcing and versions.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:2}
A canonical model: a header of identifiers, parties, book, dates, status and version, and the economics as the pricing library's instrument, with
validation; systems keep their own derived views but never their own copy of the terms.
\iqlookfor{One model, validated, shared.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:3}
At the block's average price, by a rule fixed before the trade (pro rata by target, in whole lots, remainders by largest fractional part), the
whole block allocated, with the rule and result recorded.
\iqlookfor{Average price, pre-set rule, no residual.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:4}
The replacement of one party by another, with the other party's consent; the trade continues with a new counterparty, so credit exposure,
confirmations, settlement instructions and reporting change while the economics may not.
\iqlookfor{Party change as an event with downstream effects.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:5}
Adapters from every execution source into a canonical model; UTIs and validation at capture; \omterm{def:pl:trade-capture-and-booking:capture}{booking} by rules; allocations by pre-set rules;
lifecycle events in an event-sourced store; every downstream system on the event stream with versions as of time; straight-through rate and
breaks monitored.
\iqlookfor{Capture once, events everywhere.}
\end{solution}
