\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery} --- Testing and Continuous Delivery}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:1}
Because the same closed form computed on another processor, library version or compiler can differ in the last bits of a floating-point
number; a relative $10^{-10}$ is far below any economic difference and far above rounding.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:2}
4.3\% of Asian calls across forty runs at $k=3$, against 3.4\% in theory: two independent estimates differ by more than $k$ standard errors with
probability $\mathrm{erfc}(k/2)$. The excess comes from the standard error itself being estimated from only eight seeds.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:3}
About $\log_2 10$, rounded up: four steps, each a \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:bluegreen}{rollback} of half the remaining changes and a wait for the symptom. The model year's weekly
train averages 3.93.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:4}
Its tolerance is $k$ times the gap between the 200-point and 400-point grids, and the new build moves each price by exactly that gap: one error
unit. It passes, yet it changes every American price the desk reports, so it should be released as an approved golden update, with the new grid
in the provenance, not slip through.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:5}
Its effect on each Asian call is at most two standard errors, smaller than the noise between two seeds, so no tolerance that tolerates the
noise can see it. With the seed pinned, the old and new builds use the same random numbers, the difference is the effect alone, and a closed
form's tolerance catches it on all twenty.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:6}
The switch would have been one action at one moment, and switching back a second action rather than a reinstallation; had the eighth green
host been left on the old build, the switch would still have sent it traffic. It changes how fast a bad release is undone, not whether a
partial installation is noticed: that is the consistency check's job.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:7}
The standard error falls by $\sqrt2$ while the bug's effect does not, so the bug is caught on 14 of 20 Asian calls at $k=5$ instead of 9.
Noise, however, failed 40\% of the runs: with the standard error estimated from eight seeds, some instruments' tolerances are too tight by
chance. More paths help detection; only a pinned seed removes the noise.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:8}
At ten standard errors the Monte Carlo instruments no longer see any regression smaller than ten standard errors --- the chapter's Asian-fixing
bug is two to ten --- and the closed forms gain nothing. Noise was the symptom of an unpinned seed; the fix is to pin it and release seed
changes as approved golden updates.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Units test functions in isolation, \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:tests}{integration tests} components together, \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:tests}{end-to-end tests} whole flows; each level up is slower, more
fragile and less precise, so there are fewer of them.
\item The value, its tolerance and its provenance (engine, parameters, library version, who approved it).
\item Closed forms: a relative $10^{-10}$; PDE: $k$ times the 200 against 400-point grid gap; Monte Carlo: $k$ times the standard error from
eight seeds.
\item Spreadsheets filled by copying and pasting, and a division by the sum of two rates instead of their average that likely muted
volatility by a factor of two.
\item The seed: the twenty Asian calls; the day count: all sixty; the put sign: the twenty puts; the Asian fixing: the twenty Asian calls;
the finer grid: the ten American puts.
\item The put sign error and the day count on every non-Monte-Carlo instrument: hundreds of error units or more.
\item At $k=2$ it flags 18\% of Asian calls and fails every run; at $k=5$, 0.1\% of calls and 2.5\% of runs.
\item All 20 at $k\le2$, 18 at 2.5 and 3, 15 at 3.5, 11 at 4, 9 at 5, 7 at 6.
\item $k\le2$, at \$980 per run: a test that fails on every run, which nobody will keep looking at.
\item Monte Carlo values become deterministic: compared at a closed form's tolerance, every planted regression is caught (20 of 20 Asian
fixings, 60 of 60 day counts) and noise cannot occur; a new seed is a change to approve.
\item Unit 0.02~s, golden 0.33~s, end to end 1.62~s (measured, otherwise idle machine).
\item Mean waits of 0, 0.51, 3.46 and 6.99 days; 1, 1.83, 10.25 and 20.5 changes per deployment; 0, 1.16, 3.93 and 4.86 bisection steps.
\item Who made each change, who approved it, when, how it was deployed and verified.
\item Clearly delineated methodologies to develop and test it, ensuring among other things that it does not behave in an unintended
manner, with testing in an environment separated from production.
\item Switching traffic back to the previous, still-installed set of hosts.
\item \textbf{Named result.} The sign error (20 of 20 puts) and the day count (every non-Monte-Carlo instrument) are caught at every
tolerance; the Asian-fixing bug is caught on 20, 18, 11, 9 and 7 of 20 Asian calls at $k$ = 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 while noise fails 100\%, 60\%, 20\%,
2.5\% and 0\% of runs; the model's expected cost is lowest at $k\le2$ (\$980 a run) only by failing every run --- pinned seeds catch everything
with no noise. The consistency check refuses the release because router8 reports another build's hash.
\item Dead code left callable, a flag reused, an incomplete deployment, and no review or check of the deployment.
\item The check catches the incomplete deployment; tests of the old code path with the reused flag would have shown what the flag did on an
old host.
\item Pinned golden and \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:tests}{integration tests}, a staged rollout with a consistency check before each switch, a tested \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:bluegreen}{rollback}, and \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:change}{change
control} with a second person.
\item The moment every host starts running the same, tested release --- verified, not assumed.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:1}
Many fast \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:tests}{unit tests}, fewer \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:tests}{integration tests}, few \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:tests}{end-to-end tests}: each level up is slower, more fragile and says less precisely what broke,
so the bulk of the checking belongs at the bottom.
\iqlookfor{Cost and precision per level.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:2}
Pin the seed so that the same code gives the same numbers, keep golden values with tight tolerances, release seed or path-count changes as
approved golden updates, and separately test convergence and unbiasedness against closed forms with statistical tolerances.
\iqlookfor{Pinned seeds; statistics only where they belong.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:3}
Every host reports its build and configuration hashes; the release is switched on only if all match; deployment is automated, reviewed
by a second person, staged, and reversible.
\iqlookfor{Verify every host before switching.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:4}
Small, frequent, automated deployments for research and analytics; trains with a change window, staged rollout and consistency checks for the
order path, where a bad release costs money in minutes and regulation requires documented testing.
\iqlookfor{Batch size against \omterm{def:pl:the-platform-map:blast}{blast radius}.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:5}
Unit and property tests on every merge; pinned golden values of prices and risk with engine-derived tolerances and approved updates;
simulator parity and \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:tests}{end-to-end tests} nightly; a pipeline that stops at the first failure; trains or \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:cd}{continuous delivery} by component;
\omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:bluegreen}{blue--green deployment} with a consistency check and a tested \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:bluegreen}{rollback}; \omterm{def:pl:testing-and-continuous-delivery:change}{change control} recording author, approver and time.
\iqlookfor{Tests, pinning, controlled deployment.}
\end{solution}
