\begin{solution}{exo:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:4}
Everything else that changed after the twelfth (spreads, volumes, a competitor) changes the P\&L at the same time; before and after cannot separate
them. Randomising the days (or instruments) that get the change separates it from everything else.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:5}
The competitor cuts a share of a margin; the margin is smaller once the spread has narrowed. Evaluated after the spread's change, the competitor's
effect is smaller than if it were evaluated first.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:6}
They are measured on every fill, with far less noise than a day's P\&L, which includes inventory and market moves; their effect on P\&L then follows
from the product.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:8}

after cannot say which did it; the experiment and the factor measurements can.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V\,s\,(\kappa h-a)$: market volume and half-spread from the market, share from fills, capture ratio from fill prices, adverse selection from
mark-outs.
\item Two venues; month 9's narrower spread and higher volume; a competitor on venue B on day 175; a parameter change on randomised days from day 182.
\item Change one factor at a time from month 8 to month 9 in a fixed order; the residual is what is left.
\item One market maker earned \euro1.55 a trade on the spread and lost \euro0.68 on positions.
\item See \cref{def:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:control}.
\item $-\$2\,670$ a day (s.e.\ \$1\,070); with CUPED $-\$2\,550$ (\$1\,070); share $+10\%$ and adverse selection $+0.10$ cent exactly.
\item See \cref{def:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:decay}; a one-sided CUSUM on venue B's share crosses on day 176.
\item 29.9\% of share and 14.9\% of capture ratio, against 30\% and 15\%.
\item Planted: spread $-\$51\,500$, volume $+\$48\,000$, competitor $-\$57\,400$, parameter $-\$8\,100$, residual $+\$23\,400$. Estimated: the same
except competitor $-\$53\,500$ and residual $+\$19\,600$.
\item The change point is a day late, so the competitor's effect applies to one day fewer.
\item Noise in inventory, volume and spreads, and the approximation of monthly averages.
\item The attribution depends on it; a report must be comparable month to month.
\item Of month 9's \$45\,600 fall: spread $-\$51\,500$, volume $+\$48\,000$, competition $-\$53\,500$ estimated ($-\$57\,400$ planted), parameter
$-\$8\,100$ (both), residual about \$20\,000.
\item No: it costs \$2\,670 a day.
\item See \cref{def:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:lifecycle}.
\item Day 239 at a cost of \$12\,000 a day with a 60-day window; it depends on the cost and the window.
\item The day's P\&L by factor, venue and instrument; change points; experiments running; the residual.
\item It averages each factor's contribution over all orders, removing the order's arbitrariness.
\item Its changes would be confounded with the competitor's arrival.
\item Knowing which cause to act on.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:1}
The factors: market volume and spreads, the share and capture ratio by venue, adverse selection by mark-out, inventory P\&L, fees; then any change
made to the strategy.
\iqlookfor{decompose before explaining.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:2}
A change point in the share and capture ratio on that venue alone, with market volume unchanged, and queue positions that start later.
\iqlookfor{venue-specific change points.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:3}
A change log with who, what, when, why and the experiment's assignment; overnight jobs joining fills, mark-outs, market data and assignments; the
report built from them, with the residual.
\iqlookfor{audit trail and reproducible reports.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:hf:pnl-analytics-and-the-strategy-lifecycle:4}
A written reason, review, a way to revert, and randomisation or a limited scope so its effect can be measured; exceptions only for risk reduction.
\iqlookfor{change control.}
\end{solution}
