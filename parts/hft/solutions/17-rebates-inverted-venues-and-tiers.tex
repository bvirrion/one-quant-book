\begin{solution}{exo:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:4}
Ordinary flow reaches the back of a long queue only after a long time; the event most likely to reach it first is a sweep that takes the whole level
and moves the price through it.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:5}
Padding pays when $T\cdot0.29-(T-v)\cdot0.6-v\cdot0.20>0$ with $T=22$ million: $v>22\times(0.6-0.29)/(0.6-0.20)=17.05$ million a day.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:6}
Rebates must fall with the taker fee they are paid from: the reason to queue there weakens, the queue shortens, and the fee-adjusted prices of the
venues converge.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:7}

\begin{solution}{exo:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:8}
Only fills from ordinary flow earn the half-spread; at the back of a long queue 91\% of fills are sweeps that lose half a cent before the rebate. The
model's fill earns $-0.13$ cent on average, rebate included.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item See \cref{def:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:capture}.
\item Access fee cap to \$0.001 a share for quotes of \$1 or more, compliance by November 2026; the fee pilot vacated in June 2020.
\item Execution quality falls as the venue's rebate or fee rises.
\item The fee changes the price actually received for a fill.
\item Filled by flow (half-spread less mark-out), filled by a sweep (half-spread less a tick), abandoned (nothing).
\item Rebate venue: 42\%, 91\%, $-0.05$ cent an order; inverted: 74\%, 24\%, $+0.005$.
\item Makers join the venue with the better edge until its queue removes the advantage.
\item In short queues on the rebate venue (0.40 cent at 2,000 shares); for the first to join.
\item See \cref{def:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:cliff} and \cref{def:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:marginal}.
\item $-0.20$, $-20$ and $-0.29$ cent.
\item At 18 million: gain \$584,000, cost \$504,000, net \$80,000; at 14 million: net $-\$256,000$.
\item By day 14 of 21, if the market absorbs at most 0.1\% of consolidated volume a day.
\item Rebate venue $-0.05$ and inverted $+0.005$ cent a share an order at typical queues; chasing the tier stops paying below 17.05 million shares a
day of natural volume.
\item The inverted venue at typical queues; the rebate venue only when its queue is short.
\item Smaller rebates, shorter queues, venues closer in fee-adjusted terms.
\item Trades with a genuine counterparty and risk versus trades with oneself or a coordinated party.
\item With the \omterm{def:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:marginal}{marginal fee} per venue, updated with the month's volume.
\item \omterm{def:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:capture}{Rebate capture}.
\item Tag each fill by whether the level was fully consumed within a short window after it.
\item A place at the back of a queue whose value the market has already priced.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:1}
Takers route there first, so its short queue fills sooner and with fewer sweeps; being first at a price is worth more than the fee.
\iqlookfor{queue length and routing order.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:2}
For each order, the queue ahead at entry, whether and how it filled, and the mid after; fit fill probability and mark-out by queue bucket,
separating fills from sweeps.
\iqlookfor{conditioning on queue ahead.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:3}
Compute the tier's value on the month's volume, the extra needed per remaining day and its cost; route natural flow to that venue first; pad only if
it pays and the market can absorb it.
\iqlookfor{value versus cost, with capacity.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:4}
Month-to-date volumes per venue, the schedules as data, the tier each would reach, and the \omterm{def:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:marginal}{marginal fee} of the next share including cliffs,
recomputed on each fill and published with the book data.
\iqlookfor{schedules as data, incremental updates.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:5}
Dependence on the fee schedules (which change monthly and are capped by regulation), on one venue's tier, and on fills that lose before the rebate.
\iqlookfor{regulatory and schedule risk.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:hf:rebates-inverted-venues-and-tiers:6}
If the queue is consumed in time $\tau=(Q+\ell)/\lambda$ and the other clocks are exponential with total rate $\mu=\mu_{\mathrm{t}}+\mu_{\mathrm{a}}$,
the probability is $e^{-\mu\tau}$.
\iqlookfor{competing clocks.}
\end{solution}
