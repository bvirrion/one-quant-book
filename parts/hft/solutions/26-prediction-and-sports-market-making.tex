\begin{solution}{exo:hf:prediction-and-sports-market-making:4}

\begin{solution}{exo:hf:prediction-and-sports-market-making:5}
It delays the placing of orders but not their cancellation, so makers can withdraw stale quotes before new orders reach them; delaying cancellations

\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:hf:prediction-and-sports-market-making:6}
Whether betting prices incorporate news swiftly and fully; a goal is news that breaks cleanly, at a known moment, with no leakage, and the
contracts have terminal values. They found prices update swiftly and fully.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:hf:prediction-and-sports-market-making:7}
With a 1.5-second feed the loss per goal is \$112 at one second, \$10 at two and \$0.6 at three: three seconds make it negligible instead of six.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:hf:prediction-and-sports-market-making:8}
A better model does not protect against being slower than the event: after a goal, the courtsider knows more than any model. The quotes must be
cancelled before orders based on the goal can match, whatever the model's quality.
\end{solution}

feed's latency.
\iqlookfor{mass cancel on events.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:hf:prediction-and-sports-market-making:6}
With $\ln A_f\sim N(\ln a_f,s^2)$ and $\ln A_m\sim N(\ln a_m,s^2)$, $P(A_f+D<A_m)$ has no closed form; condition on $A_f$ and integrate
$P(A_m>A_f+D)$ over its density numerically.
\iqlookfor{convolution of the two latencies.}
\end{solution}
