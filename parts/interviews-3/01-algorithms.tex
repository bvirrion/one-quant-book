\begin{ivtrains}
Choosing the data structure before writing the loop, stating its cost, and getting the edges right: empty input, ties, duplicates and values that can be negative. Prices, order books and streams appear where they change the problem. Write each solution as you would on a whiteboard, in Python or C++, then say what it costs.
\end{ivtrains}
