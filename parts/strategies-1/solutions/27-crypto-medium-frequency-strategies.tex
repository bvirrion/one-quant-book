\section*{Chapter \ref{ch:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies} --- Crypto Medium-Frequency Strategies}

\begin{solution}{exo:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:1}
$0.0001 \times 3 \times 365 = 10.95\%$ a year; at 0.1\% per interval, 109.5\%.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:2}
$(30.61\% - 0.2\%)/1.2 = 25.3\%$ on capital.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:3}
A price move changes the spot position's value and the short perpetual's by opposite amounts. The book is exposed to funding (which can turn negative),
to the basis between perpetual and spot, to margin calls on the short when the price rises (the gain sits in the spot, possibly on another venue), and
to the venues and custodians holding both legs.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:4}
$0.05 \times 0.808 = 4.0\%$ a year. At least one failure: $1 - 0.95^4 = 18.5\%$ among four venues, $1 - 0.95^8 = 33.7\%$ among eight (the simulation's
18.7\% and 34.0\% are sampling noise around these).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:5}
$25.3\% - 4.0\% = 21.3\%$ in 2021; $3.3\% - 4.0\% = -0.7\%$ in 2022: a loss once the \omterm{def:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:venue}{venue risk} is charged.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:6}
One week of returns at 80\% specific volatility measures the planted drift with far more noise than four weeks, so its ranking changes more each week:
the book trades more for a weaker signal, and at 20 basis points per unit traded the costs take everything.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:7}
With contagion the expected loss rises from 4.1\% to 7.4\% a year and the 99th percentile from 40.4\% to 60.6\% of capital; the chance of a loss in a
year stays about 18.7\%, because contagion adds failures only in years that already have one. Spreading capital over venues helps least when failures
travel together.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:8}
Five years without a venue failure measure nothing about it: the book's main risk is a loss of most of the assets on a venue, which a daily P\&L history
does not show until it happens. Charge the expected venue loss, stress the book with a failure, and count the funding years that were low or negative.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:1}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Long spot and short a perpetual, collecting funding; long spot and short a dated future, collecting the basis.
\item The average premium of the perpetual over its index plus a clamped interest term, paid every eight hours at 00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC.
\item 14.2\%, 25.3\%, 3.3\%, 6.4\%, 9.8\%, 4.1\% and 2.3\% for 2020 to 2026.
\item It can exceed 40\% a year, varies strongly, and reflects leverage demand from trend-chasing investors against limited arbitrage capital.
\item Crypto returns load on crypto-specific factors, with strong \omterm{def:s1:trend-following:trend}{time-series momentum}; market, size and momentum capture the cross-section.
\item 50 coins, a 60\% market factor, 80\% fat-tailed specific volatility, a planted drift with a 60-day half-life.
\item 0.85, 1.56 and 1.29 before costs at 7, 28 and 56 days; 0.42, 1.35 and 1.13 after 10 basis points; $-0.01$, 1.13 and 0.97 after 20.
\item A signal from public blockchain records; a planted $-0.03$ correlation recovered as an IC of $-0.031$; not that real flows predict returns.
\item The risk of losing assets held at a venue or custodian.
\item Located assets of about a fifth of customer payables (a shortfall of 80.8\%), and \$7.0 billion of net customer withdrawals in eleven days.
\item $80.8\%/k$ of capital; 5.0\%, 9.9\%, 18.7\% and 34.0\% for one to eight venues.
\item The expected loss is $pL$ on each unit of capital wherever it sits.
\item \textbf{Named result.} The \omterm{def:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:carry}{funding carry} book earned 25.3\% on capital in 2021 and 3.3\% in 2022 (14.2\% in 2020, 2.3\% in 2026 to September); spread
over four venues, it loses 20.2\% of capital when one fails, with an expected venue loss of 4.1\% a year.
\item Choosing venues and custody, keeping assets off exchanges, and withdrawing early.
\item Funding interval by interval, fees, the capital actually tied up, margin calls, and a venue-loss charge.
\item Perpetual \omterm{def:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:carry}{funding carry} held on one venue.
\item Both earn the price of holding an asset others pay to shed or to lever; here the payer is leveraged demand.
\item Leveraged long demand in a rising market.
\item Leverage demand fell after 2022's losses; the chapter does not claim the cause.
\item For supplying leverage and bearing \omterm{def:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:venue}{venue risk}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:1}
A periodic payment between longs and shorts of a perpetual future that has no expiry, set from the perpetual's premium over its index; it pulls the
perpetual's price toward the spot index by making the crowded side pay.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:2}
Buy spot and short the perpetual in equal notional; collect 60\% a year while it lasts; risks are funding falling or turning negative, margin calls on the
short if the price rises, liquidation if margin runs out, the basis moving, and the venue failing; size for the \omterm{def:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:venue}{venue risk}, not the funding.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:3}
From the loss the firm can bear if the venue fails outright: a cap per venue scaled by its assessed quality (custody, audits, jurisdiction, history), a
cap on total exposure to venues without segregated custody, and triggers to withdraw (withdrawal delays, spreads, rumours).
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:4}
Positions and margin on each venue, funding received and paid, balances and withdrawal status, transfer times between venues, price and basis
differences, and alerts when margin, funding or a venue's behaviour moves outside limits.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:5}
Build the universe as it was each week, including coins later delisted or dead, with their last tradeable prices; rank only coins tradeable at the time;
charge costs by coin.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:s1:crypto-medium-frequency-strategies:6}
The number of failures $N$ is binomial$(k, p)$ and the loss is $NL/k$: mean $pL$, variance $L^2p(1 - p)/k$. The probability that the loss is at least
$mL/k$ is $P(N \ge m) = \sum_{j \ge m}\binom{k}{j}p^j(1 - p)^{k - j}$.
\end{solution}
