\chapter{Machine Learning}\label{ch:iv1:machine-learning}

\begin{ivtrains}
Machine learning as interviews test it: bias and variance, trees and boosting, leakage and validation on time series, regularisation, neural networks by hand, metrics, and what low signal does to all of them. Almost every answer here is a number you can derive.
\end{ivtrains}

\begin{question}[$\star$ \iqroles{QR, DS, Analyst} \iqfirm{any} \iqlevel{junior}]\label{iq:iv1:machine-learning:1}
A model flags 100 days out of 1000 as days of a large upward move. Of the flagged days, 40 really were such days, and a further 10 such days went unflagged. Give accuracy, precision, recall and F1. What accuracy does a model reach that never calls anything?
\end{question}
