\chapter{Sequences, Patterns and Aptitude}\label{ch:iv1:sequences-patterns-and-aptitude}

\begin{ivtrains}
The items of a first-round online test: the next term of a sequence, a pattern stated in words, a short piece of logic and a quick calculation from a small table. Each item has one rule that fits with less machinery than any rival, and part of the skill is knowing when you have found it. Time yourself: a real test allows well under the star time.
\end{ivtrains}
