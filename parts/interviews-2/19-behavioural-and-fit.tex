\chapter{Behavioural and Fit}\label{ch:iv2:behavioural-and-fit}

\begin{ivtrains}
The questions with no number to check: why this work, what went wrong, how you handle conflict, pressure and risk, and what you ask at the end. Answer out loud, with a clock. The answers give a plan and an illustration with blanks in square brackets, and only your own true facts should go in them. An interviewer will ask you about the details, so never use a story you did not live.
\end{ivtrains}

\end{question}

Give the average, the standard deviation and the longest typical losing run for each over 250 trading days, and say which is harder to live with.
\end{question}
