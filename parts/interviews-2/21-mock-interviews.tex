\chapter{Mock Interviews}\label{ch:iv2:mock-interviews}

\begin{ivtrains}
Whole interviews, written out in order, each for one role. Each question gives the setting, the rules and a score sheet, and its answer holds the script. Do it in one sitting with a clock, on paper and out loud, or ask a friend to read the script as the interviewer. Cover the script with a sheet of paper, and slide it down one stop line at a time.
\end{ivtrains}
