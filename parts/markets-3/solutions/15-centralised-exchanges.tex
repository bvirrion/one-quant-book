\begin{solution}{exo:m3:centralised-exchanges:8}

\begin{solution}{iq:m3:centralised-exchanges:1}
A limit order that is rejected (or repriced) if it would execute at once, so that it only rests: it
guarantees the maker fee and that a quote computed from a stale price does not cross the spread. Market
makers and passive execution use it.
\iqlookfor{maker fee, no accidental take, and the rejection as the right outcome.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:m3:centralised-exchanges:2}
Treat balances as unsecured credit: per-venue limits sized to capital and venue quality, balances kept to
what trading needs and swept to own or third-party custody, \omterm{def:m3:centralised-exchanges:oes}{off-exchange settlement} where offered, \omterm{def:m3:centralised-exchanges:subaccount}{sub-accounts}
to separate strategies, monitoring of signs of stress (withdrawal delays, the venue's \omterm{def:m3:blockchains-for-traders:key}{token}, news) with a plan
to withdraw quickly.
\iqlookfor{credit limits, sweeping, and custody outside the venue.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:m3:centralised-exchanges:4}
She receives her leaf's nonce and the sibling hashes and sums on her path, recomputes the root and compares it

\begin{solution}{iq:m3:centralised-exchanges:5}

\iqlookfor{a snapshot of one side of the balance sheet.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:m3:centralised-exchanges:6}
400 quotes (two sides) cannot all be updated at 10 orders a second; hold for each instrument and side the
desired quote and the resting one, rank pending changes by value (distance from the fair price, instruments in
motion), and let a governor release the most valuable changes as the limit allows, coalescing so that only the
latest desired quote is sent; widen or pull quotes that cannot be kept current, and use amend or cancel-replace
as the venue counts them.
\iqlookfor{coalescing, prioritisation and the governor.}
\end{solution}
