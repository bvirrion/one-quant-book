\begin{solution}{exo:m3:dated-futures-options-and-etfs:6}
In kind, the participant delivers bitcoin it has bought where it chooses and at a known price, hedging the share position exactly; in
cash, the trust buys later at a price the participant does not control, so the arbitrage carries execution risk and costs more, which
widens the band around net asset value.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:m3:dated-futures-options-and-etfs:7}

faster, approaching $1 - K/F$, so their difference falls again: the premium paid in bitcoin is itself a large short exposure to
bitcoin's dollar value.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{exo:m3:dated-futures-options-and-etfs:8}
Black's delta is the dollar delta. A book whose premiums and P\&L are in coin must use the premium-adjusted delta, and deep in-the-money
calls then need markedly smaller hedges (0.645 instead of 0.942 at 60{,}000 in the snapshot); the hedge described overhedges them.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{pb:m3:dated-futures-options-and-etfs:1}
\textbf{1.} $4.95\% \times 200$ million: USD~9.9 million. \textbf{2.} $25\% + 10\%$ of notional: USD~70 million; 14.14\%. \textbf{3.}
About USD~0.26 million lost ($-0.13\%$). \textbf{4.} $4.5\% + 0.25\% + 0.30\% = 5.05\%$. \textbf{5.} Buying spot and selling the future
lends cash against bitcoin until expiry; the basis is the rate earned on that loan, which the trade compares with its own financing
rate. \textbf{6.} Authorised participants create baskets when shares trade above net asset value and redeem when below. \textbf{7.}
At the New York variant of the CME CF Bitcoin Reference Rate, computed from 3 to 4 p.m. New York time: twelve five-minute partitions,
volume-weighted medians, averaged. \textbf{8.} At the CME CF Bitcoin Reference Rate on the expiry day. \textbf{9.} If the two
benchmarks are computed at different times or from different data, their difference at expiry is left in the trade as unhedged basis.
\textbf{10.} The SEC permitted in-kind creations and redemptions for the trust: authorised participants can deliver bitcoin, which
tightens the link between the share price and bitcoin and makes the trade's unwind cheaper. \textbf{11.} The short future loses
about $10\% \times 11/12$ of notional, USD~18.3 million of variation margin to be paid in cash, although the loss reverses if the
position is held to expiry: a liquidity risk, not a loss at maturity. \textbf{12.} Capital rises to 50\% of notional: the return
falls to 9.9\%. \textbf{13.} USD~2 million, 40\% of a year's carry at a 10\% basis. \textbf{14.} The fund loses USD~60 million and the
short future gains about as much, paid in variation margin; but the financier, lending USD~180 million against USD~140 million of
collateral at a 10\% haircut, asks for USD~54 million back at once: the gains and the calls arrive in different accounts. \textbf{15.}
Margin and P\&L would be in coin: the fund must buy bitcoin to post as collateral, whose dollar value moves with the price, and the trade
carries the venue's credit, liquidation and \omterm{def:m3:margin-liquidation-and-loss-allocation:adl}{auto-deleveraging} rules instead of a clearing house's. \textbf{16.} Leveraged longs who prefer futures to spot, and are willing to
pay the basis for leverage without financing. \textbf{17.} Their exits coincide: when the basis collapses or financing tightens, they
all sell the fund and buy back futures together. \textbf{18.} Not strictly: it is a funded carry trade with liquidity, financing and
\omterm{def:m3:physical-commodity-markets:basisrisk}{basis risk} before expiry. \textbf{19.} \emph{Named result:} 4.95\% a year on notional and 14.14\% on capital at a 10\% basis; $-0.13\%$
at the snapshot basis of 4.92\%. \textbf{20.} A regulated, financeable, dollar form of bitcoin, and with it a bridge between crypto
carry and the ordinary money markets.
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:m3:dated-futures-options-and-etfs:1}
A European option on the dollar price whose premium and settlement are in the coin: premium in coin $= V/F$ with $V$ the Black dollar
price on the future $F$; multiply by $F$ to convert back.
\iqlookfor{divide by the future, not the index.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:m3:dated-futures-options-and-etfs:2}
From spot trades on a set of vetted venues over a window (an hour), split into partitions (twelve of five minutes), with a
volume-weighted median per partition and an equal-weighted average of the medians: robust to single prints, representative of
volume, and hard to move without trading most of it.
\iqlookfor{manipulation resistance by construction.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:m3:dated-futures-options-and-etfs:3}
Its delta in coin is the dollar delta less the coin premium. Deep in the money the dollar delta saturates at one while the coin premium
keeps growing towards $1 - K/F$, so the difference falls.
\iqlookfor{premium currency, and the saturation of the dollar delta.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:m3:dated-futures-options-and-etfs:4}
Long the fund (financed), short the regulated future: earns the basis less financing, fee and costs to expiry. Risks: variation margin if
the basis widens, financing withdrawal or higher haircuts, fund discounts at unwind, benchmark mismatch at expiry, and crowded exits.
\iqlookfor{carry net of financing, and liquidity before expiry.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:m3:dated-futures-options-and-etfs:5}
Higher levels; both wings bid; a skew that can flip to calls in rallies; continuous trading with no overnight or weekend close; and
premiums quoted in coin on the main crypto venue, which must be converted before comparing surfaces.
\iqlookfor{level, shape, time measure and conventions.}
\end{solution}

\begin{solution}{iq:m3:dated-futures-options-and-etfs:6}
One internal convention (dollar prices, dollar Greeks) with adapters that convert each venue's quotes, premiums, P\&L and margin; Greeks
reported both in dollars and in the currency of each account (premium-adjusted for coin accounts); coin balances marked at market;
scenarios that move the coin price and the volatility surface jointly; and margin projections per venue under those scenarios.
\iqlookfor{normalise conventions, keep currency explicit, and stress collateral with the book.}
\end{solution}
