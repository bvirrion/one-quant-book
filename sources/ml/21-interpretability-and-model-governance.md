# 21. Interpretability and Model Governance — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A risk committee asks why the model cut its position in a stock by half on Tuesday; the researcher's answer, that the gradient-boosted model's output fell, is true and useless.
- **Sections.** What an explanation is for; Global explanations: partial dependence and accumulated local effects; Local explanations and their stability; Documentation: the model card; Validating a learned model.
- **Defines.** interpretability, partial dependence, individual conditional expectation, accumulated local effects, global surrogate, counterfactual explanation, model card.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** model risk (B6.26), model validation (B6.26), model risk management (B6.26), model inventory (B6.26), model tiering (B6.26), effective challenge (B6.26), outcomes analysis (B6.26), benchmark model (B6.26), three lines of defence (B6.26), SHAP value (ch6), permutation importance (ch6), decision tree (ch5), gradient boosting (ch5), prediction target (B7.6), forecast horizon (B7.6), information coefficient (B7.6), in-sample (B7.20), out-of-sample (B7.20), cross-validation (B4.16).
- **Tutorial.** Explain the Chapter 5 LightGBM model with partial dependence, ICE, ALE, SHAP and a depth-three global surrogate; show partial dependence misleading when two features are correlated and ALE correct; measure how stable SHAP rankings are across retrains; generate a model card and a validation report with a challenger model through Book 6's firm.modelval. Data: synthetic.
- **Build.** `firm.modelcard`: partial dependence, ICE and ALE, global surrogates with fidelity, explanation stability across retrains, a model-card generator, and a learned-model validation checklist wired to firm.modelval; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Explain it to the risk committee -- named result: partial dependence's error against the true effect under correlated features compared with ALE's, and the rank stability of the top five SHAP features across ten retrains.
- **Facts to verify.** Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System SR 11-7 guidance on model risk management (2011) and its current status (dated); Prudential Regulation Authority SS1/23 model risk management principles for banks (dated); Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act) scope and timeline as it touches trading firms (dated); Friedman 2001 partial dependence; Goldstein, Kapelner, Bleich and Pitkin 2015 ICE (JCGS); Apley and Zhu 2020 accumulated local effects (JRSS B); Mitchell et al. 2019 model cards for model reporting (FAT*); Wachter, Mittelstadt and Russell 2018 counterfactual explanations (Harvard JOLT); Rudin 2019 stop explaining black box models (Nature Machine Intelligence).
