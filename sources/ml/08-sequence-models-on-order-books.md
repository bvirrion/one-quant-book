# 8. Sequence Models on Order Books — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A paper reports that a convolutional-recurrent network predicts the direction of the next mid-price move from the last hundred order-book states far better than any hand-built feature; on a laptop, on a simulated book, with the labels built honestly, the gap is smaller, and it is worth knowing exactly how much smaller.
- **Sections.** Order-book streams as inputs; Convolutional and recurrent models; Temporal convolutions and attention; What the literature replicates.
- **Defines.** convolutional neural network, recurrent neural network, long short-term memory, causal convolution, temporal convolutional network, attention mechanism, transformer.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** market-by-order (B1.19), depth imbalance (B7.8), order-book replay (B7.18), intraday alpha (B8.14), prediction horizon (B8.14), exchange simulator (B10.26), logistic regression (ch4), triple-barrier label (ch2), limit order book (B10.1), order-flow imbalance (B7.8), queue imbalance (B7.8), microprice (B7.8), mid price (B1.1), bid--ask spread (B1.1), trade sign (B7.9), multilayer perceptron (ch7), Adam (ch7), early stopping (ch5), stochastic gradient descent (B4.24), reverse mode (B4.28), prediction target (B7.6), forecast horizon (B7.6), information coefficient (B7.6), in-sample (B7.20), out-of-sample (B7.20), cross-validation (B4.16).
- **Tutorial.** Turn firm.tape sessions (training, validation and test sessions on disjoint seeds) into windows of ten-level snapshots with event-time mid-price labels; train small CNN, LSTM, TCN and attention models on the CPU and a logistic regression on order-flow imbalance and queue imbalance; report accuracy and IC per session with their spread across seeds and sessions. Data: synthetic (firm.tape; Book 10's agent-based market once frozen).
- **Build.** `firm.lobseq`: windowed order-book datasets from firm.tape (and Book 10's simulator), per-session normalisation fitted on training sessions only, the four small architectures, training and per-session evaluation; Python on PyTorch.
- **Weekend problem.** DeepLOB on a laptop -- named result: the accuracy gain of the best sequence model over logistic regression on order-flow imbalance, with its spread across seeds and sessions.
- **Facts to verify.** Zhang, Zohren and Roberts 2019 DeepLOB (IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing); Sirignano and Cont 2019 universal features of price formation (Quantitative Finance); Ntakaris et al. 2018 benchmark dataset for mid-price forecasting (J. Forecasting) and its licence; Briola et al. 2024/2025 LOBCAST benchmark of deep models on limit order books; Kolm, Turiel and Westray 2023 deep order flow imbalance (Mathematical Finance); Hochreiter and Schmidhuber 1997 LSTM; Bai, Kolter and Koltun 2018 temporal convolutional networks (arXiv); Vaswani et al. 2017 attention is all you need (NeurIPS).
