## Decisions taken in Phase A

- **No strategy files** (the outline gives none). Predictor cards only if a chapter introduces a named predictor
  (none planned).
- **Autoencoder** is defined in ch. 9 (first use, the conditional autoencoder), not ch. 10; ch. 10 defines the
  denoising variant and the self-supervised vocabulary.
- **Cumulative reward**, not "return", for the RL sum of rewards (return is the finance word everywhere else).
- **Training checkpoint**, not "checkpointing" (Book 4 ch. 28 owns checkpointing in the AD sense).
- **Shadow deployment** (a model), not "shadow trading" (Book 7 ch. 19, a strategy).
- **Backpropagation** is defined as Book 4's reverse mode applied to a network (pointer in the definition).
- Data is synthetic almost everywhere (firm.synthmkt, firm.tape, Book 10's simulator once frozen, firm.mlsynth).
  Real data only if openly licensed (EDGAR headings for tokenisation examples in ch. 13). Loughran–McDonald word
  lists: check the licence before using them; otherwise a hand-written list.
- **Hook numbers are placeholders.** Every number in a brief's hook (ch. 1's 0.4 %/0.6 %/95 %, ch. 7's seeds, ch.
  14's nine-in-ten, ch. 15's 2 %/9 %, ch. 19's 5 bp, ch. 26's 40 µs/2 µs) must be replaced by the chapter's own
  simulation output or a ledger row; if the simulation does not reproduce the pathology, rewrite the hook around
  the one it does produce (Book 7 trap).
- **Ch. 14 (LLMs)**: no network API, no downloaded weights. Tiny word-level transformer trained locally in
  seconds; BM25 and SVD/dense retrieval; the leakage demonstration is two tiny models (cut-off vs contaminated);
  the agent loop is deterministic with a stub policy and a firm.pit guard. Literature numbers (Lopez-Lira and Tang,
  Glasserman and Lin, Sarkar and Vafa) only through the ledger.
- **Ch. 26 timings**: measured on this laptop (Intel Core Ultra 7 155H, WSL2, no isolated cores), said so in the
  caption; tests assert orderings, parity and zero hot-path allocations only.

## Traps to watch in this book (from WRITING_A_QUANT_BOOK.md §9 and the batch file)

- One simulated market is one draw: report means and spreads over seeds; vary seeds, not lengths.
- Planted effects too strong at first: tune to realistic ICs (0.01–0.05) and Sharpe ratios; say when a synthetic
  number is an upper bound.
- Seeds are a source of variance for networks: every network result over several seeds; torch deterministic,
  one thread; `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1` already exported by the test and figdata scripts.
- Execution at the signal is a free lunch: every trading evaluation re-run a tick later (ch. 18, 22, 29).
- Session-disjoint splits for order-book data (windows never cross sessions; normalisation fitted on training
  sessions only).
- Listing ranges drift after `ruff --fix`; ASCII only in listed files; ≤ 40 lines per listing.
- Machine shared by four agents: never more than ~2 GB or several minutes of training; cache only small,
  script-regenerated intermediates under `figdata/ml/` or `data/ml/`.
