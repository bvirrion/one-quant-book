# 27. Monitoring and Retraining — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On a Tuesday the model stopped trading: every prediction was inside the threshold. Nothing had failed; an upstream vendor had begun sending volumes in lots instead of shares, and the model was obediently scaling them.
- **Sections.** What to monitor: inputs, outputs, outcomes; Drift statistics and alarm design; Performance alarms on noisy outcomes; Shadow, canary and champion--challenger rollouts; Retrain, roll back, or retire.
- **Defines.** model monitoring, population stability index, prediction drift, performance alarm, shadow deployment, champion--challenger.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** canary deployment (B7.21), staged rollout (B7.21), A/B test (B7.21), sequential test (B7.21), CUSUM test (B7.13), outcomes analysis (B6.26), Kolmogorov--Smirnov test (B4.12), false discovery rate (B4.12), covariate shift (ch12), concept drift (ch12), drift detector (ch12), retraining schedule (ch12), kill switch (B11.27), model artefact (ch25), feature store (ch24), training--serving skew (ch24), model registry (ch25), prediction target (B7.6), forecast horizon (B7.6), information coefficient (B7.6), in-sample (B7.20), out-of-sample (B7.20), cross-validation (B4.16).
- **Tutorial.** Monitor the Chapter 29 model over simulated sessions with three planted failures (a unit change upstream, a regime change, a slowly degrading feature) using PSI, Kolmogorov-Smirnov and prediction-drift statistics and a sequential test on realised IC; measure each alarm's delay at a fixed false-alarm rate; run a challenger in shadow, promote it through a canary with firm.abtest, and roll back on a performance alarm. Data: synthetic.
- **Build.** `firm.mlmonitor`: input, output and outcome monitors (PSI, KS, prediction drift, rolling IC with a sequential test), alarm thresholds calibrated to a false-alarm rate, shadow and canary orchestration on firm.abtest, and a rollback policy tied to the registry; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The Tuesday the model went quiet -- named result: each monitor's detection delay for the three planted failures at one false alarm a month.
- **Facts to verify.** Breck, Cai, Nielsen, Salib and Sculley 2017 the ML test score (IEEE Big Data); Rabanser, Gunnemann and Lipton 2019 failing loudly: detecting dataset shift (NeurIPS); Siddiqi 2006 Credit Risk Scorecards (PSI thresholds); Sculley et al. 2015; Shankar et al. 2024 operationalizing machine learning: an interview study (CSCW) or equivalent.
