# 9. Agriculturals and Softs — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** At noon Eastern time on the last business day of June the US Department of Agriculture publishes planted acreage; corn futures move by the daily limit within a minute.
- **Sections.** Crop calendars and the old-crop, new-crop spread; Government reports; Weather; Daily limits; Positioning reports.
- **Defines.** crop year, old crop, new crop, stocks-to-use ratio, WASDE report, limit-locked market, expanded limit, Commitments of Traders report, managed money, softs, crush spread.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** price limit, calendar spread, speculator, hedger, commodity trading advisor, open interest, futures commission merchant, backwardation, physical delivery, position limit.
- **Tutorial.** Parse the CFTC disaggregated Commitments of Traders files (public domain) for corn, compute managed money's net position as a share of open interest, and plot it over ten years.
- **Build.** `firm.cot`: positioning parser (report categories, net positions, z-scores) and limit-day state for `firm.calendar` (limit-locked, expanded limit).
- **Weekend problem.** Report day — named result: the margin a trader must hold to survive two limit-locked days at normal then expanded limits without being able to exit.
- **Facts to verify.** WASDE and Acreage release times (USDA); CBOT corn daily limit, its semi-annual reset and the expanded limit (CME rule); COT release schedule (Friday 15:30 ET for Tuesday data) and categories (CFTC); US corn marketing year (USDA); soybean crush yields per bushel (USDA or CME); ICE cocoa price surge 2024 (a public source); arabica and cocoa exchange specifications (ICE).
