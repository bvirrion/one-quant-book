# 23. DeFi Credit and Leverage — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In October 2022 a trader pushed the price of a thin token up on a few venues, borrowed over a hundred million dollars from a lending protocol against it, and left the protocol with the worthless collateral.
- **Sections.** Lending pools; Liquidations; Flash loans; Liquid staking, restaking and the staking yield; Algorithmic stablecoins and oracle-manipulation attacks.
- **Defines.** lending pool, kinked interest-rate curve, loan-to-value ratio, health factor, liquidation bonus, flash loan, staking yield, liquid staking token, restaking, algorithmic stablecoin, oracle manipulation.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** utilisation, stablecoin, depeg, smart contract, oracle, haircut, repurchase agreement, leverage, margin call, proof of stake, validator, liquidation engine, automated market maker.
- **Tutorial.** Simulate a lending pool: borrow rate as a kinked function of utilisation, a borrower's health factor through a price fall, a liquidation with close factor and bonus, and a liquidation funded by a flash loan.
- **Build.** `firm.lendpool`: lending pool engine (supply and borrow indices, kinked rates, health factor, liquidation with close factor and bonus, flash loans that must repay within the transaction); uses `firm.amm` for collateral sales.
- **Weekend problem.** The oracle pump — named result: the price pump on the oracle's source markets needed to borrow a pool dry for stated collateral and loan-to-value, and the attacker's cost against the gain.
- **Facts to verify.** Aave v3 interest-rate model and liquidation parameters (Aave docs, dated); Mango Markets October 2022 ($110 million; CFTC, SEC and DOJ complaints January 2023); Eisenberg conviction April 2024 and its vacatur (court record); Terra-Luna collapse May 2022 and Anchor rate (a regulator or court source: SEC v. Terraform); Lido share of staked ether and staking yield (dated); EigenLayer restaking (docs); bZx February 2020 flash-loan attacks (a public analysis); flash-loan fees (Aave docs, dated).
