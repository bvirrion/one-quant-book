# 16. Spot Markets — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** The same bitcoin trades on twenty venues at twenty prices; in Seoul it once traded a fifth above the rest of the world for weeks.
- **Sections.** Fragmentation and price formation across venues; Stablecoin quote pairs; Triangular and cross-venue arbitrage; Transfer latency as risk; Wash trading and measuring real liquidity.
- **Defines.** triangular arbitrage, cross-venue arbitrage, kimchi premium, prepositioned inventory, transfer latency, wash trading, quoted depth.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** currency pair, quote currency, base currency, cross rate, mid price, bid--ask spread, maker-taker pricing, volume tier, stablecoin, depeg, finality, centralised exchange, rate limit, capital control.
- **Tutorial.** Scan synthetic books on three venues for triangular and cross-venue cycles that pay after fees, then apply two published wash-trading tests (round-size clustering and the trade-size distribution) to a synthetic tape with injected wash trades.
- **Build.** `firm.triarb`: arbitrage scanner across venues and pairs (fee tiers, withdrawal fees, transfer time from `firm.gasfee`, inventory limits).
- **Weekend problem.** The kimchi premium — named result: the premium below which the round trip (buy abroad, transfer, sell in won, repatriate under capital controls) loses money, and why the premium persisted.
- **Facts to verify.** kimchi premium magnitudes and persistence (Makarov and Schoar 2020, Journal of Financial Economics); Bitwise 2019 presentation to the SEC on reported versus real volume; Cong, Li, Tang and Yang (2023) wash trading study; Korean real-name account rules (official source); stablecoin share of quote currencies (a dated public dataset).
