# 5. Power Markets I: Design — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** At noon on a sunny holiday the German day-ahead price was deeply negative: producers paid consumers to take their electricity.
- **Sections.** Electricity as a commodity; The day-ahead auction and its clearing algorithm; Merit order, spark and dark spreads; Zonal and nodal pricing; Capacity markets, ancillary services and negative prices.
- **Defines.** market time unit, day-ahead market, merit order, spark spread, dark spread, market coupling, block order, bidding zone, locational marginal price, congestion rent, capacity market, ancillary service.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** uniform-price auction, call auction, uncrossing price, price limit, pay-as-bid auction (Book 4), linear programme (Book 4).
- **Tutorial.** Clear one hour of a day-ahead auction from stepwise supply and demand bids; couple two zones through an interconnector with a capacity limit, observe price convergence or split and the congestion rent, and reproduce a negative price when inflexible supply exceeds demand.
- **Build.** `firm.dayahead`: day-ahead clearing engine (stepwise curves per market time unit, two-zone coupling with an exchange limit, block orders by enumeration at small scale).
- **Weekend problem.** The day with too much sun — named result: the negative clearing price of a stated hour and the feed-in premium below which a subsidised solar plant keeps producing through it.
- **Facts to verify.** Single Day-Ahead Coupling: gate closure 12:00 CET, EUPHEMIA algorithm (NEMO Committee documents); SDAC 15-minute market time unit go-live date; harmonised maximum and minimum clearing prices of SDAC (ACER decision); German negative-price hours per year (Bundesnetzagentur/SMARD); record negative prices (a dated example from SMARD or EPEX); PJM/ERCOT nodal pricing (LMP) documentation; ERCOT February 2021 price cap USD 9,000/MWh (PUCT); capacity markets: PJM RPM, GB Capacity Market (official sites); SMARD data licence (CC BY 4.0).
