# 27. Prediction and Betting Markets — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On the night of the 2024 US election, contracts paying one dollar if a candidate won traded on a regulated exchange and on a blockchain venue at prices a few cents apart.
- **Sections.** Bookmakers, overround and implied probabilities; Betting exchanges; Event contracts on and off chain; How trading firms make these markets; The favourite--longshot bias.
- **Defines.** bookmaker, overround, implied probability, betting exchange, back bet, lay bet, in-play betting, event contract, resolution source, favourite--longshot bias.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** odds, Kelly criterion, edge, market maker, designated contract market, oracle, stablecoin, adverse selection, price-time priority.
- **Tutorial.** Convert bookmaker odds to probabilities with the multiplicative, additive, power and Shin methods, compare them with exchange prices, and measure a favourite--longshot bias on a synthetic book of results.
- **Build.** `firm.odds`: odds conversion and overround removal, exchange commission, back and lay hedging to an equal outcome.
- **Weekend problem.** Election night — named result: the price gap between two venues' contracts on the same event that survives fees, and the Kelly stake on the cheaper one for a stated belief.
- **Facts to verify.** Kalshi v. CFTC (district court September 2024; appeal withdrawn 2025); Polymarket 2024 election volume (a dated public dataset); CFTC 2022 order against Polymarket; Polymarket's US re-entry (company or CFTC source); Betfair Exchange commission (dated); UMA optimistic oracle resolution (UMA docs); Shin (1993); Snowberg and Wolfers (2010) on the favourite--longshot bias.
