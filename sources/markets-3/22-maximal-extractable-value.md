# 22. Maximal Extractable Value — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A swap of one million dollars on a public pool was bought in front of and sold behind within the same block; the searcher kept a fraction of the profit and paid the rest to the builder of the block.
- **Sections.** Ordering power and extractable value; Front-running and the sandwich, with full profit arithmetic; Back-running, exchange-to-chain arbitrage and liquidation races; Gas auctions, proposer--builder separation and bundles; Private order flow, rollup sequencing and mitigations.
- **Defines.** maximal extractable value, searcher, front-running, sandwich attack, slippage tolerance, back-running, CEX--DEX arbitrage, priority gas auction, proposer--builder separation, block builder, MEV relay, transaction bundle, private order flow, order-flow auction.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** mempool, priority fee, base fee, gas, validator, sequencer, rollup, automated market maker, constant-product market maker, liquidation engine, payment for order flow, adverse selection, cross-venue arbitrage.
- **Tutorial.** Given a constant-product pool and a victim swap with its slippage tolerance, compute the attacker's optimal front-run size, its profit after gas and the builder tip, and the victim's loss; show that the tolerance bounds both.
- **Build.** `firm.sandwich`: sandwich and back-run simulator (optimal front-run size, profit decomposition, builder payment, detection of a sandwich in a block); uses `firm.amm`.
- **Weekend problem.** Anatomy of a sandwich — named result: the attacker's maximal net profit and the victim's loss on a stated swap in a stated pool at 0.5 and 1 percent slippage tolerance.
- **Facts to verify.** Daian et al. (2019) Flash Boys 2.0; MEV-Boost and proposer--builder separation (Flashbots docs, launch at the Merge); share of blocks built through MEV-Boost and builder concentration (a dated public dataset); US v. Peraire-Bueno (April 2023 exploit of MEV bots) and trial outcome (DOJ/court); private RPC and order-flow auction designs (Flashbots Protect, MEV-Share docs); rollup sequencing and first-come-first-served policies (rollup docs).
