# 21. On-Chain Order Books and Perpetual DEXs — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On one decentralised venue every order, cancel and liquidation of a perpetual order book is a transaction in a block, and the order in which a block processes them decides who gets filled.
- **Sections.** Why an on-chain order book is hard; Hyperliquid in detail: consensus, book, priority rules, vault, oracle and liquidations; Incidents; App-chain and oracle-priced designs; The Solana venues.
- **Defines.** perpetual DEX, on-chain order book, app-chain, in-block priority rule, liquidity vault, oracle-priced exchange, open-interest cap.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** perpetual future, mark price, funding rate, auto-deleveraging, liquidation engine, insurance fund, oracle, validator, finality, consensus protocol, price-time priority, post-only order, mempool, automated market maker, decentralised exchange.
- **Tutorial.** Replay a block of maker quotes, cancels and taker orders under two in-block orderings (arrival order, cancels and post-only first) and measure how many stale quotes are picked off under each.
- **Build.** `firm.blockbook`: block-batched order book with a configurable in-block priority rule, deterministic replay, oracle-based mark price and open-interest caps; uses `firm.perp` and `firm.liquidation`.
- **Weekend problem.** The squeezed vault — named result: the liquidity vault's loss as a function of the size of a squeeze on a thin market after it inherits a liquidated position, and the open-interest cap that bounds it.
- **Facts to verify.** Hyperliquid documentation: HyperBFT, HyperCore, order types and in-block ordering, oracle (validator-published weighted median, update frequency), HLP vault, liquidations (docs, dated); March 2025 incidents (ETH liquidation and HLP loss; JELLY delisting) from Hyperliquid announcements; October 2025 ADL on Hyperliquid (Hyperliquid statement); dYdX v4 launch on its own chain (October 2023, dYdX); GMX oracle-priced design (GMX docs); Solana order-book venues (Phoenix, OpenBook) and perps (Drift, Jupiter) from their docs; perp DEX volume shares (a dated public dataset).
