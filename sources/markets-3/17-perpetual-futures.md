# 17. Perpetual Futures — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Every eight hours, on the hour in UTC, longs and shorts in perpetual futures pay each other a funding amount that keeps a contract with no expiry tied to spot.
- **Sections.** A future that never expires; Index price and mark price; The premium index and the funding formula; Linear, inverse and quanto contracts; Cash-and-carry and funding arbitrage.
- **Defines.** perpetual future, index price, mark price, premium index, funding rate, funding interval, linear contract, inverse contract, quanto contract, funding arbitrage.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** futures contract, cash-and-carry trade, basis, convexity, notional, mark-to-market, variation margin, leverage, centralised exchange.
- **Tutorial.** Implement a venue's published funding formula (premium index sampled over the interval, interest component, clamp) on a synthetic premium series, and show the convexity of an inverse contract's P&L in dollars.
- **Build.** `firm.perp`: perpetual contract engine (index from constituent venues with outlier rules, mark price, premium sampling, funding, linear, inverse and quanto P&L).
- **Weekend problem.** The funding trade — named result: the annualised net return of a delta-neutral long-spot, short-perpetual position over a year of funding, and the margin the short leg needs to survive a 30 percent rally.
- **Facts to verify.** BitMEX perpetual launch (May 2016, BitMEX publication); Binance funding formula, interval and clamp (Binance docs, dated); BIS working paper on crypto carry (Schmeling, Schrimpf and Todorov); share of perpetuals in crypto derivatives volume (a dated public report); XBTUSD inverse contract terms (BitMEX); ETHUSD quanto terms (BitMEX).
