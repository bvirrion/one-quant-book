# 19. Dated Futures, Options and ETFs — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In January 2024 the US securities regulator approved spot bitcoin exchange-traded products; within a year one of them held tens of billions of dollars, and hedge funds bought the funds and sold futures against them.
- **Sections.** Dated futures on crypto venues and on regulated exchanges; The regulated-futures basis trade; The dominant options venue's conventions: inverse options; The crypto volatility surface; Spot ETFs: creation and redemption.
- **Defines.** dated future, coin-margined contract, inverse option, crypto reference rate, exchange-traded product, cash creation, grantor trust.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** exchange-traded fund, authorised participant, creation unit, in-kind transfer, premium, net asset value, basis trade, cash-and-carry trade, futures contract, implied volatility, volatility skew, volatility index, premium-adjusted delta, put--call parity, inverse contract, Black--Scholes model (Book 5).
- **Tutorial.** Convert inverse option premiums quoted in coin to dollars and derive the delta that includes the premium currency; then compute the annualised regulated-futures basis and the carry of an ETF-versus-futures position.
- **Build.** `firm.cryptoopt`: inverse options and coin-margined conversions (premium in coin, dollar value, premium-including delta) and basis annualisation for dated futures; uses `firm.parity`.
- **Weekend problem.** The ETF basis trade — named result: the annualised carry of long spot-ETF, short front-month regulated future, net of financing and fees, and its return on margin.
- **Facts to verify.** SEC approval of spot bitcoin ETPs, 10 January 2024 (SEC order); in-kind creation and redemption approval (SEC, 2025); largest spot bitcoin ETP assets (issuer page, dated); CME bitcoin futures launch December 2017 (CME release); Deribit conventions: inverse options, 08:00 UTC expiry, index (Deribit docs); Coinbase acquisition of Deribit (Coinbase release); CME CF Bitcoin Reference Rate methodology; GBTC discount history and conversion (issuer filings); options on spot bitcoin ETFs approval and launch (SEC/OCC notices).
