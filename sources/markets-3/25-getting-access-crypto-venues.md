# 25. Getting Access: Crypto Venues — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A new firm wants to quote on five crypto venues; its first month's fees are ten times what a large market maker pays for the same trades, and its connections are throttled at a tenth of their rate.
- **Sections.** Entities, onboarding and sub-accounts; Fee schedules, VIP tiers and tier matching; Market-maker programmes and their obligations; Rate limits and dedicated endpoints; Loan lines, off-exchange settlement and custody.
- **Defines.** know-your-customer check, tier matching, uptime requirement, dedicated endpoint, loan line, collateral mirroring.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** volume tier, incentive programme, quoting obligation, market-maker appointment, sub-account, API key, rate limit, request weight, off-exchange settlement, maker-taker pricing, prime broker, legal entity identifier.
- **Tutorial.** Given a month's volume and maker share across five venues and their published fee schedules, compute each venue's effective fee, the saving from concentrating volume, and the value of a tier match.
- **Build.** `firm.mmprogram`: market-maker programme monitor (uptime per window, maximum spread, minimum depth within a band, rebate accrual by tier); uses `firm.ratelimit` and `firm.feesched`.
- **Weekend problem.** Five venues, one budget — named result: the monthly fee saving from concentrating volume on two venues against spreading it over five, and the uptime the firm must keep to retain the rebate.
- **Facts to verify.** published VIP tier tables of three venues (dated); published market-maker programme terms (uptime, spread, depth) of venues that publish them (dated); tier-matching offers (venue announcements, dated); institutional onboarding requirements (venue docs); dedicated or market-maker endpoints where documented (venue docs); off-exchange settlement services (provider publications).
