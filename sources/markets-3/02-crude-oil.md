# 2. Crude Oil — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 20 April 2020 the May WTI contract settled at minus 37.63 dollars a barrel: holders paid to be rid of oil they could not store.
- **Sections.** Crude quality and the benchmarks; The North Sea complex: Dated Brent, forward Brent and the CFDs; The US inland benchmark and Cushing; Middle-East pricing: official selling prices and Dubai; 20 April 2020 step by step.
- **Defines.** API gravity, sweet crude, sour crude, benchmark crude, Dated Brent, forward Brent, Brent CFD, official selling price, calendar month average, last trading day.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** futures contract, physical delivery, front month, open interest, daily settlement price, cash settlement, exchange for physical, contango, backwardation, roll, exchange-traded fund, price-reporting agency, assessment window, price differential, pricing period.
- **Tutorial.** From EIA daily NYMEX WTI settlements (contracts 1-4, public domain) plot the front and second-month spread through April 2020 and compute the storage cost per barrel-month the spread implied.
- **Build.** `firm.crude`: crude toolkit (quality escalators on API gravity and sulphur, Dated Brent from the basket with quality premia, calendar month average and WTI roll adjustment, CL expiry calendar); reads `firm.contracts`.
- **Weekend problem.** The last day of the May contract — a long who cannot take delivery must sell before expiry; named result: the delivery-cost bound (storage plus transport per barrel) against the observed settlement, i.e. the break-even storage cost implied by the April 2020 contango.
- **Facts to verify.** CLK20 settlement -37.63 on 20 April 2020 (CME/EIA); CFTC Interim Staff Report on 20 April 2020 (November 2020); Cushing working storage capacity and utilisation, April 2020 (EIA); CL last-trading-day rule and delivery at Cushing (CME rulebook via CFTC filing); Dated Brent basket (Brent, Forties, Oseberg, Ekofisk, Troll, WTI Midland since June 2023) and Platts methodology; ICE Brent futures cash settlement against the ICE Brent Index; Saudi Aramco official selling prices (monthly publication); Dubai/Oman benchmarks, IFAD Murban futures launch 2021; USO changes to its roll in April 2020 (SEC filings); API gravity and sulphur of the main grades (a public assay source).
