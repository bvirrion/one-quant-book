# 24. The Crypto Trading Business — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A token project lends a market maker two percent of its supply and grants it call options on the same amount; in October 2024 US authorities charged several market makers over wash trading for such clients.
- **Sections.** Token market-making agreements; OTC desks; Treasury and custody operations; Round-the-clock operations; Regulation.
- **Defines.** token market-making agreement, loan-plus-call structure, token unlock, self-custody, hot wallet, cold wallet, multi-party computation, travel rule, crypto-asset service provider, Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** call option, market maker, request for quote, wash trading, private key, stablecoin, centralised exchange, sub-account, implied volatility, delta hedging, Monte Carlo simulation (Book 4).
- **Tutorial.** Value a loan-plus-call deal by simulation (a token loan for a year with three call strikes), derive the fee it implies, and show the short delta the market maker must hold at launch.
- **Build.** `firm.tokenloan`: market-making agreement valuation (loan, call strip, implied fee, delta schedule, obligation monitoring); posts to `firm.ledger`.
- **Weekend problem.** The listing deal — named result: the value of the call package as a share of the loaned tokens' value, and the tokens the market maker sells on day one to hedge it.
- **Facts to verify.** Operation Token Mirrors, October 2024 (SEC and DOJ releases: Gotbit, ZM Quant, CLS Global); MiCA application dates (30 June 2024 and 30 December 2024) and transitional periods (ESMA); GENIUS Act signed July 2025 (official source); FATF Recommendation 16 travel rule and its crypto guidance; EU Transfer of Funds Regulation 2023/1113; custody failures (e.g. QuadrigaCX, court or regulator source).
