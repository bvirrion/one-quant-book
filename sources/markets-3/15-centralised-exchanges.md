# 15. Centralised Exchanges — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In November 2022 a large crypto exchange stopped withdrawals, and its customers learned that the venue had been their broker, their custodian, their clearing house and, with their own money, their counterparty.
- **Sections.** The exchange that is also broker, custodian and clearing house; Accounts, matching and interfaces; Fees, rebates and market-maker programmes; The exchange as counterparty: FTX and proof of reserves; Off-exchange settlement.
- **Defines.** centralised exchange, sub-account, API key, rate limit, request weight, post-only order, proof of reserves, proof of liabilities, customer asset segregation, off-exchange settlement.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** exchange, broker, clearing, central counterparty, market maker, maker-taker pricing, volume tier, incentive programme, price-time priority, stablecoin, private key, market data feed.
- **Tutorial.** Verify a proof-of-reserves Merkle inclusion proof (SHA-256 tree of account liabilities) and show, with a constructed counter-example, what the proof does not establish.
- **Build.** `firm.ratelimit`: weight-based rate-limit governor (token buckets per venue and endpoint, order-count limits, backoff on 429 and bans on 418), in C++20 and Rust with a Python reference.
- **Weekend problem.** The hole in the balance sheet — named result: the share of customer claims covered by liquid assets at the petition date, from the bankruptcy filings.
- **Facts to verify.** FTX timeline: withdrawals halted 8 November 2022, Chapter 11 on 11 November 2022 (court filings); FTX debtors' reports on the shortfall; recovery and distributions (court documents); US v. Bankman-Fried conviction November 2023; Binance API rate-limit weights and order limits (Binance docs, dated); fee schedules of two venues (dated); proof-of-reserves Merkle schemes published by exchanges; off-exchange settlement services named only with the provider's publication; Mt. Gox 2014 (court or official source).
