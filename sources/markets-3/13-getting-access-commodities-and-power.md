# 13. Getting Access: Commodities and Power — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A new firm wants to trade German power spreads; before its first order it needs a clearing member, an exchange admission, a balancing-group contract with a grid operator, a registration with the energy regulator and collateral for all four.
- **Sections.** Exchanges and clearing for commodity futures; The metal exchange's membership categories; Balancing groups and nominations; Physical logistics: credit lines, letters of credit and trade finance; Licences and registrations before the first trade.
- **Defines.** ring dealing member, designated contract market, REMIT, ancillary activity exemption, accountability level, letter of credit, trade finance, balancing group, schedule nomination, market-based rate authorisation.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** futures commission merchant, exchange membership, clearing member, give-up, position limit, initial margin, balancing responsible party, imbalance volume, gate closure, incentive programme, commodity trading house.
- **Tutorial.** Compare the annual cost and collateral of three routes into German power (exchange through a clearing member, OTC through a broker with bilateral credit, and physical as a balancing responsible party) for a stated trading plan.
- **Build.** `firm.nominate`: schedule nominations for a balancing group (per market time unit, balanced portfolio check, gate-closure check, imbalance exposure) with a letter-of-credit exposure check for physical counterparties; uses `firm.balancing`.
- **Weekend problem.** First trade in German power — named result: the collateral a new entrant posts before trading 50 MW baseload for a year (exchange margin, balancing-group security and credit support) and its annual carrying cost.
- **Facts to verify.** LME membership categories 1-5 and the Ring (LME rulebook); REMIT (Regulation 1227/2011) and REMIT II (2024/1106): registration, ACER code, reporting; MiFID II ancillary activity exemption (Delegated Regulation 2021/1833); CFTC position limits rule (January 2021) and accountability levels; EEX and ECC clearing membership (ECC); German balancing group contract (BNetzA standard contract); FERC market-based rate authorisation (18 CFR 35.36); CFTC designated contract markets list; ICE Futures Europe membership categories.
