# 4. Natural Gas and LNG — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In late August 2022 the Dutch front-month gas contract traded above 300 euros a megawatt-hour, more than ten times its level two years earlier; a US cargo that cost a few dollars per million Btu to buy sold in Europe for many times that.
- **Sections.** Gas as a network commodity: hubs and units; Locational basis and pipelines; Storage and seasonality; LNG: liquefaction, shipping, regasification and cargo arbitrage; The 2022 crisis.
- **Defines.** virtual trading point, Henry Hub, Title Transfer Facility, locational basis, gas day, injection season, summer--winter spread, liquefied natural gas, destination flexibility, oil indexation, Japan Korea Marker.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** netback, physical market, price differential, contango, backwardation, calendar spread, margin call, initial margin, liquidity spiral, free on board.
- **Tutorial.** Compute the netbacks of a US FOB cargo delivered to Europe and to Asia from Henry Hub, a tolling fee, voyage days, charter rate and boil-off, using World Bank monthly prices (CC BY 4.0), and decide the destination month by month.
- **Build.** `firm.lngarb`: cargo arbitrage and netback engine (Henry Hub-linked purchase price, shipping cost by route, regasification, destination choice).
- **Weekend problem.** Where should the cargo go — named result: the JKM minus TTF spread at which a flexible US cargo is indifferent between Europe and Asia, given the voyage-length difference and the charter rate.
- **Facts to verify.** NYMEX Henry Hub contract (10,000 MMBtu); TTF front-month peak in August 2022 (ICE Endex or a public reporting source); EU gas storage regulation 2022/1032 (fill targets) and its 2025 amendment; EU market correction mechanism (Council Regulation 2022/2578); Nord Stream flow halt and September 2022 damage (official statements); Henry Hub x 1.15 plus fixed fee in US LNG sale contracts (Cheniere public SPA terms or filing); US LNG export volumes (EIA); gas day definitions (EU network code; US NAESB); unit conversions MMBtu, therm, MWh; Uniper rescue 2022 (German government statement); World Bank Pink Sheet licence (CC BY 4.0).
