# 7. Emissions and Environmental Markets — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A steel plant must surrender one allowance for each tonne of carbon dioxide it emitted last year; in February 2023 an allowance cost about 100 euros, more than the plant's margin on a tonne of steel.
- **Sections.** Cap and trade; The European system: auctions, free allocation, surrender; The market stability reserve; Other systems and the border adjustment; Certificates of origin and carbon credits.
- **Defines.** cap-and-trade, emission allowance, allowance surrender, free allocation, market stability reserve, total number of allowances in circulation, clean spark spread, clean dark spread, switching price, carbon border adjustment mechanism, guarantee of origin, renewable energy certificate, carbon credit.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** spark spread, dark spread, merit order, uniform-price auction, futures contract.
- **Tutorial.** Recompute the market stability reserve's intake from the Commission's published TNAC figures (intake rate and thresholds, invalidation rule), and the coal-to-gas switching price from fuel prices and plant efficiencies.
- **Build.** `firm.carbon`: compliance book (allowances held against verified emissions, surrender schedule, clean spreads from `firm.dayahead` prices, MSR intake from TNAC).
- **Weekend problem.** The utility's carbon hedge — named result: the allowance price at which gas-fired generation becomes cheaper than coal (the switching price) for stated fuel prices and efficiencies, and the allowances a utility must buy forward for next year's output.
- **Facts to verify.** EU ETS start 2005 and phases (Directive 2003/87/EC and amendments); MSR start 2019, intake rate 24 percent, thresholds 833 and 1,096 million, invalidation above auction volumes (Decision (EU) 2015/1814 as amended 2023); latest TNAC publication (Commission, May); surrender deadline moved to 30 September from 2024; ETS2 start date and any postponement; EUA price record (February 2023, a public source); CBAM definitive period from 1 January 2026 (Regulation 2023/956) and later simplification; UK ETS (2021), RGGI, California-Quebec, China national ETS (2021) official sites; guarantees of origin (Directive 2018/2001).
