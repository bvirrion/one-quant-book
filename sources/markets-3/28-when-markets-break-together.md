# 28. When Markets Break Together — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In the second week of March 2020 equities, gold, long Treasuries and bitcoin all fell together: everything that could be sold was sold.
- **Sections.** The mechanics of joint stress: fire sales, loss and margin spirals; 2008; March 2020; 2022: energy margins, nickel, gilts and crypto; What each desk saw.
- **Defines.** fire sale, loss spiral, margin spiral, flight to quality, correlation breakdown.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** liquidity spiral, procyclicality, margin call, dash for cash, liability-driven investment, variation margin, initial margin, basis trade, liquidation cascade, algorithmic stablecoin, depeg, wrong-way risk (Book 6), stress test (Book 6).
- **Tutorial.** Simulate a margin spiral across three assets held by leveraged investors (margins rising with volatility, forced sales with price impact) and measure how correlations between the assets rise in the stress; check stability under a finer time step and ablate each mechanism.
- **Build.** `firm.marginspiral`: multi-asset margin-spiral simulator (holders, margin rules, forced-sale impact, ablation switches).
- **Weekend problem.** March 2020 across the desks — named result: the forced-sale multiplier (total forced sales per dollar of initial margin shortfall) of the spiral for stated leverage and impact.
- **Facts to verify.** Brunnermeier and Pedersen (2009) liquidity spirals; March 2020 market moves and the FSB Holistic Review (November 2020); Lehman bankruptcy 15 September 2008 and 2008 cross-asset moves (official sources); 2022 energy-market margin calls (ESMA or ECB report; Uniper); Terra May 2022, Three Arrows June 2022, FTX November 2022 (court or regulator sources); the 2022 gilt crisis (reuse Book 2 ledger rows).
