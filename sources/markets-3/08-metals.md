# 8. Metals — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 8 March 2022 London nickel doubled in a morning to above 100,000 dollars a tonne; the exchange suspended trading and cancelled the day's trades.
- **Sections.** The London metal market: prompt dates and official prices; Warehouses, warrants and queues; Nickel, March 2022; Gold: the London market against futures, and March 2020; Iron ore.
- **Defines.** prompt date, three-month price, LME official price, warehouse warrant, cancelled warrant, load-out queue, LME lending rule, loco London, unallocated gold, LBMA Gold Price.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** tom-next, exchange for physical, backwardation, contango, margin call, initial margin, variation margin, short squeeze, clearly erroneous trade, cost of carry, physical delivery.
- **Tutorial.** Build the LME prompt-date calendar (daily to three months, weekly to six, monthly beyond) and price a carry from cash to three months; then compute the variation margin on a short nickel position over 7 and 8 March 2022.
- **Build.** `firm.prompts`: LME prompt-date calendar and carry calculator (cash, tom, three-month date, third Wednesdays, holiday rolls) with a margin-call estimator.
- **Weekend problem.** Tuesday 8 March 2022 — named result: the variation margin a short of a stated tonnage owed between the close of 7 March and the pre-suspension high, and the loss the cancellation removed.
- **Facts to verify.** LME nickel prices and timeline 7-8 March 2022 (LME notices); cancellation of trades after 00:00 UK on 8 March and suspension until 16 March (LME); value of cancelled trades (LME or court judgment); High Court judgment Elliott and Jane Street v LME (2023) and appeal outcome; FCA final notice to LME (2025) and fine; LME prompt-date structure (LME rulebook); LBMA Gold Price: administrator IBA, auction times; March 2020 EFP dislocation between COMEX and London (a public report); SGX iron ore swaps and DCE futures (exchange pages); LME lending rules for dominant positions (LME).
