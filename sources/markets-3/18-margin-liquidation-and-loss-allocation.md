# 18. Margin, Liquidation and Loss Allocation — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 10 October 2025 crypto venues liquidated the largest volume of leveraged positions in their history within a day, and some closed winning positions of other traders to cover the losses.
- **Sections.** Isolated, cross and portfolio margin; The liquidation price; Liquidation engines; Insurance funds, auto-deleveraging and socialised losses; A liquidation cascade dissected.
- **Defines.** isolated margin, cross margin, liquidation price, bankruptcy price, liquidation engine, insurance fund, auto-deleveraging, socialised loss, liquidation cascade.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** initial margin, maintenance margin, variation margin, portfolio margining, leverage, default waterfall, default fund, mark price, perpetual future, liquidity spiral, margin call.
- **Tutorial.** Derive liquidation prices for linear and inverse contracts, then simulate a cascade: leveraged longs spread over liquidation prices, an order book of stated depth, each liquidation selling into it; test the result's stability under a finer time step and ablate each mechanism.
- **Build.** `firm.liquidation`: margin and liquidation engine (isolated and cross, tiered maintenance, liquidation into the book, insurance fund, auto-deleveraging ranking); uses `firm.perp`.
- **Weekend problem.** Anatomy of a cascade — named result: the initial price shock above which liquidations become self-sustaining for a stated leverage distribution and depth, and the loss that reaches the insurance fund and then auto-deleveraging.
- **Facts to verify.** 10-11 October 2025 liquidation totals (a dated data source) and venue statements on ADL; Binance tiered maintenance margin (docs, dated); insurance fund balances published by venues (dated); ADL ranking formulas (venue docs); BitMEX 12-13 March 2020 events (BitMEX publication); FTX backstop liquidity provider programme (public page, archived).
