# 28. Observability and Incident Response — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 22 August 2013 the system that disseminates quotes for one of the US equity exchanges' listed securities stopped for about three hours; trading in those stocks halted across all venues. Somewhere in every trading firm, a dashboard stayed green because it measured the firm's own processes, not the data they were waiting for.
- **Sections.** Metrics, logs and traces; Service levels and error budgets; Alerting that people act on; On call and incident command; An incident replayed, and the review that follows.
- **Defines.** observability, metric, structured logging, distributed tracing, span, service-level indicator, service-level objective, error budget, burn-rate alert, on-call rotation, incident commander, runbook, blameless review, time to detect, time to restore.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** latency histogram (B13.5), tail latency (B13.1), binary log (B13.23), operational risk (B6.28), operational loss event (B6.28), model monitoring (B12.27), performance alarm (B12.27), CUSUM test (B7.13), service-level agreement (B14.15), data-flow map (ch1), data lineage (ch7), position service (ch17).
- **Tutorial.** Instrument the platform's chain (capture, tick store, position service, real-time risk) with metrics, structured logs and trace spans; define service-level indicators and objectives; replay a scheduled feed outage and a slow consumer on firm.exchsim, and compare static-threshold alerts with multi-window burn-rate alerts on time to detect and false pages over many quiet days; write the incident's timeline from the traces and its blameless review. End state: time to detect against false pages a month for each alerting rule, and the incident's trace.
- **Build.** `firm.observe`: metric registry (counters, gauges, histograms on firm.lathist), structured log records, spans with trace and parent identifiers propagated across the chain, SLO definitions and error-budget accounting, threshold and multi-window burn-rate alert rules evaluated on the metric streams, an incident timeline builder; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Three hours of silence -- named result: the time to detect a feed stall and a slow consumer under a static threshold and under burn-rate alerts, and the false pages a month each costs, on the replayed incident and on thirty quiet days.
- **Facts to verify.** SEC or Nasdaq report on the 22 August 2013 SIP outage (Nasdaq UTP SIP): duration and halt; Beyer et al. 2016, Site Reliability Engineering: SLOs, error budgets, incident command; Google SRE workbook: multi-window multi-burn-rate alerts; Allspaw 2012, Blameless postmortems (Etsy engineering blog); OpenTelemetry specification: traces, spans, metrics.
- **Data.** firm.exchsim with scheduled outages and slow consumers; synthetic quiet days.
