# 29. Security — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In February 2025 a large crypto exchange lost about one and a half billion dollars of ether from a wallet that required several signatures: the attackers did not break the cryptography, they changed what the signers were shown. Security in a trading firm is mostly about the people and machines allowed to do things, and what they are allowed to see.
- **Sections.** What a trading firm protects, and from whom; Identity, access and least privilege; Secrets and keys; Segmentation and insider risk; Exchange and wallet credentials.
- **Defines.** threat model, least privilege, role-based access control, multi-factor authentication, secrets management, secret rotation, hardware security module, insider threat, data-loss prevention, zero-trust architecture, network segmentation, withdrawal allow-list.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** API key (B3.15), private key (B3.14), hot wallet (B3.24), cold wallet (B3.24), multi-party computation (B3.24), segregation of duties (B6.28), operational risk (B6.28), audit trail (B12.25), trade secret (B16.11), entitlement (ch23), structured logging (ch28).
- **Tutorial.** Write the firm's access policy as data (roles, resources, actions) and check it for toxic combinations under segregation-of-duties rules; issue, sign with HMAC (Book 13's signer) and rotate exchange API credentials with scopes (trade, no withdrawal) and an address allow-list; generate a year of access logs with a planted insider who copies strategy code and signal data slowly, and detect it with per-user baselines at a fixed false-alarm rate. End state: the toxic combinations found, and detection delay against false alarms for the insider.
- **Build.** `firm.accessctl`: policy model (roles, grants, deny rules, segregation-of-duties constraints) and evaluator, a secrets vault simulation (versions, leases, rotation schedule, expiry alerts), credential scopes for venue keys with allow-lists, access-log generation and per-user anomaly scoring with calibrated thresholds; Python, standard library hashing only.
- **Weekend problem.** The slow copy -- named result: the days until a planted insider's slow exfiltration is flagged at one false alarm a month per hundred users, and the number of toxic role combinations in the firm's policy before and after the fix.
- **Facts to verify.** FBI public service announcement on the February 2025 Bybit theft (attribution and amount); United States v. Aleynikov, 676 F.3d 71 (2d Cir. 2012): trading-code theft conviction reversed (outcome stated neutrally); NIST SP 800-207 Zero Trust Architecture (2020); NIST SP 800-63B digital identity (authenticators, dated); NIST SP 800-57 key management; Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report share of insider-caused breaches (dated).
- **Data.** Synthetic access logs and policies.
