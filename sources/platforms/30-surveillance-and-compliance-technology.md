# 30. Surveillance and Compliance Technology — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In September 2022 a US regulator announced penalties of more than a billion dollars against sixteen firms whose employees had discussed business on personal messaging apps that the firms did not archive: the rules were old, the phones were new, and the archive had a hole the size of a pocket.
- **Sections.** The surveillance pipeline; From alert to case; Communications archiving and surveillance; Transaction reporting; Measuring a surveillance system.
- **Defines.** trade surveillance system, surveillance alert, alert triage, case management system, communications archive, communications surveillance, off-channel communication, transaction reporting.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** spoofing (B9.29), layering (B9.29), marking the close (B9.29), wash trading (B3.16), trade reporting (B2.22), legal entity identifier (B2.28), audit trail (B12.25), precision--recall curve (B12.20), exchange simulator (B10.26), event log (B3.26), canonical trade model (ch21), data lineage (ch7), entitlement (ch23).
- **Tutorial.** Run a population with planted spoofing, marking-the-close and wash episodes on firm.exchsim, compute Book 9's account-day features from the orders and trades, score them with firm.surveil's detectors, and route alerts into a case-management queue with analyst capacity and ageing; search a synthetic communications archive with a lexicon; generate transaction reports from chapter 21's trades, validate them against a field specification and reconcile them with the trades. End state: alerts a day against analyst capacity for three thresholds, backlog over a quarter, and the report reconciliation.
- **Build.** `firm.survpipe`: feature extraction from exchsim orders and trades into firm.surveil's account-day schema, detector scoring and thresholds, an alert and case store with states, assignment and ageing, lexicon search over a message archive with retention holds, a transaction-report generator with field validation and a report-to-trade reconciliation; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** A hole the size of a pocket -- named result: the backlog after a quarter at each alert threshold for a team of fixed size, the planted episodes caught, and the share of transaction reports that fail validation or reconciliation before and after the fixes.
- **Facts to verify.** SEC press release 2022-174 (September 2022): off-channel communications penalties against sixteen firms; CFTC parallel action (September 2022); Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/590 (RTS 22) transaction reporting fields; SEC Rule 613 Consolidated Audit Trail (dated); SEC Rule 17a-4 records preservation (electronic storage, dated); ESMA or FCA data on transaction-report data quality (dated).
- **Data.** firm.exchsim populations with planted episodes; synthetic communications archive.

## EXCLUDED

- SEC Rule 613 (Consolidated Audit Trail) and Rule 17a-4: not fetched; the chapter names neither; the SEC release's "recordkeeping provisions" wording is used.
- ESMA or FCA statistics on transaction-report data quality: not fetched; the chapter gives none.
- The planted episodes are drawn from Book 9's firm.surveil account-day populations rather than from firm.exchsim runs (deviation from the brief: the feature extraction from order events, firm.survpipe.features, is tested on event streams, and the quarter uses Book 9's feature populations directly).
- LEI (ISO 17442, MOD 97-10) and ISIN (Luhn) check-digit rules are implemented and tested in code; the text names the check digits without describing the algorithms.
- All identifiers in the model are synthetic (FIRM..., CLNT..., CCPX..., XS...); no real LEI or ISIN of a named firm is printed except the well-known test ISIN in a unit test.
