# 26. Cloud Against On-Premises — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In November 2021 a large derivatives exchange announced a ten-year agreement to move its technology, including eventually its trading systems, to a public cloud provider; the same year, most trading firms still kept every order path in their own racks. Both decisions can be right, for different workloads.
- **Sections.** What moves to the cloud and what cannot; The economics of compute: owned, reserved, on demand, interruptible; Data gravity and egress; Hybrid research clusters; Security, control and regulators.
- **Defines.** on-demand capacity, reserved capacity, spot capacity, cloud bursting, data gravity, infrastructure as code, shared responsibility model.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** public cloud (B14.16), cloud region (B3.26), availability zone (B14.16), total cost of ownership (B16.20), compute cluster (ch13), job scheduler (ch13), makespan (ch13), training checkpoint (B12.23), storage tier (ch2), operational risk (B6.28).
- **Tutorial.** Model a research cluster's year: owned nodes (capital, power, space, staff, depreciation) against reserved, on-demand and spot capacity with preemption and checkpoint-restart from chapter 13's simulator, data egress for results and ingress of the tick store, and a hybrid policy that bursts beyond owned capacity; take every price from cited, dated price lists. End state: annual cost against utilisation for each option, with the break-even utilisation marked, and the burst sweep's cost on spot against on demand.
- **Build.** `firm.cloudcost`: price lists as dated data with sources, a TCO model for owned hardware, a capacity-purchase optimiser over reserved, on-demand and spot given a demand profile, preemption and checkpoint overheads from firm.jobgraph, egress and storage costs, and a hybrid bursting policy; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Own, rent or both -- named result: the utilisation above which owning a research cluster costs less than renting it at cited prices, and the cost of the chapter 13 sweep on spot capacity with preemption against on demand.
- **Facts to verify.** CME Group and Google Cloud announcement of a ten-year partnership (November 2021); Nasdaq and AWS announcement on moving markets to the cloud (2021); a cloud provider's on-demand, reserved and spot price for a compute instance (dated); a cloud provider's data-egress price per GB (dated); a cloud provider's shared-responsibility-model documentation; EBA Guidelines on outsourcing arrangements (2019) or equivalent regulator guidance on cloud outsourcing (dated).
- **Data.** Cited price lists (dated); simulated demand from chapter 13.
