# 17. Position and P\&L Services — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** At 10:12 the order-entry session dropped for four seconds; when it came back, the strategy believed it was flat, and the drop copy said it was long three thousand shares. The position service had to decide which one to believe, and the answer was: neither, until they agreed.
- **Sections.** Positions from executions; Two feeds of the same truth: sessions and drop copies; Busts, corrections and late fills; Intraday P\&L and end-of-day marks; Start of day and the books that close.
- **Defines.** position service, start-of-day position, trade-date position, settlement-date position, intraday P\&L, end-of-day mark, trade bust, execution identifier.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** position (B1.7), mark-to-market (B1.7), average cost (B1.7), realised P\&L (B1.7), unrealised P\&L (B1.7), settlement cycle (B1.5), drop copy (B13.21), execution report (B10.26), idempotent processing (B13.24), cancel on disconnect (B10.26), closing price (B1.13), product control (B6.27), stream processing (ch1).
- **Tutorial.** Run a strategy on firm.exchsim with a drop-copy session and a scheduled session drop; feed the gateway's execution reports and the drop copy into a position service that deduplicates by execution identifier, tolerates reordering, applies a trade bust sent later, marks intraday from the feed and at the close from the official closing price, and rolls the start-of-day position; reconcile against a simulated clearing statement. End state: the position and P\&L through the day from each source, and the reconciliation.
- **Build.** `firm.posservice`: event-sourced positions per account and instrument from executions (idempotent on execution identifier), drop-copy merge with source precedence and a pending state until sources agree, busts and corrections, trade-date and settlement-date views, intraday P\&L on marks (exact integer arithmetic through firm.pnl), end-of-day marks and roll, reconciliation report; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Four seconds without a session -- named result: the position and P\&L error at the close without the drop copy, with it, and with it plus the bust, over the scheduled drops of ten seeds, and the time until the service's position agrees with the clearing statement.
- **Facts to verify.** a venue's drop-copy service description (dated); SEC Rule 15c3-5 (market access rule) requirement for post-trade surveillance of positions (pointer to B11.27); FINRA or exchange rules on clearly erroneous executions and busts (pointer B1.31); SEC T+1 settlement cycle effective 28 May 2024 (pointer B1.5).
- **Data.** firm.exchsim with drop copy and scheduled session drops, generated by script.
