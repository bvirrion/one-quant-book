# 15. The Research Environment — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In 2013 a graduate student asked for the spreadsheet behind an influential paper on public debt and growth; a selection error that left five countries out of an average had changed its headline number. The error was not in the economics; it was in an environment where nobody could rerun the calculation from the top.
- **Sections.** Notebooks and their hidden state; From notebook to library; Review; Environments and containers; Reproducibility as a routine.
- **Defines.** computational notebook, hidden state, research library, code review, container image, monorepo.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** reproducible result (B7.29), data snapshot (B7.29), content-addressed storage (B7.29), environment lock (B7.29), research log (B7.1), research review (B7.1), review checklist (B7.29), pipeline stage (B7.29), experiment tracker (B12.25), data versioning (B12.25), bitwise reproducibility (B4.25).
- **Tutorial.** Take a planted notebook (as .ipynb JSON) whose saved result depends on cells run out of order and on a variable defined in a deleted cell; detect the hidden state from execution counts and names, rerun it top to bottom in a fresh interpreter and watch the number change; promote its useful function to a tested research library; capture the environment and data snapshot and reproduce the result from a clean checkout. End state: the result before and after, and an audit report of the notebook and the environment.
- **Build.** `firm.envaudit`: notebook audit from the .ipynb JSON (out-of-order execution, undefined-before-use names, outputs without sources), clean rerun of code cells as a script in a subprocess with a fresh interpreter, environment capture and comparison against a lock, data-snapshot check, and a promotion checklist; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Rerun from the top -- named result: the planted notebook's headline number as saved and as rerun, the audit findings that explain the difference, and the reproduction of the corrected result from a clean environment.
- **Facts to verify.** Herndon, Ash and Pollin 2014, Does high public debt consistently stifle economic growth? (Cambridge Journal of Economics): the spreadsheet error; Pimentel et al. 2019, A large-scale study about quality and reproducibility of Jupyter notebooks (MSR); Jupyter nbformat documentation; Potvin and Levenberg 2016, Why Google stores billions of lines of code in a single repository (CACM); Open Container Initiative image specification.
- **Data.** A planted notebook written by script; no external data.
