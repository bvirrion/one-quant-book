# 23. Market-Data Distribution and Entitlements — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** An exchange's auditor asked for the list of every person and every program that had used its data in the past two years; the firm could name forty screens but not the eleven applications that read the same feed, and each of those was a licence it had not paid for.
- **Sections.** Distributing data inside the firm; Licences, uses and units of count; Entitlements; Usage reporting and exchange audits; Derived data.
- **Defines.** market-data distribution platform, entitlement, entitlement system, usage report, market-data audit, derived data, unit of count.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** non-display fee (B1.29), market data feed (B1.4), direct feed (B1.9), securities information processor (B1.9), conflation (B13.12), slow consumer (B13.12), back-pressure (B13.12), publish--subscribe (ch24), tick store (ch4).
- **Tutorial.** Distribute a simulated feed inside the process to subscribers of different kinds (screens, a strategy, a risk service, a research job) with per-subscriber conflation; check every subscription against entitlements by user or application, data source and use; log usage; produce the monthly usage report a venue asks for and count what must be licensed, display and non-display, against what the firm holds, with fees from a cited schedule. End state: the usage report and the licence shortfall's cost.
- **Build.** `firm.entitle`: an in-process distribution bus with topics, per-subscriber queues and conflation, an entitlement model (subject, source, use, unit of count) and checks at subscribe and at delivery, a usage log, the usage report and an audit reconciliation of usage against licences; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Eleven applications -- named result: the licence shortfall in display users and non-display applications found by reconciling two years of usage logs, and its back-billed cost under a cited fee schedule.
- **Facts to verify.** a major exchange's market-data policies: display, non-display and derived-data definitions (dated); the same exchange's market-data fee schedule (dated); an exchange's audit policy: look-back period and back-billing (dated); FISD or industry guidance on usage reporting (dated).
- **Data.** firm.exchsim feed; synthetic subscribers and usage logs.

## EXCLUDED

- Interest on back-billed fees: the subscriber agreement says "together with interest" without a rate in the retrieved sections; the model prices no interest.
- The model back-bills every month at the 2026 fees; the 2025 fees (F1: $80.50 and $396.00) and earlier years' are lower, so the model's total is an upper bound for a real three-year look-back.
- FISD or industry guidance on usage reporting: not fetched; not cited.
- Other exchanges' policies (NYSE, Cboe): not fetched; the chapter uses one venue's documents as the example and says so.
- The hook's firm, its forty screens and eleven applications are the chapter's model, not a reported case.
