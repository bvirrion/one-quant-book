# 16. Protocols II: Binary Exchange Protocols — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A futures exchange sends every market-data packet twice, over two separate networks, as fixed-layout binary messages a handler can read without parsing; the handler's first job is to keep whichever copy arrives first and forget the other.
- **Sections.** Order-by-order feeds and their messages; Packets, sequence numbers and multicast; Incremental and snapshot channels, retransmission and recovery; Arbitration of redundant lines; Binary order entry and simple binary encoding.
- **Defines.** multicast, line arbitration, message gap, incremental channel, snapshot channel, simple binary encoding, flyweight codec.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** market data feed (B1.4), sequence number (B1.28), normalisation (B1.28), market-by-order (B1.19), market-by-price (B1.19), level 1 (B1.28), level 2 (B1.28), level 3 (B1.28), exchange simulator (B10.26), matching engine (B10.1), redundant feed lines (B10.26), retransmission request (B10.26), snapshot recovery (B10.26), order-entry protocol (B10.26), order-entry session (B10.26), mass quote (B11.19), FIX session (ch15), direct feed (B1.9), snapshot-and-delta feed (B3.26).
- **Tutorial.** Decode the exchange simulator's recorded A and B lines with C++ and Rust flyweights, zero-copy; generate a little-endian SBE-style codec for the same messages from an XML schema with a Python generator and compare it with Book 1's big-endian decoder; count gaps on each line and on the arbitrated stream. End state: gap counts per line and arbitrated, and decode throughput by codec, measured on this laptop.
- **Build.** `firm.wirecodec`: flyweight codecs for the simulator's feed (packet header, order-by-order messages, snapshot) and order-entry messages, generated from one schema into C++20 and Rust (generated files committed and checked for no diff), validated against Book 10's golden fixtures; Python generator.
- **Weekend problem.** Two copies of everything -- named result: the probability that a message is lost on both lines at a per-packet loss rate, independent and with correlated bursts, and the recovery time from the snapshot channel at its publication interval.
- **Data.** Book 10 simulator's recorded lines and golden fixtures; until it lands, Book 1's firm.feed sample packetised by the chapter's script.
- **Facts to verify.** Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH 5.0, MoldUDP64 and SoupBinTCP specifications; Nasdaq OUCH 5.0 specification; CME MDP 3.0: SBE encoding, A/B feeds, market recovery (client systems wiki, dated); FIX Simple Binary Encoding standard (version and date); Eurex EOBI/ETI documentation (dated).
