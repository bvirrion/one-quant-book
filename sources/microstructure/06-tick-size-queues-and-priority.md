# 6. Tick Size, Queues and Priority — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** From October 2016 the SEC's tick-size pilot quoted and traded about twelve hundred small stocks in five-cent increments instead of one. Spreads widened, displayed depth grew, and the value of standing first in a queue changed for every market maker in them.
- **Sections.** Large-tick and small-tick assets; The value of queue position; Queue dynamics and the time to fill; Tick-size experiments; Priority rules beyond time.
- **Defines.** relative tick size, large-tick asset, small-tick asset, queue value, uncertainty-zone model, difference-in-differences.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** tick size (ch1), limit order book (ch1), queue position (B7.18), passive fill probability (B7.18), price-time priority (B1.19), pro-rata allocation (B1.19), top-order allocation (B1.19), round lot (B1.9), odd lot (B1.9), queue imbalance (B7.8), microprice (B7.8), birth--death process (B4.8), hitting probability (B4.8), clustered standard errors (B4.16), adverse selection (B1.1).
- **Tutorial.** Run firm.exchsim with the tape's background flow at tick sizes of one, two, five and ten cents: spread in ticks, queue lengths, time to fill and the share of time the spread is one tick; value a queue slot by simulation and by the birth-death formulas of firm.queues; estimate a difference-in-differences effect of a tick change on simulated treated and control stocks. Data: simulated; the pilot's published results.
- **Build.** `firm.queuevalue`: fill probability and expected time to fill from a queue position (birth-death and empirical), value of a queue slot (spread capture less adverse selection conditional on a fill), front- against back-of-queue comparison, uncertainty-zone estimator of the implicit spread; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Five cents instead of one -- named result: the change in spread, depth and queue value when the tick is multiplied by five in the simulated market, against the pilot's measured effects.
- **Facts to verify.** SEC Tick Size Pilot plan (2014 order, 2016 start, 2018 end; test groups); SEC DERA / published assessments of the tick size pilot (e.g. Albuquerque, Song, Yao 2020 JFE; Chung, Lee, Rosch 2020 JFE); Dayri and Rosenbaum 2015 large tick assets: implicit spread and optimal tick size (Market Microstructure and Liquidity); Robert and Rosenbaum 2011 a new approach for the dynamics of ultra-high-frequency data: the model with uncertainty zones (J. Financial Econometrics); Moallemi and Yuan queue position valuation (published version if any); SEC 2024 Regulation NMS amendments: minimum pricing increments (half-cent tick) and their effective date (dated); EU tick-size regime (as in Book 1 ch. 11).
