# 23. Request-for-Quote and Dealer Markets in Theory — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A client who asks five dealers for a price on a corporate bond gets a better price than one who asks two, up to a point: every dealer asked learns that someone is selling, and the ones who lose the request can trade on it.
- **Sections.** Search and bargaining; Dealer networks; The request for quote as an auction; How many dealers to ask; Relationships and information chasing.
- **Defines.** over-the-counter search model, search friction, bargaining power, core--periphery network, information chasing.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** request for quote (B2.22), winner's curse (B2.30), dealer-to-client platform (B2.4), inter-dealer broker (B2.4), all-to-all trading (B2.22), common-value auction (B4.29), first-price auction (B4.29), Bayes--Nash equilibrium (B4.29), bid shading (B4.29), information leakage (ch9), Poisson process (B4.6), continuous-time Markov chain (B4.8).
- **Tutorial.** Solve the Duffie-Garleanu-Pedersen search model's steady state and prices; simulate request-for-quote competition with leakage (extending Book 2's firm.rfq) and find the client's cost-minimising number of dealers; let dealers chase informed flow and watch spreads by client type. Data: simulated.
- **Build.** `firm.otcsearch`: search-and-bargaining steady state and prices, RFQ auctions with private and common values, leakage and winner's curse, the optimal number of dealers, a core-periphery network simulator; Python (extends Book 2's firm.rfq by composition).
- **Weekend problem.** How many dealers to ask -- named result: the cost-minimising number of dealers and its dependence on the leakage cost per losing dealer.
- **Facts to verify.** Duffie, Garleanu, Pedersen 2005 over-the-counter markets (Econometrica); Hendershott and Madhavan 2015 (JF); Riggs, Onur, Reiffen, Zhu 2020 swap trading after Dodd-Frank: evidence from index CDS (JFE); Li and Schurhoff 2019 dealer networks (JF); Di Maggio, Kermani, Song 2017 the value of trading relations in turbulent times (JFE); Hendershott, Li, Livdan, Schurhoff 2020 relationship trading in over-the-counter markets (JF).
