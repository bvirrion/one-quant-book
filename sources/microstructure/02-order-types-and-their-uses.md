# 2. Order Types and Their Uses — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** US exchanges publish dozens of order types; in 2015 one exchange group paid to settle regulators' charges that some of its order types behaved in ways it had not fully disclosed to all members. An order type is a contract about what the matching engine will do with your order, and its fine print decides who trades first.
- **Sections.** Price conditions: limit, market, marketable limit, stop; Time conditions: day, good-till-cancelled, immediate-or-cancel, fill-or-kill; Display conditions: hidden, iceberg, pegged; Who uses which, and why; A limit order is a free option.
- **Defines.** marketable limit order, stop order, stop-limit order, time in force, immediate-or-cancel order, fill-or-kill order, good-till-cancelled order, hidden order, iceberg order, pegged order, midpoint peg, free option of a limit order.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** limit order (ch1), market order (ch1), limit order book (ch1), matching engine (ch1), post-only order (B3.15), intermarket sweep order (B1.9), market-on-close order (B1.13), limit-on-close order (B1.13), national best bid and offer (B1.9), trade-through (B1.9), stub quote (B1.31), flash crash (B1.31), dark pool (B1.9), price improvement (B1.10), adverse selection (B1.1), maker-taker pricing (B1.9).
- **Tutorial.** Implement the order types on firm.lob (stops triggered by trades, iceberg refresh with a new time priority, pegs repriced on each change of the best quotes, IOC and FOK, post-only) and replay a scripted morning through them; then detect icebergs in the public feed from same-price refills, and value a resting limit order as a short option against the next price move. Data: firm.tape and scripted scenarios (simulated).
- **Build.** `firm.ordertypes`: the order-type layer on top of firm.lob (time in force, stop triggers, iceberg display and refresh, primary and midpoint pegs, post-only, IOC / FOK, minimum quantity, self-trade prevention hooks) with a conformance table of scenarios that chapter 26's C++20 and Rust engines must reproduce; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The iceberg in the tape -- named result: the detection rate and false-alarm rate of a refill-based iceberg detector against the display size and the randomisation of the refill.
- **Facts to verify.** SEC 2015 settlement with Direct Edge exchanges over undisclosed order-type behaviour (order and penalty); SEC investor bulletin on stop and stop-limit orders; Nasdaq and Cboe BZX order-type documentation: midpoint peg, reserve (iceberg), post-only (dated); Copeland and Galai 1983 information effects on the bid-ask spread (JF); Hautsch and Huang 2012 on the dark side of the market: hidden liquidity (JFM / or equivalent); CFTC-SEC 2010 report on 6 May 2010 (stop orders converted to market orders).
