# 12. Transient Impact and Propagator Models — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** The signs of trades are predictable for hours: a buy is followed by buys long after any one trader's order has finished. Yet prices do not trend. Something must undo the impact of each trade, and how fast it does decides what an execution costs.
- **Sections.** The long memory of order flow; The propagator model; Transient impact in continuous time; No-manipulation conditions; Estimating the kernel.
- **Defines.** propagator model, decay kernel, transient impact model, Obizhaeva--Wang model, no-dynamic-arbitrage condition, transaction-triggered price manipulation.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** long memory (B4.17), Hurst exponent (B4.17), autocorrelation function (B4.17), order-sign autocorrelation (B7.9), trade sign (B7.9), metaorder (B7.9), market impact (B7.18), temporary impact (B7.27), permanent impact (B7.27), impact curve (ch11), impact reversion (ch11), book resilience (ch3), Hawkes process (B4.7).
- **Tutorial.** Fit a propagator kernel to firm.exchsim trades by inverting the sign-correlation Toeplitz system (Bouchaud et al. 2004); check that the fitted kernel makes prices diffusive; simulate round trips under exponential and power-law kernels and exhibit a profitable round trip when the kernel's decay violates the no-arbitrage conditions. Data: simulated.
- **Build.** `firm.propagator`: response functions, kernel estimation (correlation inversion by Levinson recursion), transient-impact simulation for exponential and power-law kernels, round-trip cost and the positive-definiteness check, the Obizhaeva-Wang limit-order-book model; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The kernel that paid you to trade -- named result: the round-trip profit under a kernel that decays too fast after large trades, and the decay rate at which it vanishes.
- **Facts to verify.** Bouchaud, Gefen, Potters, Wyart 2004 fluctuations and response in financial markets (QF); Lillo and Farmer 2004 the long memory of the efficient market (SNDE); Obizhaeva and Wang 2013 optimal trading strategy and supply/demand dynamics (JFM); Gatheral 2010 no-dynamic-arbitrage and market impact (QF); Alfonsi, Schied, Slynko 2012 order book resilience, price manipulation and the positive portfolio problem (SIFIN); Huberman and Stanzl 2004 price manipulation and quasi-arbitrage (Econometrica); Bouchaud, Bonart, Donier, Gould 2018 Trades, Quotes and Prices (CUP).
