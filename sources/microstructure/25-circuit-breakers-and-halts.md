# 25. Circuit Breakers and Halts — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 9 March 2020 the S&P 500 fell seven per cent minutes after the open and all US equity trading stopped for fifteen minutes; it happened three more times that month. Whether stopping a falling market calms it or makes it fall faster toward the stop is an old argument with new data.
- **Sections.** Designs: bands, pauses, halts, limits; Single-stock and market-wide mechanisms; Futures: velocity logic and stop logic; Magnet effects in the evidence; Reopening.
- **Defines.** static price band, dynamic price band, trading pause, reopening auction, velocity logic.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** limit up--limit down (B1.31), market-wide circuit breaker (B1.31), volatility interruption (B1.13), price limit (B1.12), trading halt (B8.17), magnet effect (B8.18), limit-locked market (B3.9), clearly erroneous trade (B1.31), call auction (B1.13), flash crash (B1.31), auction collar (ch10), stop order (ch2).
- **Tutorial.** Add bands, pauses and a market-wide halt to firm.exchsim through firm.halts; run a crash scenario with agents who accelerate their selling near a band, and measure the magnet effect: the probability of reaching the limit against the distance to it, with and without the mechanism; replay the pause with Book 1's firm.replay monitors. Data: simulated; published trigger events.
- **Build.** `firm.halts`: static and dynamic bands, LULD-style limit states and pauses, market-wide levels, velocity logic, and the reopening through firm.auctionsim, as the module firm.exchsim loads; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Fifteen minutes of silence -- named result: the probability of reaching the band conditional on the distance to it, with and without the band, and the size of the simulated magnet effect.
- **Facts to verify.** NYSE / SEC market-wide circuit breaker triggers of March 2020 (dates, times, levels); LULD plan and its amendments (dated); CME Globex velocity logic and stop spike logic documentation (dated); Subrahmanyam 1994 circuit breakers and market volatility: a theoretical perspective (JF); Cho, Russell, Tiao, Tsay 2003 the magnet effect of price limits (J. Empirical Finance); Brugler, Linton, Noss, Pedace 2018 or a published study of single-stock halts; Eurex / Xetra volatility interruption and static/dynamic bands (dated).
