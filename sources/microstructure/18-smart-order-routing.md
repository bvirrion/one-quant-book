# 18. Smart Order Routing — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A router splits an order across five venues. Sent at once, the pieces arrive microseconds apart, and quotes on the later venues vanish in between: the first fill told fast traders what was coming.
- **Sections.** What a router decides; Venue ranking and fill probabilities; Aggressive routing and quote fade; Passive allocation across venues; Fee-aware routing and anti-gaming.
- **Defines.** smart order router, venue ranking, spray order, quote fade, synchronised routing, anti-gaming logic.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** national best bid and offer (B1.9), intermarket sweep order (B1.9), order protection rule (B1.9), fee-adjusted price (ch7), market fragmentation (ch8), protected quote (ch8), dark pool (B1.9), pinging order (ch9), information leakage (ch9), passive fill probability (B7.18), market-data latency (B7.18), order-entry latency (B7.18), latency arbitrage (B11.9, forward), convex optimisation problem (B4.23).
- **Tutorial.** On the multi-venue firm.exchsim with fast liquidity providers who cancel on seeing a fill elsewhere, route an aggressive order by spray and by synchronised routing (sends timed to arrive together) and measure the fill ratio; allocate a passive order across venues by the Cont-Kukanov convex programme; add fees and a dark venue. Data: simulated.
- **Build.** `firm.sor`: a router with a venue model (fees, latency, fill probability, fade), aggressive sweep with arrival-time alignment, passive allocation (Cont-Kukanov), dark-first logic and anti-gaming rules; Python on the multi-venue simulator.
- **Weekend problem.** The order that arrived after the quotes had gone -- named result: the fill ratio of a sweep with and without arrival-time alignment, and its fee-adjusted cost.
- **Facts to verify.** Cont and Kukanov 2017 (QF); Foucault and Menkveld 2008 competition for order flow and smart order routing systems (JF); Maglaras, Moallemi, Zheng 2021 optimal execution in a limit order book and an associated microstructure market impact model / or queueing model of routing (if cited); a public description of arrival-synchronised routing (patent or court record); Battalio, Corwin, Jennings 2016 (JF).
