# 9. Dark Pools and Blocks — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A pension fund wants to buy two million shares of a stock that trades five million a day. Shown on a lit book, the order would move the price before a tenth of it traded; sent to a dark pool, it waits for a seller, and every venue it touches learns a little of what it wants.
- **Sections.** Midpoint crossing and minimum quantities; Conditional orders and block venues; Who trades in the dark, and the evidence; Information leakage; Dark pools in enforcement records.
- **Defines.** minimum execution quantity, conditional order, firm-up rate, information leakage, pinging order, indication of interest, crossing network.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** dark pool (B1.9), large-in-scale waiver (B1.11), reference price waiver (B1.11), double volume cap (B1.11), periodic auction (B1.11), block trade (B1.2), alternative trading system (B1.4), systematic internaliser (B1.11), internalisation (B1.10), midpoint peg (ch2), hidden order (ch2), immediate-or-cancel order (ch2), adverse selection (B1.1), mark-out (B2.15), mark-out curve (B7.23), informed trader (ch4).
- **Tutorial.** Add a midpoint dark venue to the multi-venue firm.exchsim; let informed and uninformed flow choose between lit and dark (Zhu 2014); measure dark fill rates, the mark-outs of dark against lit fills, and how much a parent order's footprint (pings, conditionals that do not firm up) moves the price before it completes. Data: simulated; SEC and FINRA published statistics.
- **Build.** `firm.darkpool`: a midpoint crossing venue with minimum quantities and conditional orders (invitation, firm-up timer, firm-up rate per participant), leakage metrics (pre-completion drift, counterparty mark-outs), plugged into firm.exchsim as a venue type; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The block that leaked -- named result: the price drift before completion of a parent order worked in the dark against in the lit market, and the leakage cost in basis points.
- **Facts to verify.** Zhu 2014 do dark pools harm price discovery? (RFS); Comerton-Forde and Putnins 2015 dark trading and price discovery (JFE); SEC orders: ITG POSIT 2015; Barclays LX 2016; Credit Suisse Crossfinder 2016 (amounts and findings); FINRA ATS transparency data: dark share of US volume (dated); ESMA double volume cap and its 2024 MiFIR review changes (dated); Kavajecz and Keim / block-venue evidence if cited.
