# 24. Risk Models — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A market-neutral book is forecast at four per cent annual volatility and realises nine. Its risk model had no factor for what the book was really exposed to.
- **Sections.** Three families of factor models; Estimating factor returns; Factor covariance; Specific risk; Evaluating a risk model; Build: an equity risk model.
- **Defines.** fundamental factor model, statistical factor model, hybrid factor model, factor exposure, factor return, specific return, specific risk, style factor, industry factor, bias statistic, volatility regime adjustment.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** factor model (B4.22), principal component analysis (B4.22), covariance matrix (B4.22), linear shrinkage (B4.22), eigenvalue clipping (B4.22), EWMA volatility (B4.18), weighted least squares (B4.16), HAC estimator (B4.11), residual return (ch6), market beta (ch22), synthmkt (ch5).
- **Tutorial.** Build a fundamental model (style and industry factors by weighted cross-sectional regression) and a statistical model on firm.synthmkt; forecast portfolio risk out of sample and compare bias statistics, including a book exposed to a factor the model omits.
- **Build.** `firm.riskmodel`: equity risk model (exposures, constrained WLS factor returns, EWMA factor covariance with Newey--West and volatility-regime adjustment, shrunk specific variances, portfolio risk and its decomposition, bias-statistic evaluation); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Predicted four, realised nine — named result: the bias statistic of a model missing a planted factor, and the realised-to-predicted volatility ratio of the exposed book.
- **Facts to verify.** Rosenberg 1974 (JFQA); Connor 1995, the three types of factor models (FAJ); Fama and French 1993 (JFE); Menchero, Orr, Wang 2011, the Barra US equity model USE4 methodology notes (public PDF); Menchero, Wang, Orr 2012 eigenfactor risk adjustment; Chen, Roll, Ross 1986 macro factors (J. Business).
