# 3. Point-in-Time Data and the Biases — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** The first estimate of US nonfarm payrolls for a month and the figure in today's database for the same month differ by more than a hundred thousand jobs; a macro signal backtested on today's figures trades on numbers nobody had.
- **Sections.** Look-ahead bias and timestamp semantics; Survivorship bias; Restatements, revisions and vintages; As-of joins and bitemporal storage; A catalogue of the other biases.
- **Defines.** look-ahead bias, survivorship bias, point-in-time data, bitemporal data, valid time, knowledge time, as-of join, restatement, data vintage, backfill bias.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** exchange timestamp (B1.28), receive timestamp (B1.28), consensus forecast (B2.31), data snooping (B4.12), research log (ch1).
- **Tutorial.** Download the real-time vintages of US payrolls (ALFRED, public), build a bitemporal table, run an as-of join, and compare a payroll-surprise signal computed with first releases against one computed with the latest vintage.
- **Build.** `firm.pit`: bitemporal store (valid time, knowledge time) with as-of queries, as-of joins with tolerance, and a leakage guard that fails when a feature reads knowledge after its decision time; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The survivors' premium — named result: the annual return inflation of an equal-weighted universe that drops delisted names, in a synthetic market with a known delisting rate and delisting return, against the closed-form approximation.
- **Facts to verify.** ALFRED real-time vintages of PAYEMS (St. Louis Fed); Croushore and Stark 2001, a real-time data set for macroeconomists (J. Econometrics); Shumway 1997, the delisting bias in CRSP data (JF); Brown, Goetzmann, Ibbotson, Ross 1992, survivorship bias in performance studies (RFS); Elton, Gruber, Blake 1996 survivorship bias and mutual fund performance (RFS); Banz and Breen 1986, sample-dependent results using accounting and market data (JF); BLS revision practice for payrolls (benchmark revisions).
