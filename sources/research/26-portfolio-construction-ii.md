# 26. Portfolio Construction II — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Move one stock's alpha by a single basis point and the optimal portfolio turns over a third of its gross.
- **Sections.** Robust optimisation; Views blended with equilibrium; Risk parity and risk budgeting; Hierarchical allocation; Multi-period optimisation with costs.
- **Defines.** robust portfolio optimisation, uncertainty set, implied equilibrium returns, Black--Litterman model, risk contribution, risk budgeting, equal risk contribution portfolio, risk parity, hierarchical risk parity, aim portfolio, multi-period optimisation.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** second-order cone programme (B4.23), Euler allocation (B6.20), stochastic control problem (B4.9), no-trade region (B4.10), posterior distribution (B4.14), mean--variance optimisation (ch25), efficient frontier (ch25), turnover penalty (ch25).
- **Tutorial.** Build the same book by mean--variance, robust, Black--Litterman, equal-risk-contribution and hierarchical rules; measure weight stability under a one-basis-point alpha perturbation; run the aim-portfolio trading rule with quadratic costs and compare net Sharpe ratios.
- **Build.** `firm.allocation`: robust mean--variance by SOCP, Black--Litterman posterior, equal risk contribution by Newton, hierarchical risk parity, aim-portfolio multi-period rule with quadratic costs; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The one-basis-point flip — named result: turnover per basis point of alpha perturbation for each method, and the net Sharpe ratio of the aim portfolio against daily re-optimisation.
- **Facts to verify.** Black and Litterman 1992 (FAJ); He and Litterman 1999 (Goldman Sachs working paper); Goldfarb and Iyengar 2003 (Math. of Operations Research); Ceria and Stubbs 2006 (J. Asset Management); Maillard, Roncalli, Teiletche 2010 ERC (JPM); Lopez de Prado 2016 HRP (JPM); Garleanu and Pedersen 2013 (JF); Boyd et al. 2017, multi-period trading via convex optimization (Foundations and Trends in Optimization); Asness, Frazzini, Pedersen 2012 leverage aversion and risk parity (FAJ).
