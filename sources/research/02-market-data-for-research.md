# 2. Market Data for Research — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Two researchers measure the same stock's five-minute volatility on the same day and disagree by thirty per cent: one used trade prices, the other midquotes.
- **Sections.** What a message feed contains; Sampling clocks: time, tick, volume and value bars; What each representation destroys; Continuous futures series; Volumes, storage and formats.
- **Defines.** bar, time bar, tick bar, volume bar, dollar bar, imbalance bar, volume-weighted average price, midquote series, continuous futures series, back-adjustment, ratio adjustment.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** level 1 (B1.28), level 2 (B1.28), level 3 (B1.28), market-by-order (B1.19), market-by-price (B1.19), trade condition (B1.28), exchange timestamp (B1.28), receive timestamp (B1.28), sequence number (B1.28), roll (B1.21), front month (B1.18), bid--ask bounce (B4.21), microstructure noise (B4.21), realised variance (B4.18), Hawkes process (B4.7).
- **Tutorial.** Generate a day of message-level data with firm.tape (a latent efficient price, a queue-reactive book, Hawkes-clustered trades and a news burst); build time, tick, volume and dollar bars; compare the kurtosis and autocorrelation of bar returns and the realised variance from trades and from mids.
- **Build.** `firm.tape`: the book's synthetic message-level market for one or two instruments (ITCH-like add/cancel/execute events, known trade signs, informed and noise flow, deterministic seeds) and `firm.bars` (time, tick, volume, dollar and imbalance bars, VWAP, midquote sampling, continuous futures with back- and ratio-adjustment); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Which clock? — named result: the kurtosis of five-minute time-bar returns against dollar-bar returns on a simulated day with a news burst, and the trade-versus-mid volatility gap.
- **Facts to verify.** Ane and Geman 2000, order flow, transaction clock and normality (JF); Clark 1973 subordinated process (Econometrica); Easley, Lopez de Prado, O'Hara 2012, The volume clock (JPM); Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH message counts per day (dated); roll methods for continuous futures (data-vendor documentation).
