# 5. Stylised Facts of Returns — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 19 October 1987 the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 22.6 per cent in one session: under a normal law fitted to the preceding years, a move of more than twenty standard deviations.
- **Sections.** Heavy tails and aggregational Gaussianity; Volatility clustering and long memory in size; The leverage effect and gain--loss asymmetry; Intraday seasonality; Cross-sectional structure.
- **Defines.** stylised fact, aggregational Gaussianity, gain--loss asymmetry, Taylor effect, intraday seasonality, intraday volume profile, exceedance correlation, correlation asymmetry.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** heavy-tailed distribution (B4.15), tail index (B4.15), Hill estimator (B4.15), volatility clustering (B4.18), leverage effect (B4.18), GARCH model (B4.18), long memory (B4.17), autocorrelation function (B4.17), principal component analysis (B4.22), Marchenko--Pastur law (B4.22), realised volatility (B1.25), market-on-close order (B1.13).
- **Tutorial.** Compute the stylised facts from summary statistics of a century of daily US market factor returns (derived from the Fama--French data library) and from firm.tape and firm.synthmkt, and score the simulators against the data.
- **Build.** `firm.synthmkt`: the book's synthetic daily equity universe (factor structure with GARCH-t factors and leverage, heterogeneous specific volatility, earnings jumps, listings and delistings, corporate actions, point-in-time fundamentals with restatements, planted predictors with known information coefficients); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Is the simulator realistic? — named result: the stylised-fact scorecard of firm.synthmkt against the real daily data (tail index, autocorrelation of absolute returns at lags 1 and 20, leverage correlation, first eigenvalue share).
- **Facts to verify.** Cont 2001, Empirical properties of asset returns: stylized facts (Quantitative Finance); Dow Jones fall of 22.6% on 19 October 1987 (Federal Reserve History); Mandelbrot 1963 (J. Business); Black 1976 leverage effect; Ang and Chen 2002 asymmetric correlations (JFE); Longin and Solnik 2001 (JF); Wood, McInish, Ord 1985 intraday U-shape (JF); Andersen and Bollerslev 1997 intraday periodicity (JEF); Taylor 1986 (book) absolute-return autocorrelation; Kenneth French data library, daily factors (copyright; derived statistics only).
