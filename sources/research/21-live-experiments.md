# 21. Live Experiments — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A new execution algorithm handles ten per cent of the firm's orders for two weeks and saves 0.4 basis points on average, with a standard error of 0.5.
- **Sections.** Paper trading and shadow mode; Canaries and staged rollouts; Randomised A/B tests on live flow; Variance reduction; Sizing and stopping an experiment.
- **Defines.** paper trading, canary deployment, staged rollout, A/B test, randomisation unit, interference, CUPED, minimum detectable effect, sequential test.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** power of a test (B4.12), size of a test (B4.12), p-value (B4.12), stratified sampling (B4.26), shadow trading (ch19), implementation shortfall (ch19), arrival price (ch19).
- **Tutorial.** Simulate an A/B test of two execution algorithms on order-level costs, with randomisation by order and by day; compute power curves, the gain from CUPED and stratification, and an always-valid sequential stop.
- **Build.** `firm.abtest`: hash-based assignment, difference-in-means with CUPED and stratification, power and minimum detectable effect, mixture sequential probability ratio test; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Is the new algorithm better? — named result: the trading days needed to detect a 0.3 basis point improvement at 80% power with and without CUPED.
- **Facts to verify.** Deng, Xu, Kohavi, Walker 2013 CUPED (WSDM); Kohavi, Tang, Xu 2020, Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments; Johari, Koomen, Pekelis, Walsh 2017/2022 always-valid inference (KDD / Operations Research); Wald 1945 SPRT (Annals of Math. Stat.); SEC Knight Capital order, October 2013 (deployment failure, 1 August 2012).
