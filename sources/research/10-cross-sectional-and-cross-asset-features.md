# 10. Cross-Sectional and Cross-Asset Features — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** When a customer firm reports bad news, its main supplier's stock falls — but on average a month later, not the same day.
- **Sections.** Peer and sector residuals; Lead--lag at daily horizons; Futures-to-cash and cross-venue; Cross-asset signals; Predictor cards.
- **Defines.** peer group, peer-relative return, lead--lag relationship, lead--lag estimator, economic link, futures-to-cash lead, cross-asset feature.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** Hayashi--Yoshida estimator (B4.21), Epps effect (B4.21), non-synchronous trading (B4.21), Granger causality (B4.20), vector autoregression (B4.20), principal component analysis (B4.22), fair value (B1.21), exchange-traded fund (B1.14), residual return (ch6), neutralisation (ch6), tape (ch2), synthmkt (ch5).
- **Tutorial.** Estimate the lead of a future over an ETF on two firm.tape instruments with a planted latency by the Hayashi--Yoshida cross-correlation at lags; then recover the planted economic links of firm.synthmkt at daily horizons.
- **Build.** `firm.leadlag`: lead--lag estimator on asynchronous data (Hayashi--Yoshida at lags, argmax and confidence band), peer-residual engine and a lagged-peer feature builder; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Who moves first? — named result: the estimated lead against the planted latency, and the share of cross-venue predictability left at each sampling interval.
- **Facts to verify.** Cohen and Frazzini 2008, Economic links and predictable returns (JF); Menzly and Ozbas 2010 (JF); Hou 2007 industry information diffusion and lead-lag (RFS); Lo and MacKinlay 1990 (RFS); Kawaller, Koch, Koch 1987 futures lead cash (JF); Hoffmann, Rosenbaum, Yoshida 2013 lead-lag estimation (Bernoulli); Huth and Abergel 2014 high-frequency lead-lag (J. Empirical Finance).
