# 12. Alternative and Text Data — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A vendor offers five years of card-transaction data covering four hundred retailers at an annual fee of several hundred thousand dollars; the trial file arrives with a note that the panel changed provider in its third year.
- **Sections.** What the datasets are; Text as data; Evaluating a vendor; Law and ethics; The buy decision.
- **Defines.** alternative data, panel drift, data trial, incremental information coefficient, dictionary method, sentiment score, document embedding, material non-public information.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** point-in-time data (ch3), backfill bias (ch3), information coefficient (ch6), neutralisation (ch6), predictor card (ch6), earnings surprise (ch11).
- **Tutorial.** Run a vendor trial on a synthetic panel with drift and backfill: coverage through time, point-in-time check, incremental IC after orthogonalising on existing signals, and the break-even price; then score a small text corpus with a word list and compare it with a planted tone.
- **Build.** `firm.vendoreval`: vendor-trial harness (coverage, history, point-in-time and backfill checks, panel-drift detection, incremental IC, value of information net of fee and capacity); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Should we buy the card panel? — named result: the break-even annual price of a dataset given its incremental IC, breadth, capacity and decay.
- **Facts to verify.** Tetlock 2007, media pessimism (JF); Loughran and McDonald 2011 (JF); SEC v. App Annie, September 2021 settlement (alternative-data MNPI case); hiQ Labs v. LinkedIn, Ninth Circuit 2022 (scraping); Froot, Kang, Ozik, Sadka 2017 real-time sales data (JFE); Zhu 2019, Big data as a governance mechanism (RFS); EU GDPR personal data definition (Regulation 2016/679).
