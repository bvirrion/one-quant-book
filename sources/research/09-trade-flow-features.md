# 9. Trade-Flow Features — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In May 2010 a measure of order-flow toxicity was reported to have reached a historical high an hour before the flash crash; four years later another study found it did not forecast volatility once volume was controlled for.
- **Sections.** Signing trades; Order-sign autocorrelation; Flow toxicity; Fitted excitation intensities; Large-trade and metaorder detection; Predictor cards.
- **Defines.** trade sign, tick rule, quote rule, Lee--Ready algorithm, bulk volume classification, order-sign autocorrelation, metaorder, flow toxicity, VPIN, Kyle's lambda.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** adverse selection (B1.1), mark-out (B2.15), flash crash (B1.31), Hawkes process (B4.7), branching ratio (B4.7), long memory (B4.17), Hurst exponent (B4.17), order-flow imbalance (ch8), tape (ch2), volume bar (ch2).
- **Tutorial.** Sign the trades of firm.tape by the tick, quote, Lee--Ready and bulk-volume rules against the simulator's true signs; measure the sign autocorrelation; compute VPIN and a mark-out toxicity measure through a planted toxic episode; fit a two-dimensional Hawkes process to buys and sells.
- **Build.** `firm.tradeflow`: trade classifiers, bulk volume classification, VPIN on volume bars, Kyle's lambda by rolling regression, Hawkes-intensity features (imports firm.hawkes); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Did VPIN see it coming? — named result: VPIN's lead time and false-alarm rate against the mark-out toxicity measure on a synthetic tape with a toxic episode.
- **Facts to verify.** Lee and Ready 1991 (JF); Ellis, Michaely, O'Hara 2000 classification accuracy (JFQA); Easley, Lopez de Prado, O'Hara 2011, The microstructure of the flash crash (JPM); Easley, Lopez de Prado, O'Hara 2012 VPIN (RFS); Andersen and Bondarenko 2014 VPIN critique (J. Financial Markets); Lillo and Farmer 2004 long memory of order signs (SNDE); Kyle 1985 (Econometrica); Chakrabarty, Pascual, Shkilko 2015 BVC accuracy (J. Financial Markets).
