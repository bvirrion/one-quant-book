# 7. Offers, Compensation and Non-Competes — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Two offers arrive in the same week. One pays a higher base and a guaranteed first-year bonus, with a sign-on payment repayable if the candidate leaves within two years and a twelve-month non-compete. The other pays less cash now, a larger share deferred over three years, and no non-compete at all. Which is worth more depends on numbers the letters do not print: how likely the candidate is to leave, when, and what the non-compete costs in the year it bites.
- **Sections.** Reading an offer letter: every line and what it commits each side to; Valuing an offer: deferral, vesting, repayment and the probability of leaving; Negotiating: alternatives, deadlines and what is negotiable; Restrictive covenants: non-competes, garden leave and notice, and where the law stands.
- **Defines.** offer letter, exploding offer, counteroffer.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** bonus pool (B16.10), formulaic payout (B16.10), discretionary bonus (B16.10), deferred compensation (B16.10), vesting schedule (B16.10), malus (B16.10), clawback (B16.10), deferral buyout (B16.10), material risk taker (B16.10), non-compete clause (B16.11), garden leave (B16.11), non-solicitation clause (B16.11), confidentiality agreement (B16.11), invention assignment clause (B16.11), trade secret (B16.11), best alternative to a negotiated agreement (B16.23), reservation value (B16.23), zone of possible agreement (B16.23), base salary (B17.13), total compensation (B17.13), sign-on bonus (B17.13), guaranteed bonus (B17.13), forfeiture (B17.13), good-leaver provision (B17.13), restricted stock unit (B17.13), notice period (B17.28), lateral hire (B17.28), return offer (B17.28), pay band (B17.9), recruiting cycle (ch2), final round (ch6).
- **Question bank.** 10 questions, 3/4/3. Families: valuing an offer with deferral, vesting and a repayment clause under a leaving probability (numeric, exact); comparing two offers with a non-compete priced as lost pay (numeric); answering 'what are your other offers?' and 'what number do you want?'; an exploding offer and what can be asked; a counteroffer from the current employer; reading a covenant clause and what it restricts. No legal advice: the questions test reading and arithmetic. Roles: trader 3, researcher 3, developer 2, bank 1, risk 1. Firms: market maker 2, proprietary firm 2, multi-manager fund 2, bank 2, any 2.
- **Facts to verify.** US FTC Non-Compete Clause Rule (16 CFR part 910, 2024), its setting aside in Ryan LLC v. FTC (N.D. Tex., 2024) and the FTC's later action on the appeals (dated); California Business and Professions Code sections 16600 and 16600.5 and the 2024 notice requirement (dated); UK government policy on limiting non-compete clauses (2023 announcement and any later consultation or working paper) (dated); pay-range transparency: NYC Local Law 32 of 2022, New York State Labor Law 194-b, California SB 1162, EU Directive 2023/970 (transposition deadline) (dated); salary-history bans: California Labor Code 432.3 and NYC's 2017 law (dated); UK removal of the bonus cap (PRA PS9/23, 2023) and any later PRA/FCA remuneration changes to deferral (dated; pointer to Book 16 ch. 10 ledger); a university career service's published employer offer-deadline policy, as an example of offer-timing norms (dated).
- **Data.** Figures: one chart (the value of two offers against the probability of leaving within two years, from fig_iv_offer.py).
