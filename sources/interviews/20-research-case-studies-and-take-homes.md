# 20. Research Case Studies and Take-Homes — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** The e-mail arrives at nine: a 3-million-row file of trades and quotes for twenty stocks, one sentence ('Is there anything predictable here?') and a four-hour deadline. The submission that wins is not the one with the most models; it is the one whose first page says what was checked, what was found, how sure the author is, and what she would do with another week.
- **Sections.** What a reviewer reads first: the memo; The four hours: data checks, one clean question, one honest test; Two worked case studies with a reviewer's comments; Presenting and defending the result.
- **Defines.** take-home assignment, research case study.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** point-in-time data (B7.3), look-ahead bias (B7.3), survivorship bias (B7.3), as-of join (B7.3), vectorised backtest (B7.16), execution lag (B7.16), walk-forward analysis (B7.20), multiple testing (B4.12), deflated Sharpe ratio (B4.12), standard error (B4.11), microprice (B7.8), order imbalance (B1.13), machine-learning design question (ch19).
- **Question bank.** 12 questions, 4/5/3. Families: the data check list for a supplied file (planted defects: duplicated timestamps, crossed quotes, a split not adjusted; tested on the generated file); what one clean question to ask of a vague prompt; the honest test of a predictability claim (numeric on the supplied data); defending a result against 'is it just the bid-ask bounce?' (numeric, planted); the reviewer's reading of two memos; how to present a null result. Two worked case studies in the lesson (an imbalance predictor on generated quotes; a calendar effect in a generated daily panel) with the reviewer's comments. Roles: researcher 8, mle 2, trader 1, developer 1. Firms: systematic fund 5, market maker 3, multi-manager fund 2, any 2.
- **Facts to verify.** none external: the datasets are generated by the chapter's script with planted defects and a planted small effect; Roll 1984 for the bid-ask bounce (pointer to Book 4 ch. 21).
- **Data.** Figures: one chart (the planted imbalance effect with its confidence band, and the same measured with the bounce left in; fig_iv_case.py). Code: iv_case.py (generator of the take-home datasets, small by default, with defects and effects planted; the reference analysis; tests that the defects are found, the effect's estimate covers the plant over seeds, and the bounce artefact appears when uncorrected).
