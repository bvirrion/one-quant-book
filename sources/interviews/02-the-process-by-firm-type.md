# 2. The Process by Firm Type — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A candidate holds an internship offer from a bank that expires in ten days, is between the second and third rounds at a market maker whose process has taken five weeks so far, and has not heard from a systematic fund that interviewed her a month ago. Three firm types, three clocks, and a decision due before two of them stop.
- **Sections.** The stages: application, screen, technical rounds, final round, offer; Market makers and proprietary firms; Multi-manager funds and systematic funds: researcher hiring and portfolio-manager hiring; Banks and asset managers: the graduate cycle and lateral hiring; Timelines and how to run several processes at once.
- **Defines.** recruiting cycle, hiring committee.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** market maker (B1.1), proprietary trading firm (B1.1), hedge fund (B1.1), multi-manager platform (B1.1), asset manager (B1.1), sell side (B1.2), buy side (B1.3), risk trader (B17.16), execution trader (B17.16), algorithm supervisor (B17.16), quantitative researcher (B17.17), desk strategist (B17.18), model validator (B17.18), risk quant (B17.18), pod (B8.28), track record portability (B16.25), internship (B17.28), return offer (B17.28), graduate programme (B17.28), lateral hire (B17.28), interview loop (ch1), technical interview (ch1), interview rubric (ch1).
- **Question bank.** 8 questions, 3/3/2. Families: scheduling several processes against deadlines (an optimal-stopping flavour: accept, hold or decline, numeric); what a stage is for and what fails it; the portfolio-manager hire's due diligence (what a platform asks for, what it verifies); the conversion arithmetic of an internship class (numeric); what to ask the recruiter at each stage. Roles: trader 3, researcher 3, bank 2, developer 1, risk 1. Firms: market maker 2, proprietary firm 1, multi-manager fund 2, systematic fund 1, bank 2, asset manager 1, any 1.
- **Facts to verify.** published process descriptions (stages, number of rounds, typical duration) of one market maker, one proprietary firm, one systematic fund, one multi-manager fund, one bank and one asset manager, from their own careers pages (dated); bank graduate-programme application windows as published for the current cycle (dated); press reporting on recruiting cycles moving earlier (FT, WSJ, Bloomberg) as context only (dated); the multi-manager portfolio-manager hiring process as described in public sources (press, Book 16 ch. 3 and 25 ledgers).
- **Data.** Figures: one schematic (the stages by firm type as a timeline, TikZ). Code: iv_process.py, the multi-process deadline model (secretary-problem-style threshold rule against a known offer distribution) and conversion arithmetic; Fraction tests plus a seeded simulation of the threshold rule.
