# 3. Applications — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Four hundred CVs arrive for twenty first-round slots, and the reader has one afternoon: thirty-six seconds a CV. Whatever is not on the first half of the first page is, for practical purposes, not there, and a line that says what was built and what number it moved is read where a line that lists technologies is not.
- **Sections.** The one-page CV: what a reader looks for in the first half-page; Internships, projects and competitions as evidence; Referrals and recruiters; Automated screening and what it changes.
- **Defines.** CV screen, employee referral, agency recruiter.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** risk trader (B17.16), execution trader (B17.16), algorithm supervisor (B17.16), quantitative researcher (B17.17), desk strategist (B17.18), model validator (B17.18), risk quant (B17.18), quant developer (B16.21), research engineer (B17.19), low-latency engineer (B17.20), machine-learning engineer (B12.28), internship (B17.28), return offer (B17.28), placement report (B17.29), recruiting cycle (ch2).
- **Question bank.** 8 questions, 3/3/2. Families: 'walk me through your CV' and the ten-second version of each line; defending a project's numbers (what was measured, against what baseline); a competition result under questioning (overfitting the leaderboard, numeric: the expected maximum of k noisy leaderboard scores); why this firm type; a recruiter's constraints and incentives (the fee arithmetic, numeric). Roles: researcher 2, trader 2, developer 2, mle 1, bank 1. Firms: any 5, market maker 1, systematic fund 1, bank 1.
- **Facts to verify.** Burks, Cowgill, Hoffman and Housman 2015 (QJE), the value of hiring through employee referrals; UK Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003: no fee charged to work-seekers (dated); NYC Local Law 144 of 2021 on automated employment decision tools: bias audit and notice (effective 2023) (dated); EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), Annex III: AI systems for recruitment and selection are high-risk, and the application dates (dated); public competition pages (a market-prediction competition on Kaggle run by a trading firm; a trading-firm-run trading competition) for what they test, as examples only (dated; pointer to Book 17 ch. 29).
- **Data.** Figures: none planned (a CV line before and after may be a small two-column table). Code: iv_apply.py, the expected maximum of k noisy leaderboard scores (exact for Gaussians by numerical integration, checked by simulation) and the recruiter-fee arithmetic.
