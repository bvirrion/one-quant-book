# 14. Depositary Receipts and Dual Listings — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A company's shares trade in London and its receipts in New York; for three hours both are open and one is a currency conversion of the other, and for the rest of the day one of them is priced by a market maker who can only guess.
- **Sections.** Receipts, ratios and the depositary; Arbitrage in overlapping hours; Pricing across non-overlapping hours; Conversion: issuance, cancellation and its frictions.
- **Defines.** depositary receipt ratio, depositary receipt issuance, pre-release.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** depositary receipt (B1.17), dual listing (B1.17), cross rate (B2.14), spot value date (B2.14), settlement cycle (B1.5), cross-instrument fair price (ch2), law of one price (B5.1), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), implementation shortfall (B7.19).
- **Strategy files.** receipt against ordinary shares in overlapping hours; pricing the receipt when the home market is closed; conversion arbitrage through the depositary.
- **Tutorial.** Simulate a home listing, its receipt and the currency with independent liquidity; quote the receipt from the home market and the currency when both are open and from a futures proxy when not, and decide when converting receipts is worth the fees and the settlement lag.
- **Build.** `firm.drarb`: receipt fair price across hours (home price, ratio, currency, futures proxy), overlapping-hours arbitrage band, conversion decision with fees and settlement; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Two prices, one company — named result: the receipt market maker's quoting error in closed hours against open hours, and the conversion threshold after fees.
- **Facts to verify.** Gagnon and Karolyi 2010 Multi-market trading and arbitrage (JFE); SEC enforcement against depositary banks over ADR pre-release 2018-2019 (dated); Froot and Dabora 1999 How are stock prices affected by the location of trade? (JFE); A depositary's published fee schedule (dated).
