# 7. Short-Horizon Alpha — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** The bid queue is three times the ask queue and the futures ticked up two milliseconds ago; a market maker who ignores both will sell at the ask to someone who did not.
- **Sections.** What predicts the next second; Building the forecast: book, flow, cross-asset; Horizon, decay and evaluation at high frequency; Coupling the forecast to quotes: skew; When to take instead of make.
- **Defines.** short-horizon alpha, forecast skew, take threshold.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** order-flow imbalance (B7.8), queue imbalance (B7.8), microprice (B7.8), futures-to-cash lead (B7.10), information coefficient (B7.6), signal half-life (B7.13), intraday alpha (B8.14), prediction horizon (B8.14), market maker (B1.1), bid--ask spread (B1.1), adverse selection (B1.1), mid price (B1.1), inventory (B2.30), mark-out curve (B7.23), spread capture (B7.23), adverse-selection cost (B7.23), inventory P\&L (B7.23), fill rate (B7.23).
- **Strategy files.** book-imbalance quote skew; order-flow-imbalance taking; trade-sign continuation; futures-led quoting; post-sweep reversion.
- **Tutorial.** Build a seconds-horizon forecast from firm.lobfeat features and the paired instrument's returns on firm_tape, evaluate it out of sample, and couple it to a quoter as a skew and a take threshold; measure the P&L of each coupling rerun a tick later.
- **Build.** `firm.hfalpha`: event-time forecast targets, feature assembly from firm.lobfeat and firm.tradeflow, ridge forecasts with decay by horizon, skew and take-threshold couplings as Quoter wrappers; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Three to one on the bid — named result: the capture per share of the forecast-skewed quoter against the unskewed one, and the fraction lost when the forecast arrives one message late.
- **Facts to verify.** Cont, Kukanov, Stoikov 2014 The price impact of order book events (JFEc); Kolm, Turiel, Westray 2023 Deep order flow imbalance (Math Finance); Cartea, Donnelly, Jaimungal 2018 Enhancing trading strategies with order book signals (AMF); Lehalle and Mounjid 2017 Limit order strategic placement with adverse selection risk and the role of latency (MMF).
