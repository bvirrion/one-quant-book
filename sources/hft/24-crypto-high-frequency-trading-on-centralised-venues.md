# 24. Crypto High-Frequency Trading on Centralised Venues — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Bitcoin trades on dozens of exchanges around the clock with no consolidated tape; a market maker quotes on one, hedges on another, pays funding every few hours on a third, and has to fit it all inside each venue's request budget.
- **Sections.** Cross-exchange making and taking; Funding and basis at speed; Liquidation flows; Rate-limit-aware quoting; Market-maker programmes and their obligations.
- **Defines.** cross-exchange market making, hedge venue, liquidation absorption.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** funding rate (B3.17), funding interval (B3.17), perpetual future (B3.17), mark price (B3.17), liquidation cascade (B3.18), liquidation engine (B3.18), auto-deleveraging (B3.18), rate limit (B3.15), request weight (B3.15), post-only order (B3.15), cross-venue arbitrage (B3.16), prepositioned inventory (B3.16), uptime requirement (B3.25), token market-making agreement (B3.24), market maker (B1.1), bid--ask spread (B1.1), adverse selection (B1.1), mid price (B1.1), inventory (B2.30).
- **Strategy files.** cross-exchange market making; cross-exchange latency taking; funding-timestamp trading; liquidation-flow absorption; rate-limit-aware quoting under a programme; spot-perpetual basis market making.
- **Tutorial.** Quote a synthetic perpetual on one venue while hedging on another's spot book, with funding intervals, a planted liquidation cascade and each venue's rate limits from firm.ratelimit; measure capture, hedge slippage, funding P&L and uptime.
- **Build.** `firm.cryptohft`: two-venue making and hedging with transfer and inventory limits, funding accrual at timestamps, liquidation-cascade flow, a rate-limit budget allocator; Python, on firm.perp, firm.liquidation, firm.ratelimit and firm.mmprogram.
- **Weekend problem.** Around the clock — named result: the cross-exchange maker's P&L by component over a week with one cascade, and the capture it gives up to stay inside its request budget.
- **Facts to verify.** Makarov and Schoar 2020 Trading and arbitrage in cryptocurrency markets (JFE); A major venue's API rate limits and market-maker programme terms (dated); Funding intervals of major perpetual venues (dated); The 10 October 2025 liquidation event: size and venues (dated; as Book 3 where sourced); Kaiko or venue research on cross-exchange spreads (dated).
