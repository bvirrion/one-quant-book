# 27. Risk Controls — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 1 August 2012 a market maker's new code sent millions of unintended orders in the first three quarters of an hour of trading; the loss was about four hundred and sixty million dollars, and the regulator's order afterwards reads as a checklist of the controls it did not have.
- **Sections.** Pre-trade limits; Position, loss and message limits; Price collars and fat-finger checks; Kill switches: who pulls them and when; The regulatory requirements for algorithmic firms.
- **Defines.** kill switch, price collar, pre-trade risk check, market access rule, loss limit.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** risk limit (B6.29), position limit (B1.27), drawdown limit (B8.28), limit up--limit down (B1.31), clearly erroneous trade (B1.31), sponsored access (B1.4), direct market access (B1.4), rogue trading (B6.28), message throttle (ch10), order-to-trade ratio (ch10).
- **Tutorial.** Replay a synthetic runaway algorithm through firm.riskctl with each control switched on in turn (order-rate limit, position limit, loss limit, collar, kill switch) and measure the loss each one would have capped and the false alarms each raises on normal days.
- **Build.** `firm.riskctl`: a limits hierarchy (firm, desk, strategy, instrument) in a declarative schema, pre-trade checks, post-trade loss and position monitors, kill-switch semantics and an audit log; Python reference with fixtures that Book 13's nanosecond gate replays.
- **Weekend problem.** Forty-five minutes — named result: the loss the runaway algorithm would have made under each control and the earliest control that stops it.
- **Facts to verify.** SEC order In the Matter of Knight Capital Americas 2013 (Release 34-70694) (dated); SEC Rule 15c3-5 market access rule (dated); Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/589 (RTS 6) (dated); CFTC electronic trading risk principles 2021 (dated); FIA best practices for automated trading risk controls (dated); FCA and PRA fines on a bank's May 2022 erroneous basket order, 2024 (dated).
