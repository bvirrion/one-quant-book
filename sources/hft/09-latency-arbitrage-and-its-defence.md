# 9. Latency Arbitrage and Its Defence — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A quote that was right a millisecond ago is still resting on one venue after the price has moved on another; the first firm to reach it wins a riskless tick, and the loser of the race is the market maker who left it there.
- **Sections.** Stale quotes and races; The evidence: races, their size and who wins; Defence by the market maker; Defence by the venue: speed bumps, asymmetric delays, batch auctions; Last look as a defence.
- **Defines.** latency arbitrage, latency race, stale-quote sniping, speed bump, asymmetric speed bump, frequent batch auction.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** last look (B2.15), hold time (B2.15), national best bid and offer (B1.9), order protection rule (B1.9), market-data latency (B7.18), order-entry latency (B7.18), cross-venue lead (ch8), market maker (B1.1), bid--ask spread (B1.1), adverse selection (B1.1), mid price (B1.1), inventory (B2.30).
- **Strategy files.** cross-venue stale-quote sniping; futures-led sniping of equity quotes; liquidity provision behind an asymmetric speed bump; defensive quote cancellation on a lead signal.
- **Tutorial.** Simulate races between a liquidity provider and snipers with latency distributions on a two-venue market (firm_tape pair now, Book 10's simulator later); measure the provider's loss to sniping, then add a speed bump, an asymmetric delay and a batch auction and measure again.
- **Build.** `firm.latrace`: race simulation with per-agent latency distributions, venue delays (symmetric, asymmetric for takers) and a periodic batch-auction venue; the sniping tax on liquidity providers; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The race nobody sees — named result: the latency-arbitrage tax as a share of the provider's spread, with and without each venue defence.
- **Facts to verify.** Budish, Cramton, Shim 2015 The high-frequency trading arms race (QJE); Aquilina, Budish, O'Neill 2022 Quantifying the high-frequency trading arms race (QJE); Baldauf and Mollner 2020 High-frequency trading and market performance (JF); SEC approval of IEX exchange registration and its speed bump 2016 (dated); Asymmetric speed bumps: SEC orders on Cboe EDGA and others; TSX Alpha; ICE/Eurex passive protection (dated); Shkilko and Sokolov 2020 Every cloud has a silver lining (JF).
