# 12. The Asia-Pacific Map — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** India's securities regulator found that the National Stock Exchange's colocation facility had sent its tick-by-tick data over TCP, member by member, so that a member that logged in first, to a less crowded server, saw prices first; the case ran from 2015 until a settlement that the Supreme Court allowed in September 2026.
- **Sections.** Tokyo; Hong Kong and Singapore; Sydney; Mumbai and the colocation case; Onshore China and its restrictions.
- **Defines.** unicast dissemination, dissemination fairness.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** geodesic distance (ch10), latency floor (ch10), group index (ch10), route factor (ch10), colocation (ch9), cross-connect (ch9), proximity hosting (ch9), multicast (B13.16), market data feed (B1.4), Stock Connect (B1.12), serialisation delay (ch1), head-of-line blocking (ch1), high-frequency trading (B10.24).
- **Tutorial.** Extend the site table with Tokyo, Hong Kong, Singapore, Sydney and Mumbai (ledger rows) and compute the Asian latency floors; then simulate unicast dissemination of one tick to members on several servers (per-connection send cost, server load, connection order: parameters stated, labelled as a simulation) against multicast, and measure each member's receive delay by connection rank and server. End state: the Asia-Pacific map and a chart of receive delay against connection rank for unicast and multicast.
- **Build.** `firm.dissem`: a dissemination model -- unicast over TCP to members spread over servers (connection order, per-message send cost, server load) and multicast with per-port replication; per-member receive times and fairness measures (spread of receive delay, stability of the rank order over ticks); Python. Used to audit a venue's documented dissemination design in chapter 22.
- **Weekend problem.** First to log in -- named result: the receive-time advantage of the first-connected member over the median member for a given number of members per server, and its value against the spread-crossing race, with and without multicast.
- **Facts to verify.** SEBI orders in the NSE colocation matter (2019) and the Securities Appellate Tribunal's January 2023 decision (Rs 100 crore deposit; disgorgement set aside); SEBI's acceptance of NSE's settlement (about Rs 1,492 crore) and the Supreme Court's order of September 2026 allowing it; JPX colocation service (arrownet, primary data centre in Tokyo) (dated); HKEX colocation data centre (Tseung Kwan O) (dated); SGX colocation (dated); ASX Australian Liquidity Centre (dated); CSRC Securities Programme Trading Rules (in force 9 October 2024) and the SSE/SZSE implementation rules (7 July 2025) on colocation and system access.
- **Data.** Site coordinates (ledger rows); dissemination model is a labelled simulation.
