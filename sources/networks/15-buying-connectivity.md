# 15. Buying Connectivity — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A firm that wants the fastest route between two cities rarely builds it: it rents a few megabits a second of someone else's microwave network, or a feed normalised and carried over it, under a contract whose most important clause says what happens when it rains.
- **Sections.** What is bought: circuits, wavelengths, bandwidth and feeds; Financial extranets and managed infrastructure; Wireless-bandwidth vendors; Contracts and service levels; Orders of magnitude for price.
- **Defines.** financial extranet, managed infrastructure, service-level agreement, service credit, mean time between failures, mean time to repair, route diversity, metro circuit.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** colocation (ch9), cross-connect (ch9), proximity hosting (ch9), lit service (ch13), wavelength service (ch13), dark fibre (ch13), indefeasible right of use (ch13), link availability (ch14), microwave link (ch14), direct feed (B1.9), build against buy (B16.20).
- **Tutorial.** Simulate a year of outages for single, dual and dual-diverse circuits (Poisson failures, lognormal repairs, a common-mode share for circuits in one duct), compute availability, SLA credits under a published credit schedule and the cost of downtime, and plot availability against annual cost. End state: an availability-against-cost chart for the three designs and the credit each earns.
- **Build.** `firm.slamodel`: a circuit catalogue as data (type, provider category, latency, availability commitment, credit schedule, price with source row), availability from MTBF and MTTR with common-mode failures, an SLA credit calculator, and a downtime-cost model; Python. Consumed by chapters 28 and 29.
- **Weekend problem.** Two circuits are not twice as good -- named result: the availability of two circuits that share a duct against two diverse ones, the expected annual minutes down, and the service credit against the trading loss.
- **Facts to verify.** venues' own published lists of connectivity and extranet providers (CME, Deutsche Börse/Eurex, Nasdaq) (dated); a public SLA document of a data-centre or network provider (availability commitment, credit schedule) (dated); a wireless market-data product's public description (dated); exchange connectivity fee schedules (dated); public price points only; otherwise orders of magnitude stated generically.
- **Data.** Catalogue rows (dated ledger rows); outages simulated.
