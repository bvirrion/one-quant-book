# 28. Disaster Recovery and Regulatory Requirements — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** When Hurricane Sandy struck New York in October 2012 the U.S. equity and options markets stayed closed for two days; two years later the SEC's systems-integrity rules required critical exchange systems to be able to resume within two hours of a wide-scale disruption, and required members designated by each venue to test the backup site with it every year.
- **Sections.** Secondary sites and how they are placed; Exchange failover tests; The resilience rules firms are examined on; Recovery objectives and runbooks.
- **Defines.** primary data centre, disaster-recovery site, recovery time objective, recovery point objective, business continuity plan, exchange failover test, operational resilience, important business service, impact tolerance.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** failover (B13.24), primary--backup replication (B13.24), split brain (B13.24), replicated state machine (B13.24), operational risk (B6.28), kill switch (B11.27), algorithmic trading (B10.24), incident response (B15.28), route diversity (ch15), service-level agreement (ch15), geodesic distance (ch10), latency floor (ch10), group index (ch10), route factor (ch10).
- **Tutorial.** Model a primary and a disaster-recovery site (their separation from firm.geomap, a hazard radius), replication lag giving the recovery point, and a failover runbook of steps with duration distributions giving the recovery time; simulate hot and warm designs and compare their probability of meeting a two-hour target and their costs. End state: recovery-time distributions against the two-hour line for each design.
- **Build.** `firm.drplan`: a disaster-recovery plan as data (sites, systems, replication modes, runbook steps with durations, test calendar), a recovery-time and recovery-point simulator, compliance checks against targets, and an exchange-test calendar (dated rows); Python. Consumed by chapter 29.
- **Weekend problem.** The two-hour rule -- named result: the probability that warm and hot recovery designs resume within two hours, and the annual cost of the difference.
- **Facts to verify.** Regulation SCI, 17 CFR 242.1000-1007 (2014): two-hour resumption goal for critical SCI systems, business continuity and disaster recovery testing with designated members (242.1004); US equity and options market closure of 29-30 October 2012 (Hurricane Sandy) from an SEC or exchange statement; Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/589 (RTS 6): business continuity for algorithmic trading firms; FCA PS21/3 and PRA SS1/21: important business services, impact tolerances, 31 March 2025 deadline; Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA), applying from 17 January 2025; an exchange's published disaster-recovery site and annual failover-test notice (dated).
- **Data.** Plan as data; simulation labelled; durations stated.
