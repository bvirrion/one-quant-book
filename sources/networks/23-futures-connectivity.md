# 23. Futures Connectivity — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** When each order-entry gateway of a futures exchange had its own queue and its own jitter, some firms opened many sessions on many gateways and sent each order down whichever had been fastest lately; the exchange's answer was to change the gateways, and the history of those changes is a history of what 'fair' means at the microsecond.
- **Sections.** Order-entry sessions and market-segment gateways; Gateway fairness and its history; Drop copy and clearing-firm risk layers; Market data: channels, recovery and bandwidth.
- **Defines.** market segment gateway, gateway fairness, clearing-firm credit control.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** order-entry session (B10.26), futures commission merchant (B1.30), clearing member (B1.30), drop copy (B13.21), simple binary encoding (B13.16), incremental channel (B13.16), snapshot channel (B13.16), kill switch (B11.27), pre-trade risk check (B11.27), colocation (ch9), jitter (B13.1).
- **Tutorial.** A labelled simulation: orders to several gateways with independent queues and service jitter, a firm spreading orders over k sessions against one with a single session, then the same under a single segment gateway with deterministic ordering; count race wins and price the extra sessions from a dated fee schedule. End state: a chart of win rate against sessions under the two designs.
- **Build.** `firm.gwmodel`: gateway and session models (per-gateway queues, jitter, segment routing, fairness design), a session planner under an exchange's session limits and dated fees, and a hook for firm.ordergw's drop-copy reconciler (wrapped); Python. Consumed by chapter 29.
- **Weekend problem.** Twelve sessions for one order -- named result: the probability of winning a race with k sessions under jittery gateways against a segment gateway, and the session fee above which the extra sessions do not pay.
- **Facts to verify.** CME Group Client Systems Wiki: market segment gateway, iLink 3, drop copy, Risk Management Interface (dated); CME Group Globex fees for order-entry sessions (dated); Eurex T7 connectivity guide: gateways and their fairness features (dated); a public source on the history of gateway fairness changes (exchange notice, regulatory document or conference talk).
- **Data.** Fees and limits (dated rows); simulation labelled.
