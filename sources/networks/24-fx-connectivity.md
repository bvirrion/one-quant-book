# 24. FX Connectivity — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** The same currency pair trades on matching engines in London, New York and Tokyo, each the primary venue for some pairs; a liquidity provider in London streaming yen prices to the Tokyo engine quotes on information that is a Eurasian landmass old by the time it arrives.
- **Sections.** The matching sites; Credit checks in the order path; Integrating as a liquidity provider; Aggregators.
- **Defines.** bilateral credit screen, multi-site matching.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** primary venue (B2.14), electronic communication network (B2.14), single-dealer platform (B2.14), FX prime brokerage (B2.14), streaming quote (B2.15), last look (B2.15), hold time (B2.15), liquidity aggregator (B10.21), FIX session (B13.15), geodesic distance (ch10), latency floor (ch10), group index (ch10), route factor (ch10).
- **Tutorial.** Place the FX matching sites on the map (dated ledger rows) and compute their latency floors; simulate, with stated parameters, a liquidity provider's quote stream fanned out to several venues and aggregators from London, and measure quote staleness at each site against the hold time of its last look. End state: staleness by site and the hold time that would neutralise it.
- **Build.** `firm.fxlinks`: an FX connectivity model -- matching sites and primary pairs as dated data, a liquidity provider's stream fan-out, credit-screen stage latency in the order path, and staleness at each venue; Python. Consumed by chapter 29.
- **Weekend problem.** USDJPY at three sites -- named result: the quote staleness at the Tokyo engine of a London-hosted liquidity provider, and the hold time that would neutralise it.
- **Facts to verify.** EBS Market matching-engine locations (London, New York, Tokyo) from LSEG/EBS documentation (dated); LSEG FX Matching location (dated); FX Global Code principle 17 on last look (pointer B2.15); a venue's documentation of credit screening in its order path (dated).
- **Data.** Site table (ledger rows); simulation labelled.
