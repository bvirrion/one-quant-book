# 9. Colocation Products and How They Are Sold — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In an exchange's own data centre a customer does not choose where its cabinet stands relative to the matching engine, and does not need to: every cross-connect to the exchange's systems is cut to the same length, so a cabinet next to the engine waits for its signal to run the same metres of fibre as one at the far end of the hall.
- **Sections.** What colocation sells: space, power, cooling and hands; Cross-connects and the meet-me room; Equal cable lengths and the fairness rules; Port speeds, latency tiers and connectivity products; Fee schedules and how a footprint is bought.
- **Defines.** colocation, proximity hosting, colocation cabinet, colocation cage, cross-connect, meet-me room, latency equalisation, remote hands, power density.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** exchange member (B1.4), sponsored access (B1.4), direct feed (B1.9), non-display fee (B1.29), rack unit (ch8), thermal design power (ch8), line rate (ch1), speed bump (B11.9), latency arbitrage (B11.9).
- **Tutorial.** Turn an exchange's published colocation fee schedule into data (cabinet, kilowatts, cross-connects, ports, installation fees: dated ledger rows) and price three footprints (a quarter cabinet with two 10 Gb/s ports, a full cabinet, a cage of four cabinets) over three years with installation amortised; compute the delay that latency equalisation adds to the nearest cabinet from the coil length. End state: an annual-cost bar chart by footprint and cost component, and the equalisation arithmetic.
- **Build.** `firm.colobill`: colocation fee schedules as data (one file per venue, every row with its ledger id and date), a bill calculator (monthly recurring, installation amortised, market-data connection fees), a comparison across venues and footprints, and staleness warnings for rows older than 60 days; Python. Consumed by chapters 22, 23 and 29.
- **Weekend problem.** The cage that paid for itself -- named result: the monthly all-in bill of a given footprint at one venue, and the edge per share (or per contract) that pays for it at a given daily volume.
- **Facts to verify.** an exchange's colocation fee schedule in its rulebook or price list (Nasdaq Rule 7034 or the NYSE colocation price list) (dated); an SEC release or exchange filing stating that cross-connects are of equal length (to verify; otherwise stated generically); Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/573 (RTS 10): fair and non-discriminatory colocation and proximity hosting; SEC orders approving colocation services as exchange services subject to fair-access rules; CME Group colocation service description (Aurora) (dated); a named exchange's wireless or laser connectivity product (e.g., the NYSE Wireless Connections filings, SR-NYSE-2020-05) (dated); signal delay in fibre per metre (group index, pointer to chapter 10).
- **Data.** Fee schedules (dated ledger rows); computed bills.
