# 11. The European Map — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 6 June 2022 Euronext finished moving its core data centre from Basildon, east of London, to Bergamo in northern Italy: overnight the matching engines of Paris, Amsterdam, Brussels, Lisbon, Dublin, Oslo and Milan were more than a thousand kilometres further from London's trading firms.
- **Sections.** The London-area sites; Frankfurt and Zurich; The northern-Italy migration; The Nordics and the rest; The London-Frankfurt corridor.
- **Defines.** carrier-neutral data centre, point of presence, latency corridor.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** geodesic distance (ch10), latency floor (ch10), group index (ch10), route factor (ch10), colocation (ch9), cross-connect (ch9), proximity hosting (ch9), multilateral trading facility (B1.4), latency arbitrage (B11.9).
- **Tutorial.** Extend the site table with the European sites (ledger rows), compute the London-Frankfurt, London-Bergamo, Frankfurt-Bergamo and Basildon-to-each distances and latency floors, compare them with published corridor latencies, and redraw the London-Frankfurt-Milan triangle before and after the migration. End state: the European map from computed coordinates and a before-and-after latency table for the migration.
- **Build.** `firm.venuesites`: the venue-to-site registry -- for each venue (by MIC, joined to Book 1's firm.venues registry through a wrapper, never an edit) its primary data centre, disaster-recovery site, points of presence and market-data distribution points, each with a source row and date, and queries such as 'nearest site of each venue to a given data centre'; Python, on firm.geomap. Consumed by chapters 12, 17, 22-26 and 29.
- **Weekend problem.** Basildon to Bergamo -- named result: the change in the one-way latency floor from London's main trading data centre to Euronext's matching engines caused by the 2022 migration, and the new shape of the London-Frankfurt-Milan triangle.
- **Facts to verify.** Euronext press release, 15 June 2022: migration of the core data centre from Basildon to the Aruba Global Cloud Data Centre in Bergamo, completed 6 June 2022; Deutsche Börse / Eurex colocation site (Equinix FR2, Frankfurt) (dated); LSEG / London Stock Exchange data centre and colocation location (dated); ICE Futures Europe data centre (Basildon) (dated); SIX Swiss Exchange data centre (dated); Nasdaq Nordic colocation site (dated); Eurex or Euronext points of presence in London and elsewhere (dated); a published London-Frankfurt wireless latency (dated).
- **Data.** Site coordinates (ledger rows); computed distances and floors.
