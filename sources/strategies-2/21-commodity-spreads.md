# 21. Commodity Spreads — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A refinery earns the difference between crude and the products made from it; traders who hold that spread rather than oil itself are betting on refining margins, storage and seasons, not on the price of oil.
- **Sections.** Calendar spreads again; Quality and location; Processing spreads: crack, spark, crush; Risk in spread books.
- **Defines.** processing-spread trade, quality spread, location spread.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** crack spread (B3.3), 3-2-1 crack spread (B3.3), spark spread (B3.5), crush spread (B3.9), locational basis (B3.4), calendar spread (B1.19), backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28).
- **Strategy files.** crack-spread mean reversion; spark spread with heat-rate view; soybean crush trade; Brent-WTI location spread; sweet-sour quality spread; seasonal gasoline crack.
- **Tutorial.** Measure crack and location spreads from EIA product and crude data where available, simulate processing margins with planted seasonal and mean-reverting components, and trade them hedged.
- **Build.** `firm.commspread`: spread construction by ratio, mean-reversion and seasonal signals on spreads, hedged spread books; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Not the price of oil — named result: the crack-spread trade's Sharpe ratio and its correlation with crude.
- **Facts to verify.** EIA product and crude prices (as B3); Girma and Paulson 1999 risk arbitrage opportunities in petroleum futures spreads (J. Futures Markets).

## EXCLUDED

- Futures prices of crack, crush and spark spreads (NYMEX, CBOT, power exchanges): licensed; the real-data rules use EIA spot assessments, an optimistic proxy for tradable prices.
- Spark spreads and heat rates from real power markets: not fetched; the spark-spread strategy file states the mechanism only.
- Sweet-sour quality spreads (for example Mars against WTI): no public-domain daily series fetched; the quality spread is synthetic.
- Girma and Paulson 1998 (seasonality in petroleum futures spreads): no abstract available; not cited.
- Named firms' refining-margin or location trades: no citable primary source; no firm named.
