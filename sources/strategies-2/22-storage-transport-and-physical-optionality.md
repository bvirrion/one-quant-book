# 22. Storage, Transport and Physical Optionality — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A trader who owns a gas storage cavern owns a string of calendar spread options; one who owns pipeline capacity owns location spread options; the physical asset is valued as the options it contains.
- **Sections.** Storage as an option; Transport as an option; Monetising the options; What the asset-backed trader owns.
- **Defines.** storage deal, transport option, asset-backed trading.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** rolling intrinsic (B3.10), intrinsic storage value (B6.16), calendar-spread option (B3.12), locational basis (B3.4), backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28).
- **Strategy files.** rolling-intrinsic storage; spread-option storage hedge; transport capacity optimisation; cargo diversion.
- **Tutorial.** Value a storage facility and a transport contract on synthetic gas curves by intrinsic, rolling intrinsic and spread-option methods, and trade their hedges.
- **Build.** `firm.physopt`: storage and transport valuation (intrinsic, rolling intrinsic, spread-option bounds) and hedging programmes; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The asset as options — named result: storage value by method and the extra value of rolling the hedge.
- **Facts to verify.** Eydeland and Wolyniec 2003 energy and power risk management (Wiley); Secomandi 2010 optimal commodity trading with a capacitated storage asset (Management Science).
