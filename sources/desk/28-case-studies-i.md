# 28. Case Studies I — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 23 September 1998 fourteen banks and brokers agreed to put about 3.6 billion dollars into a hedge fund that had begun the year with under five billion of capital and more than a hundred billion of assets on its balance sheet. Every number in that sentence was the result of a firm-level decision taken years before.
- **Sections.** How to read a failure; 1998: leverage and the terms of funding; 2006: a natural-gas fund's concentration; August 2007: the crowded exit; What the three share.
- **Defines.** funding liquidity risk, market liquidity risk, days to liquidate.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** leverage (B1.6), haircut (B1.6), repurchase agreement (B1.6), margin call (B1.6), liquidity spiral (B1.31), fire sale (B3.28), crowding (B7.28), crowded trade (B7.28), unwind (B7.28), open interest (B1.18), accountability level (B3.13), position limit (B1.27), stat-arb book (B8.1), value at risk (B6.21), stress test (B6.22), concentration limit (ch7), survival horizon (ch14).
- **Tutorial.** Reconstruct from the public record the 1998 fund's capital, assets and leverage through the year and its losses of August and September, and rerun the path at half the leverage and with longer-term funding; reconstruct the natural-gas fund's share of open interest and its days to liquidate; rerun Book 7's August 2007 unwind (firm.capacity.unwind) for a holder and a seller. End state: a three-panel chart of the reconstructed paths with the counterfactuals.
- **Build.** `firm.casebook` (part 1): committed derived tables of the public record for each case (with source and page), reconstruction functions (capital and leverage paths, concentration, days to liquidate), and counterfactual runs (leverage, funding term, concentration limit, sell or hold); Python; uses firm.capacity and firm.marginspiral.
- **Weekend problem.** Three firms, three decisions -- named result: on the public figures, the 1998 fund's capital at the end of September had it run half the leverage; the natural-gas fund's days to liquidate at a stated share of volume; and the August 2007 seller's loss against the holder's.
- **Facts to verify.** GAO report GAO/GGD-00-3 (1999) on Long-Term Capital Management: capital, balance sheet, derivatives, losses, recapitalisation; President's Working Group on Financial Markets, Hedge funds, leverage, and the lessons of Long-Term Capital Management (April 1999); US Senate PSI staff report, Excessive speculation in the natural gas market (June 2007): Amaranth positions, share of open interest, losses; CFTC v. Amaranth Advisors (2007) and its 2009 settlement; FERC order and Hunter v. FERC (D.C. Cir. 2013) (dated); Khandani and Lo 2011 (pointer to Book 7 ch. 28 and Book 8 ch. 1); Goldman Sachs statement on the recapitalisation of Global Equity Opportunities, August 2007.
- **Data.** data/desk/case_ltcm.csv, case_amaranth.csv (derived from the reports).
