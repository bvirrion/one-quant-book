# 30. Competition and the Future — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** When the US securities regulator proposed in 2022 to make retail orders compete in auctions, its own analysis found that a handful of wholesalers executed most marketable retail orders. Trading looks like a market of many firms; business by business it is often a market of a few.
- **Sections.** Moats in trading; Consolidation and market structure; Tokenised assets; Round-the-clock markets; What machine-learning tooling changes.
- **Defines.** economic moat, market concentration, Herfindahl--Hirschman index, free-entry equilibrium, asset tokenisation, tokenised fund, round-the-clock trading.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** wholesaler (B1.10), payment for order flow (B1.10), internalisation (B1.10), token (B3.14), smart contract (B3.14), large language model (B12.14), machine-learning platform (B12.28), revenue capture (B11.1), capture decay (B11.28), strategy capacity (B7.28), fixed cost base (ch1), technology treadmill (ch2), latency arms race (ch19).
- **Tutorial.** A market of size M with firms that pay a fixed cost F and compete on capture: compute the free-entry number of firms and the Herfindahl-Hirschman index; halve F (cloud, open-source tooling) and double it (a speed tier); simulate a merger with cost synergies; and measure concentration from public market-share data where the regulator has published it (derived tables). End state: a chart of the free-entry number of firms and the index against the fixed cost, with the public figures marked.
- **Build.** `firm.moats`: market shares to the Herfindahl-Hirschman index and concentration ratios, a free-entry Cournot model with fixed costs, a merger simulator with synergies, and a scenario runner for falling or rising fixed costs; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The fixed cost falls -- named result: the free-entry number of firms and the index at the book's parameters, and how both move when the fixed cost halves.
- **Facts to verify.** SEC Release 34-96495, Order Competition Rule proposal (December 2022): wholesaler concentration, and its withdrawal (2025) (dated); US DOJ/FTC Merger Guidelines 2023: concentration thresholds (dated); Virtu's acquisitions of KCG (2017) and ITG (2019) (filings); a tokenised money-market fund's public documents (prospectus or SEC filing) (dated); SEC approval of 24X National Exchange (2024) and exchanges' extended-hours plans (dated); DTCC/NSCC plans for extended clearing hours (dated); Sutton 1991, Sunk costs and market structure.
- **Data.** data/desk/concentration.csv (derived from the SEC release); synthetic market.
