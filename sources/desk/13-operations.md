# 13. Operations — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 28 May 2024 most US securities moved from settlement two days after the trade to one. A firm that had had a day to find a missing settlement instruction now had the evening of the trade date, and its operations team's working day moved with it.
- **Sections.** Front, middle and back office; Reconciliation and breaks; Corporate actions and reference events; Collateral management; Settlement discipline.
- **Defines.** front office, middle office, back office, collateral management, collateral dispute, settlement discipline regime, key risk indicator.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** settlement (B1.5), settlement fail (B1.5), settlement cycle (B1.5), central securities depository (B1.5), delivery versus payment (B1.5), variation margin (B1.5), margin call (B1.6), fails charge (B2.5), credit support annex (B2.10), collateral threshold (B6.17), minimum transfer amount (B6.17), buy-in (B1.16), M/M/1 queue (B4.8), segregation of duties (B6.28), operational risk (B6.28), reconciliation break (B15.22), break ageing (B15.22), trade capture (B15.21).
- **Tutorial.** Breaks arrive, age and are resolved by a team: model the queue (arrival rate, resolution times by break type, staff), compute the ageing profile and the cost of breaks that become settlement fails under a penalty schedule, and size the team for a service level; then shorten the settlement cycle and watch the fail cost move. End state: an ageing chart by staffing level, and the staff needed for a target.
- **Build.** `firm.opsmetrics`: a break inventory with types and ageing buckets, a queueing model of resolution (uses firm.queues), fail cost under a penalty schedule parameterised by asset class (rates as inputs), a collateral-call workflow with disputes, key-risk-indicator thresholds and a daily operations report; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** One day less -- named result: the staff needed to keep 95 per cent of breaks resolved before settlement under a two-day and a one-day cycle, and the fail cost avoided.
- **Facts to verify.** SEC T+1 final rule (February 2023; compliance 28 May 2024) (dated); DTCC reports on affirmation rates and fails after T+1 (dated); EU CSDR settlement discipline regime: cash penalties since 1 February 2022; mandatory buy-in amendments (Regulation (EU) 2023/2845) (dated); EU and UK moves to T+1 (October 2027) (dated); ISDA or BCBS-IOSCO material on collateral disputes (dated); Book 1 ch. 8 corporate actions (pointer).
- **Data.** Synthetic; dated boxes from DTCC and ESMA publications.
