# 14. Treasury and Funding — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In March 2020 initial margin at the world's major clearing houses rose by hundreds of billions of dollars in a few weeks. Every firm with cleared positions had to find the cash that morning, from wherever its treasury had left it.
- **Sections.** What treasury does in a trading firm; Prime-broker relationships and margin terms; Margin optimisation; Forecasting cash and collateral; Funding in stress.
- **Defines.** house margin, margin lock-up, margin optimisation, unencumbered cash, liquidity buffer, survival horizon.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** leverage (B1.6), prime broker (B1.6), margin call (B1.6), haircut (B1.6), rehypothecation (B1.6), initial margin (B1.5), variation margin (B1.5), portfolio margining (B1.20), procyclicality (B1.20), repurchase agreement (B1.6), funds-transfer pricing (B6.24), liquidity coverage ratio (B6.24), margin spiral (B3.28), initial-margin model (B6.25), cross margin (B3.18), futures commission merchant (B1.30).
- **Tutorial.** A book of positions across three prime brokers and a clearing broker, each with its own house-margin rule (gross, net, concentration add-ons) and financing spread: find the allocation that minimises margin plus financing (linear programme), then forecast cash over thirty days with a stressed margin path and compute the survival horizon. End state: margin by allocation (one broker, pro rata, optimised), and a cash-runway chart under base and stress.
- **Build.** `firm.treasury`: counterparty margin rules as data, the allocation optimiser (scipy linprog), a cash and collateral forecast engine (flows, margin calls from firm.initmargin and firm.margin, financing), and liquidity-buffer and survival-horizon metrics; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Three brokers and a bad week -- named result: the margin saved by optimising across three brokers against using one, and the survival horizon in the stress week with and without margin lock-up.
- **Facts to verify.** BCBS-CPMI-IOSCO, Review of margining practices (September 2022): March 2020 increases in initial margin; FSB, Liquidity preparedness for margin and collateral calls (final report, December 2024) (dated); Credit Suisse Special Committee report on Archegos (29 July 2021): static margin terms (pointer to Book 1 ch. 6); FINRA Rule 4210 portfolio margin and Regulation T (dated); PwC administrators' reports on Lehman Brothers International (Europe) client assets (pointer to Book 1 ch. 6).
- **Data.** Synthetic.
