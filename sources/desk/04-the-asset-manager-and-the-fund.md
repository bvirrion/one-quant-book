# 4. The Asset Manager and the Fund — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 3 June 2019 a UK equity income fund of a few billion pounds suspended dealing: its investors had asked for more money back than it could raise by selling what it held, on the terms it had promised them. The fund never reopened. A fund's liquidity terms are a promise about the assets, written in the documents of the vehicle.
- **Sections.** Structures: manager, fund and vehicle; Service providers: administrator, custodian, auditor, prime broker; Fees in practice: classes, hurdles, crystallisation and equalisation; Liquidity terms and the liquidity mismatch; What investors ask for: side letters and managed accounts.
- **Defines.** master--feeder structure, general partner, limited partner, fund administrator, custodian, UCITS, separately managed account, share class, fee hurdle, crystallisation, equalisation, redemption notice period, lock-up period, redemption gate, side pocket, swing pricing, liquidity mismatch, side letter, most-favoured-nation clause.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** asset manager (B1.1), hedge fund (B1.1), assets under management (B1.1), mandate (B1.3), management fee (B1.3), performance fee (B1.3), high-water mark (B1.3), net asset value (B1.14), prime broker (B1.6), commodity trading advisor (B1.3), fire sale (B3.28), tracking error (B1.3).
- **Tutorial.** A fund with two share classes and investors entering at different net asset values: run a year of monthly returns through firm.fees (wrapped) with a hurdle and annual crystallisation, then equalise; then stress it: a 30 per cent redemption request against a portfolio with a liquidity ladder, with and without a 10 per cent gate and swing pricing. End state: the fee each investor pays with and without equalisation, and the staying investors' NAV after the stress under the three policies.
- **Build.** `firm.fundterms`: fund terms as data (classes, fees, hurdle, crystallisation, notice, lock-up, gates at investor and fund level, side pockets), an investor register, dealing-day processing of subscriptions and redemptions against a portfolio liquidity ladder, equalisation and swing pricing; Python; wraps firm.fees.
- **Weekend problem.** The Friday queue -- named result: the largest redemption the fund can meet within its notice period without selling any position at more than a set impact, and the dilution the staying investors bear without swing pricing.
- **Facts to verify.** FCA statements and final notice on the LF Woodford Equity Income Fund suspension (date, size, findings) (dated); SEC Private Funds Statistics (Form PF): portfolio liquidity against investor liquidity of qualifying hedge funds (derived table) (dated); UCITS Directive 2009/65/EC: eligible assets, liquidity, redemption (dated); SEC Rule 22e-4 liquidity risk management; swing pricing rules (dated); IOSCO/FSB liquidity management tools guidance (2023-2025) (dated); Cayman Islands Mutual Funds Act registration (CIMA) (dated); an AMF sanction on side pockets (H2O Asset Management) as a regulator record (dated); industry note on equalisation and series accounting (AIMA or an administrator's publication).
- **Data.** data/desk/form_pf_liquidity.csv (derived from SEC statistics); synthetic fund.
