# 5. The Bank Markets Division — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A large bank's annual report allocates tens of billions of dollars of common equity to its corporate and investment bank and reports the return earned on it; every desk inside answers for its share of that equity, measured by whichever capital rule binds. A repo desk that looks free under one rule can be the most expensive desk in the building under another.
- **Sections.** What a markets division sells: flow, financing and structuring; The capital stack: common equity, leverage and stress buffers; Return on capital by desk; The balance sheet as a scarce input; The client franchise.
- **Defines.** common equity tier 1 capital, leverage ratio, leverage exposure measure, stress capital buffer, G-SIB surcharge, allocated equity, binding capital constraint, balance-sheet charge.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** sell side (B1.2), broker-dealer (B1.2), flow trading (B1.2), franchise (B1.2), prime services (B1.2), risk-weighted assets (B1.2), trading book (B6.23), banking book (B6.23), hurdle rate (B6.19), capital valuation adjustment (B6.19), funds-transfer pricing (B6.24), franchise P\&L (B9.24), client tiering (B9.24), central risk book (B9.25), repurchase agreement (B1.6), return on equity (ch1).
- **Tutorial.** Six stylised desks (flow rates, repo, prime services, equity derivatives, FX, credit) with revenue, costs, risk-weighted assets, leverage exposure and stress loss: allocate the division's equity by each constraint (risk-weighted assets times a target ratio, leverage exposure times the leverage requirement, stress loss) and by the binding maximum; compute each desk's return on allocated equity and see which desks change sign. End state: a chart of return on allocated equity by desk under the three keys and the binding one.
- **Build.** `firm.bankdesk`: a desk balance-sheet and capital model (risk-weighted assets by type, leverage exposure, stress contribution), equity allocation by constraint, return on allocated equity, a balance-sheet charge per unit of leverage exposure, and the desk mix that maximises the division's return under the binding constraint (linear programme, scipy); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The desk that uses no capital -- named result: the repo desk's return on allocated equity under the risk-weighted and the leverage keys, and the balance-sheet charge at which it breaks even.
- **Facts to verify.** JPMorgan Chase Form 10-K: equity allocated by segment and the corporate and investment bank's return (dated); Goldman Sachs Form 10-K: Global Banking & Markets revenue and attributed equity (dated); Basel III leverage ratio framework (BCBS 2014, 2017): 3% minimum and G-SIB buffer (dated); Federal Reserve stress capital buffer rule (2020) and the latest results (dated); Federal Reserve G-SIB surcharge method, 12 CFR 217 subpart H (dated); US enhanced supplementary leverage ratio changes (2025) (dated); a European bank's return on tangible equity by division (annual report) (dated).
- **Data.** data/desk/bank_segments.csv (derived); synthetic desks.
