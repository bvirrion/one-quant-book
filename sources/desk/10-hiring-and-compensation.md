# 10. Hiring and Compensation — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A trader paid fifteen per cent of a twenty-million-dollar year and a trader paid nothing after a two-million-dollar loss on the next desk may have exactly the same skill: one year of P&L measures luck at least as much as ability. A pay rule decides which of the two the firm is buying.
- **Sections.** The pipeline and the interview; Pay structures: base, bonus and deferral; Sizing a bonus pool; Formulaic against discretionary: paying for skill, not luck; Deferral, clawback and retention.
- **Defines.** structured interview, bonus pool, formulaic payout, discretionary bonus, deferred compensation, vesting schedule, malus, clawback, deferral buyout, material risk taker.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** Sharpe ratio (B4.11), hit rate (B7.22), Shapley value (B12.6), performance fee (B1.3), franchise (B1.2), compensation ratio (ch1), payout rate (ch3), netting risk (ch3), credit attribution (ch9), capital charge (ch7).
- **Tutorial.** Twenty traders over five years, each with a true Sharpe ratio drawn from a prior and yearly P&L drawn around it: pay them by a formulaic payout of P&L, by a payout of risk-adjusted P&L, and by a discretionary rule that shrinks each year's P&L towards the desk average; measure the correlation of five-year pay with true skill and the share of pay that rewards luck; then value each trader's unvested deferred pay as a retention tie. End state: a chart of pay against true skill under the three rules, and the unvested balance by seniority.
- **Build.** `firm.bonuspool`: pool sizing (a share of revenue, or of net profit after costs and a capital charge), allocation (formulaic, risk-adjusted, discretionary shrinkage), deferral schedules with vesting, malus and clawback events, the unvested balance and the buyout a competitor must pay, and the luck share of pay; Python; uses firm.projsel's attribution.
- **Weekend problem.** Paid for luck -- named result: the share of a one-year formulaic payout that rewards luck rather than skill at a given dispersion of true Sharpe ratios, and the number of years of P&L after which pay tracks skill with a correlation of 0.8.
- **Facts to verify.** CRD V remuneration rules (Directive 2013/36/EU as amended): variable-pay ratio, deferral, instruments, malus and clawback (dated); UK removal of the bonus cap (PRA/FCA, October 2023) and later deferral changes (dated); EBA report on high earners (latest year) (dated); SEC Rule 10D-1 clawback (2022) and the reproposed incentive-compensation rule under Dodd-Frank s.956 (2024) (dated); Schmidt and Hunter 1998 (Psych. Bulletin) and Sackett et al. 2022 (J. Applied Psych.): validity of structured interviews; a listed firm's remuneration report: deferral and vesting of variable pay (Man Group or a bank) (dated).
- **Data.** Synthetic; EBA high-earner figures in a dated box.
