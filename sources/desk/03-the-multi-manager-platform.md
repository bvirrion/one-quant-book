# 3. The Multi-Manager Platform — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A multi-manager platform's regulatory brochure lists, among the expenses its funds bear, the pay of the portfolio managers who run its teams: the investor pays each team's salaries and bonuses directly, and pays a winning team's bonus even in a year when the fund as a whole loses money.
- **Sections.** Pods, the centre and the platform's promise; Pass-through fees and the investor's bill; Paying winners: the netting problem; Capital allocation and drawdown rules; Centre books and overlays.
- **Defines.** pass-through fee, payout rate, netting risk, centre book, factor overlay.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** multi-manager platform (B1.1), hedge fund (B1.1), pod (B8.28), multi-strategy book (B8.28), strategy correlation (B8.28), management fee (B1.3), performance fee (B1.3), high-water mark (B1.3), drawdown limit (B8.28), risk budgeting (B7.26), crowding (B7.28), prime broker (B1.6), leverage (B1.6), compensation ratio (ch1).
- **Tutorial.** On firm.multistrat's synthetic pods, compute the investor's bill two ways -- a classic management and performance fee, and pass-through costs plus a payout of each pod's own profit -- and measure netting risk as the payouts on winning pods in years the fund lost; then add a factor overlay that hedges the pods' summed factor exposure and a two-step drawdown ladder. End state: a waterfall from gross pod P&L to the investor's net return, and netting risk against the number of pods and their correlation.
- **Build.** `firm.podshop`: platform economics on firm.multistrat's pods: payout rules (rate, per-pod high-water mark, netting across pods or not), a pass-through cost ledger, the investor's net return, a two-step drawdown ladder, capital reallocation after cuts, and a centre book that trades the pods' aggregate factor exposure; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Paid for the winners -- named result: the expected payout on n independent pods of Sharpe ratio S against the payout on their sum (closed form), and the pass-through cost as a share of gross profit for fifty pods.
- **Facts to verify.** SEC Form ADV Part 2A brochures of two multi-manager advisers: expense pass-through language (IAPD) (dated); press reports of pass-through expense levels at multi-manager funds (FT, Bloomberg, WSJ) as context (dated); any published drawdown rule at a platform (only with a citable source; otherwise EXCLUDED and stated generically); published data on platform size, pod counts and team turnover (press, industry reports) (dated); Grossman and Zhou 1993 (pointer to Book 8 ch. 28).
- **Data.** Synthetic (firm.multistrat).
