# 15. Finance, Product Control and Tax — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** For three years Denmark's tax authority paid out billions of kroner of refunds of dividend tax, claimed by pension plans and traders on shares they had never owned; a Danish court convicted the scheme's organiser in 2024, and London's High Court heard the tax authority's civil claim against the traders. A tax rule shapes which trades exist; a dividend trade whose profit is a tax refund is a legal question before it is a trading question.
- **Sections.** The daily P&L: flash, final and sign-off; Valuation control in a trading firm; Taxes that shape strategies; Legal entities and transfer pricing.
- **Defines.** flash P\&L, P\&L sign-off, flash-to-final walk, tax drag, cum-ex trading, wash-sale rule, transfer pricing.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** product control (B6.27), independent price verification (B6.27), prudent valuation (B6.27), unexplained P\&L (B6.27), P\&L attribution (B1.7), mark-to-market (B1.7), realised P\&L (B1.7), unrealised P\&L (B1.7), withholding tax (B1.17), securities transaction tax (B1.12), stamp duty (B1.12), total return swap (B1.6), securities lending (B1.6).
- **Tutorial.** A day's flash P&L against the final: explain the difference by category (late trades, prices moved after the flash, fees, valuation adjustments) and flag sign-off exceptions; then compute the tax drag of three stylised strategies under parameterised regimes -- a transaction tax on turnover, withholding on dividends received, a split long- and short-term treatment of gains -- with every rate an input and the dated box holding current values. End state: the flash-to-final walk table, and a chart of after-tax against pre-tax return by turnover.
- **Build.** `firm.signoff`: flash and final P&L from firm.pnl, the walk between them, sign-off thresholds and exceptions with an audit trail, and a tax-drag calculator whose rates and rules are inputs (transaction taxes on turnover, withholding on dividend flows, holding-period rules); Python; uses firm.pnl and firm.pnlexplain.
- **Weekend problem.** The turnover that tax takes -- named result: the annual turnover at which a transaction tax of t basis points removes half a strategy's net return, and the flash-to-final difference that should stop a sign-off.
- **Facts to verify.** Danish court judgment convicting the organiser of the dividend-refund scheme (Glostrup, December 2024) and the UK High Court judgment in Skatteforvaltningen v Solo Capital Partners (2025) (dated); German Federal Court of Justice cum-ex ruling, 28 July 2021, 1 StR 519/20 (pointer to Book 1 ch. 16); US Internal Revenue Code s.1256 (60/40) and s.1091 (wash sales) (dated); French and Italian financial transaction taxes, UK stamp duty reserve tax rates (dated); OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines (2022); Book 6 ch. 27 product control and IPV (pointer).
- **Data.** Synthetic.
