# 18. Legal Documentation — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** When a large dealer went bankrupt in September 2008, one of its counterparties stopped paying it under their swap and simply waited, relying on a clause of the standard master agreement. A year later a New York bankruptcy court told it that it had waited too long. A few lines of a standard document decided who owed what to whom.
- **Sections.** The master agreement and why it exists; Events of default, termination events and close-out; Collateral annexes; Prime-brokerage and clearing agreements; Exchange memberships and give-ups.
- **Defines.** event of default, termination event, additional termination event, NAV trigger, cross-default clause, flawed-asset clause, prime-brokerage agreement.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** ISDA master agreement (B2.28), Global Master Repurchase Agreement (B2.28), credit support annex (B2.10), close-out netting (B6.17), close-out amount (B6.18), collateral threshold (B6.17), minimum transfer amount (B6.17), independent amount (B6.17), netting set (B6.17), uncleared margin rules (B2.10), give-up (B1.30), give-up line (B2.27), exchange membership (B1.30), rehypothecation (B1.6), prime broker (B1.6), margin lock-up (ch14), house margin (ch14).
- **Tutorial.** A fund with two prime brokers and three swap counterparties: load their terms, run a year of NAV and exposures, compute each day's collateral calls under each annex and the first day any NAV trigger trips, then close out one counterparty with set-off. End state: a timeline chart of NAV against the trigger levels, and a table of calls and the close-out amount.
- **Build.** No honest build: the subject is legal documentation. Analytical tool instead: `firm.docterms`, agreement terms as data (thresholds, minimum transfer amounts, rounding, independent amounts, eligible collateral and haircuts, NAV triggers over one, three and twelve months, notice periods), a daily margin-call calculator for a collateral annex, a NAV-trigger monitor, and a close-out amount with set-off across agreements; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The trigger -- named result: the first day a declining NAV trips an additional termination event under each counterparty's terms, and the close-out amount after set-off.
- **Facts to verify.** ISDA 2002 Master Agreement: structure of ss.2(a)(iii), 5, 6 (ISDA public summaries); In re Lehman Brothers Holdings Inc. (Metavante), Bankr. S.D.N.Y., September 2009; Lomas v JFB Firth Rixson [2012] EWCA Civ 419; Lehman estate: number of derivative contracts at the bankruptcy (court or estate document); ISDA 2016 Credit Support Annex for variation margin (public description) (dated); ISDA 2018 US Resolution Stay Protocol and the US stay rules (dated); CFTC Part 22 (LSOC) customer protection for cleared swaps (dated).
- **Data.** Synthetic.
