# 11. Intellectual Property and Non-Competes — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In 2009 a programmer left a large bank's high-frequency trading group for a start-up, having uploaded part of the bank's source code to a server abroad. He was convicted in 2010; in 2012 an appeals court reversed the conviction because, it held, the code did not fall within the federal statute as then written. Congress amended the statute within the year.
- **Sections.** What a trading firm owns: code, models, data and know-how; Trade secrets and how they are protected; Non-competes, garden leave and non-solicitation; The public litigation record.
- **Defines.** trade secret, confidentiality agreement, invention assignment clause, non-compete clause, garden leave, non-solicitation clause.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** signal half-life (B7.13), post-publication decay (B7.13), research log (B7.1), segregation of duties (B6.28), crowding (B7.28), deferred compensation (ch10), vesting schedule (ch10).
- **Tutorial.** Estimate how fast a strategy's edge decays after a key researcher leaves under three assumptions about what they carry (the model's structure, its parameters, its data), with Book 7's decay tooling on a synthetic strategy; price garden leave of three, six and twelve months against the edge protected. End state: a chart of protected value against garden-leave length, with the cost line.
- **Build.** No honest build: the chapter's subject is legal. Analytical tool instead: `firm.gardenleave`, the value to the old employer of keeping a departing person out of the market, as a decaying asset (the information's half-life from Book 7's decay estimates) against the cost of paying garden leave, and the expected leak cost against the cost of enforcement; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The six-month question -- named result: the garden-leave length at which the marginal edge protected equals the marginal salary cost, for a strategy with a given half-life.
- **Facts to verify.** United States v. Aleynikov, 676 F.3d 71 (2d Cir. 2012); Theft of Trade Secrets Clarification Act of 2012; Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, 18 U.S.C. s.1836 (dated); Directive (EU) 2016/943 and the UK Trade Secrets (Enforcement, etc.) Regulations 2018 (dated); FTC Non-Compete Clause Rule (2024), Ryan LLC v. FTC (N.D. Tex. 2024) and the FTC's 2025 withdrawal of its appeal (dated); UK government's announced three-month limit on non-competes (2023) and its status (dated); Jane Street Group v. Millennium Management et al. (S.D.N.Y. 2024): complaint and settlement (docket); Tullett Prebon v BGC Brokers [2011] EWCA Civ 131 (garden leave, poaching).
- **Data.** Synthetic.
