# 29. Case Studies II — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 1 August 2012 a market maker lost more than 460 million dollars in forty-five minutes. Within a week it had sold 400 million dollars of convertible securities to a group of investors, most of its old shareholders' stake gone with them; within a year it had merged with a high-frequency trading firm. The controls failed in the morning; the firm-level decisions came in the next five days.
- **Sections.** 2012: a software incident and a rescue; 2021: a family office and its prime brokers; 2022: an exchange that was also a counterparty; 2022: the nickel squeeze and the exchange's decision; What the four share.
- **Defines.** dynamic margining, exit race.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** loss limit (B11.27), kill switch (B11.27), market access rule (B11.27), total return swap (B1.6), prime broker (B1.6), margin call (B1.6), centralised exchange (B3.15), customer asset segregation (B3.15), proof of reserves (B3.15), hot wallet (B3.24), margin spiral (B3.28), default waterfall (B1.5), house margin (ch14), NAV trigger (ch18), additional termination event (ch18), crisis management plan (ch27), trapped assets (ch27), days to liquidate (ch28), funding liquidity risk (ch28).
- **Tutorial.** Extend the casebook: the 2012 rescue (loss against equity, dilution of the old shareholders); the 2021 exit race (several prime brokers holding the same concentrated swaps sell in turn into a falling market; each one's loss by exit order and margin level, calibrated to the public figures); the 2022 exchange's customer shortfall (Book 3's data); and the nickel margin call against a short's available liquidity. End state: the exit-race chart (loss by prime broker against exit order) and the rescue-dilution table.
- **Build.** `firm.casebook` (part 2): the four cases' derived tables, a rescue-dilution calculator, the exit-race model (sequential liquidation with impact), and the nickel margin-call calculation; Python; uses firm.impactfit or firm.capacity for impact.
- **Weekend problem.** The last to sell -- named result: each prime broker's loss as a function of its place in the exit race and of its margin, calibrated to the public figures, and the dilution of the 2012 rescue.
- **Facts to verify.** SEC order In the Matter of Knight Capital Americas LLC, 34-70694 (pointer to Book 11 ch. 27 ledger); Knight Capital Group Form 8-K of 6 August 2012: the securities purchase agreement; KCG Holdings merger completion, July 2013 (Form 8-K); Credit Suisse Special Committee report on Archegos (29 July 2021) (pointer to Book 1 ch. 6 ledger); Federal Reserve and PRA penalties on Credit Suisse over Archegos (July 2023); SEC v. Hwang et al. (2022) and the 2024 criminal verdict (dated); FTX: declaration of John J. Ray III (17 November 2022) and the debtors' shortfall analysis (pointer to Book 3 ch. 15 ledger); FCA final notice to the London Metal Exchange (2025); R (Elliott Associates and Jane Street) v London Metal Exchange [2023] EWHC 2969 (Admin); the independent review of the LME nickel events (2023).
- **Data.** data/desk/case_knight.csv, case_archegos.csv, case_nickel.csv (derived).
