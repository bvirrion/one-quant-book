## Set-up (steps 1–3, 2026-11-11)

- Entry files `one_quant_book_{06..10}_*_fr.tex`, built from the English entries with Book 5's French pattern
  (`\booklang`, French `\bookline`/`\bookhead`/`\title`, `colophon.fr`, `preface.fr`). Book 8's line is *Stratégies I :
  actions et contrats à terme*: Book 1 fixed *futures* → *contrats à terme* in prose.
- `styles/lang/fr.tex`: the 24 part titles of Books 6–10 (an undefined `omstr@` key prints nothing, silently).
- `tools/termlink/books.py` `LANGS` (Books 1–10), `latexmkrc`, the release body of `.github/workflows/ci.yml`.
  `tools/check_translation.sh` already lists every slug.
- Term configs `tools/term_config/book{6..10}_fr.py`, generated by `draft/pilot-quant-fr/mkconfig.py`: the English
  config's STOP and DROP mapped through the run glossary (Book 2's lesson), its structural masks copied, plus
  *points de base*.
- Glossaries `sources/GLOSSARY_BOOK_{6..10}_FR.md` (845 lines written by the coordinator; 851 defined terms, every one
  covered; only 8 had a Books 1–5 line already). Run glossaries `GLOSSARY_BOOK_N_FR.run.md` generated by
  `draft/pilot-quant-fr/chain.py`: the book's own lines, then every earlier book's lines as `00` (Book 10's run file
  carries 2,127 inherited lines). `glossary.py split` run for all five.
- Shared sweep scripts in `draft/pilot-quant-fr/`, taken from Book 5's final `work/` and parameterised by slug, so no
  book rediscovers a defect class: `sweep_report.py` (line counts, keys, headings, elision, doubled words, stranded
  articles, `\($`, bare-noun definitions, `\cref` articles, decimal commas, high punctuation), `typo_apply.py`
  (non-breaking space before `: ; ? !`, `~\%`), `crefart_apply.py`, `dollars_apply.py`, `lens_report.py`,
  `shift_report.py`, and the new `extra_report.py` (series name in prose, English months and ordinals in figures,
  `\textsuperscript`, *le frais*, fixed headings left in English).
- Step 4: `draft/pilot-quant-fr/queue.sh`, at most three `run.py --workers 3` at once (Books 6–8, then 9 and 10),
  up to five passes per book, waiting on PIDs (`wait -n`); log `draft/pilot-quant-fr/queue.log`.

## Findings

- **Book 6** (`sources/TRANSLATION_BOOK_6_FR.md`): 88, 343 pp, 313 calls, $2.28. The sample found *numéraire* for cash
  collateral in all of ch. 2 (fixed: *espèces*); fifteen figures printed English tick labels from symbolic coordinates.
- **Book 7** (`sources/TRANSLATION_BOOK_7_FR.md`): 88, 370 pp, 280 calls, $2.39. Tables overflow from French headers
  (18), ten figure collisions; `tools/figcrop.py` reads only `build/`, so `draft/pilot-quant-fr/figcrop_pdf.py` takes
  the PDF from `OQB_PDF`; `overfull.py` unwraps the 79-column log before naming files.
- **Book 8** (`sources/TRANSLATION_BOOK_8_FR.md`): 88, 356 pp, 302 calls, $2.14. Two defect classes no earlier sweep
  caught, both then run back over Books 6 and 7 (rebuilt, still 0/0/0): English roles in `\iqroles{}` (74 lines in
  all; `iqroles_fr.py`) and definitions opening on a *lowercase* bare noun (21; `bare_lower_fix.py`).
- **Book 9** (`sources/TRANSLATION_BOOK_9_FR.md`): 88, 356 pp, 243 calls, $1.68. Weekend-problem labels came back in six
  French forms in every book (Books 1–5 too); Books 6–9 now use one form (`wkend_labels.py`; Books 1–5 left as they are).
  English quotes in French prose (`quotes_fr.py`, also Books 7–8). `fix_common.py` read `xtick={1,2,3,4,5}` as decimal
  commas (fixed). `numfile.py` compares numbers per file, since the table fixes shift lines.
- **Book 10** (`sources/TRANSLATION_BOOK_10_FR.md`): 88, 327 pp, 359 calls, $2.42, the most repairs of the five. The mid
  price came back as *point médian* / *cours médian* in eleven files (now *prix milieu*). `\index{}` keys split across a
  line end broke the gate (`index_join.py`).
