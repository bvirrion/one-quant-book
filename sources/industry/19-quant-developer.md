# 19. Quant Developer — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In the labour-condition filings, positions titled quant developer are filed under at least three occupation codes -- software developers, financial quantitative analysts and others -- with different wage ranges. Three different jobs share the title: the researcher's engineer, the desk's library developer and the trading-system developer.
- **Sections.** The researcher's engineer; The desk's library developer; The trading-system developer; What each ships and how it is judged; Moving between the three.
- **Defines.** research engineer, library developer, trading-system developer.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** quant developer (B16.21), embedded team (B16.21), central team (B16.21), machine-learning engineer (B12.28), tick-to-trade latency (B13.1), latency budget (B13.1), library quant (ch18), labor condition application (ch14), prevailing wage (ch14), wage level (ch14), Standard Occupational Classification (ch14), small-cell suppression (ch14).
- **Tutorial.** The role's pay evidence: LCA base-salary ranges (10th-90th percentile, median) for the role family by employer type and wage level from data/industry/lca_ranges.csv through firm.paydata, cells under ten filings suppressed, beside the OEWS range for the matching occupation in the securities industry. Then the title study with firm.roles: normalise job titles, cross-tabulate the quant-developer family against SOC codes, and compare wage ranges by SOC group with bootstrap intervals. End state: a stacked chart of SOC codes within the title and the range gap.
- **Build.** `firm.roles` (increment): a job-title normaliser (patterns, seniority prefixes, abbreviations), the title-by-SOC cross-tabulation, and the three quant-developer role cards; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** One title, three jobs -- named result: the SOC mix of quant-developer filings and the difference in median offered base between the software-developer and the quantitative-analyst codes, with its bootstrap interval.
- **Facts to verify.** SOC 2018: 15-1252, 13-2099, 15-2041, 15-2051 definitions; O*NET 13-2099.01; Book 16 ch. 21's definition of the quant developer (pointer).
- **Data.** data/industry/lca_titles.csv (derived).
