# 22. Portfolio Manager and Pod Analyst — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A portfolio manager at a platform is paid a share of the team's P&L after costs and loses the job at a set drawdown: the deal is a call option on one's own skill with a knock-out. Its value depends on the Sharpe ratio the manager actually has, which neither side knows at the start.
- **Sections.** The deal: capital, payout, limits and costs; The deal as an option with a knock-out; The analyst: from research to running capital; Track records and moving; Portfolio managers outside the platforms.
- **Defines.** portfolio manager, pod analyst, sub-portfolio manager.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** pod (B8.28), drawdown limit (B8.28), payout rate (B16.3), pass-through fee (B16.3), de-risking ladder (B16.8), track record portability (B16.25), emerging manager (B16.25), formulaic payout (B16.10), Sharpe ratio (B4.11), team tenure (ch5), total compensation (ch13).
- **Tutorial.** The role's pay evidence: LCA base-salary ranges (10th-90th percentile, median) for the role family by employer type and wage level from data/industry/lca_ranges.csv through firm.paydata, cells under ten filings suppressed, beside the OEWS range for the matching occupation in the securities industry. Then the deal with firm.roles (wrapping firm.podshop and firm.payoffer): payout of a stated range of P&L, a two-step drawdown ladder and capital as parameters; for true Sharpe ratios from 0 to 2, the distribution of three-year pay and the probability of being stopped out. End state: expected pay and stop-out probability against Sharpe ratio.
- **Build.** `firm.roles` (increment): the portfolio-manager and analyst role cards and the PM-deal model (payout, deficits carried forward, ladder, capital changes after cuts); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The deal -- named result: the true Sharpe ratio at which the manager's expected three-year pay exceeds a stated salaried alternative, and the stop-out probability there.
- **Facts to verify.** press ranges of platform payout percentages and drawdown rules (dated; context only; else EXCLUDED); Form ADV counts of advisory employees at platforms (ch. 5); pointer to Book 16 ch. 3 and 25.
- **Data.** Synthetic, parameters labelled; LCA ranges for analyst titles.
