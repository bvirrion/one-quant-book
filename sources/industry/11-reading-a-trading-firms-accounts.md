# 11. Reading a Trading Firm's Accounts — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** For nothing but a download, the UK register gives ten years of a trading firm's accounts, many of them machine-readable: turnover, operating profit, average number of employees, staff costs, and for a partnership the profit shared among its members. Staff costs over average employees is the most honest pay figure in the industry -- and it still hides the partners.
- **Sections.** Where the filings are: registries, EDGAR, annual reports, prospectuses, divisional and Pillar 3 reports; What statutory accounts contain; Machine-readable filings: inline XBRL and XBRL; Pitfalls: entity against group, members against employees, currencies and one-offs; Per-head measures and their caveats.
- **Defines.** company registry, statutory accounts, consolidated accounts, inline XBRL, staff costs, average headcount, members' remuneration, FOCUS report, Pillar 3 disclosure.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** net trading revenue (B16.1), compensation ratio (B16.1), operating margin (B16.1), non-GAAP measure (B16.1), return on equity (B16.1), limited liability partnership (B16.2), members' capital (B16.2), transfer pricing (B16.15), broker-dealer (B1.2), net capital rule (B16.2), firm profile (ch2).
- **Tutorial.** Pull five years of one firm's filings: a committed snapshot (facts extracted from the firm's Companies House iXBRL accounts and, for a US broker-dealer, its X-17A-5 statement of financial condition, each fact with filing id, URL and page) read with firm.filings; reconcile turnover, operating profit, staff costs and average headcount; compute staff cost per head and, for an LLP, members' remuneration per member. A fetch script re-pulls the originals into the scratchpad (never committed); tests run offline on the snapshot and on a synthetic iXBRL fixture. End state: a five-year table and a chart of revenue and staff cost per head.
- **Build.** `firm.filings`: an inline-XBRL facts parser (standard library only: contexts, units, scale, sign and format attributes), an EDGAR company-facts JSON reader, a Snapshot of facts with provenance, and a mapping to firm.firmecon's Statement lines; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Five years at Companies House -- named result: the firm's staff cost per head and revenue per head as ranges over five years, and the share of profit before members' remuneration paid to members (for an LLP) or distributed (for a company).
- **Facts to verify.** Companies Act 2006 s. 411 (staff numbers and costs) and the LLP (Accounts and Audit) Regulations 2008 with the LLP SORP on members' remuneration; Companies House: iXBRL filing share, free document download, the Accounts Data Product and its licence (dated); SEC EDGAR fair-access policy (user agent, rate limit) and company-facts API; SEC Rule 17a-5: public parts of the annual report (statement of financial condition) (dated); CRR Part Eight, Article 450 (remuneration disclosure) and EBA ITS templates REM1-REM5 (dated); ESMA European Single Electronic Format (ESEF) requirement; Rule 144A offering memoranda are not public: what the press reports of them is context only; the chosen firm's filings, five years (dated).
- **Data.** data/industry/filings_snapshot/*.csv (extracted facts with provenance); tests/fixtures synthetic iXBRL.

## EXCLUDED

- Rule 144A offering memoranda: not public; press reports of their contents are not used.
- LLP SORP (CCAB) text: not fetched (the SORP is sold); the chapter states members' remuneration from the LLP regulations only.
- The highest-paid-director line of Quadrature's notes and directors' remuneration lines of all filings: dropped at extraction (privacy rule).
- Individual REM4 bands with fewer than ten people (from EUR 3.5m up): not printed; totals only.
- X-17A-5 statements of US broker-dealers: chapter 2 already reads one (Citadel Securities LLC, chapter 2 F13); not repeated.
- XTX Markets Limited accounts for 2025: read, not used (a UK service entity of a larger group; the pitfall is shown with Jane Street's entities).
