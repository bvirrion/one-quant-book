# 14. Pay Levels by Role, Firm Type and Seniority — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Every public source of pay sees one slice. US visa filings see offered base salaries for sponsored positions, and nothing of bonuses; employer surveys by the government cap what they report; filed accounts see total staff cost per head, bonuses included, but average away the roles; high-earner reports count only the people above a million euros. A range worth printing is built from several slices, each with its caveat.
- **Sections.** What each source measures; Building ranges from labour-condition filings; Ranges by role, firm type and seniority; From base to total: what staff cost per head says about bonuses; The tail: high-earner counts and risk-taker disclosures.
- **Defines.** labor condition application, prevailing wage, wage level, Standard Occupational Classification, top-coding, small-cell suppression, high earner, median-employee pay.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** bonus pool (B16.10), formulaic payout (B16.10), discretionary bonus (B16.10), material risk taker (B16.10), bootstrap (B4.13), survivorship bias (B7.3), staff costs (ch11), average headcount (ch11), Pillar 3 disclosure (ch11), pay band (ch9), base salary (ch13), total compensation (ch13), employer taxonomy (ch1).
- **Tutorial.** The LCA pipeline end to end with firm.paydata, on the committed derived tables and a small synthetic fixture of raw rows: classify employers (firm.industrymap plus the sourced employer list) and roles (SOC code plus title patterns), annualise offered wages, take wage level as the seniority proxy, compute percentiles with bootstrap intervals, suppress cells under ten; then join OEWS ranges, the US pay-ratio rule's median-employee pay of listed firms, staff cost per head from filings and EBA high-earner counts. End state: the chapter's range table and a chart of base ranges by role and firm type.
- **Build.** `firm.paydata`: streaming LCA reader (openpyxl read_only, chunked; raw files never committed), classifiers, annualisation, percentile ranges with suppression, and a PayEvidence record (source, measure, population, caveat) that every other source is converted into; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Four sources, one number -- named result: the implied ratio of total pay to base, staff cost per head over LCA median base for the same firm type, as a range with the caveats that bound it.
- **Facts to verify.** DOL OFLC LCA disclosure data FY2021-FY2026: files, record layout, field definitions, the statement that determinations may change on appeal (dated); 20 CFR 655 Subpart H and Form ETA-9035 (the attestation); OFLC prevailing-wage guidance: four wage levels (dated); SOC 2018 manual and O*NET 13-2099.01 Financial Quantitative Analysts; BLS OEWS May 2025 national industry-specific estimates for NAICS 5231/5239/5221 and the wage top-code (dated); SEC pay-ratio rule, Item 402(u) of Regulation S-K: median-employee pay disclosed by listed firms (firm-level only) (dated); EBA high-earners report on 2024 data (April 2026): counts by country and business area (dated); banks' Pillar 3 REM tables (UK and EU): material risk takers, fixed and variable totals by business area (dated); NY Comptroller average securities-industry pay (dated); US federal works are public domain (17 U.S.C. 105).
- **Data.** data/industry/lca_ranges.csv, lca_titles.csv, oews_finance.csv, pay_ratio_medians.csv, eba_high_earners.csv, pillar3_rem.csv (all derived); LICENSES.md.
