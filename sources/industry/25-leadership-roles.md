# 25. Leadership Roles — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Under the UK senior managers regime each firm must name the person responsible for each prescribed function and give them a written statement of their responsibilities; the regulator can hold that person to account for a failure in their area. At the top of a trading firm, a job title is also a legal accountability.
- **Sections.** Head of desk; Chief risk, technology and operating officers; Partner; Chief executive and founder; Accountability regimes.
- **Defines.** head of desk, senior manager function, statement of responsibilities.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** chief risk officer (B16.12), revenue budget (B16.6), risk appetite (B16.6), limited liability partnership (B16.2), members' capital (B16.2), retention ratio (B16.2), partnership share (ch13), members' remuneration (ch11).
- **Tutorial.** Making partner with firm.roles (wrapping firm.partnership): a new partner's points, capital contribution, profit share and dilution when later partners are admitted, over five years under the stated retention rule; and, as data, the number of senior-manager functions a firm of each type must allocate. End state: a chart of the new partner's income and capital at risk by year.
- **Build.** `firm.roles` (increment): the leadership role cards, the accountability map (functions to roles) and the partner-economics analysis; Python; wraps firm.partnership.
- **Weekend problem.** Making partner -- named result: the new partner's expected five-year income and capital at risk under the chapter's points and retention rules, and the break-even share of profit against staying a salaried head of desk.
- **Facts to verify.** FCA/PRA SM&CR senior manager functions and statements of responsibilities (dated); FINRA principal registrations (Series 24) (dated); EBA/ESMA joint guidelines on suitability of management body members (dated); UK LLP Act 2000 (pointer Book 16 ch. 2).
- **Data.** Illustrative parameters (labelled).
