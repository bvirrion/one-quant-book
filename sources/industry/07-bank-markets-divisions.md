# 7. Bank Markets Divisions — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Banks report their markets revenue in two lines, fixed income, currencies and commodities against equities, and the split differs across banks by more than their size does: in the same year some earn more than half their markets revenue from equities, most from rates and credit. The mix is the product strength, and it decides which quant jobs the bank has.
- **Sections.** What banks publish about their markets businesses; American banks; The French banks and equity derivatives; Other European and Asian banks; League tables: who compiles them and what they measure; Quant headcounts: what is and is not public.
- **Defines.** FICC, league table, revenue pool.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** sell side (B1.2), buy side (B1.3), broker-dealer (B1.2), franchise (B1.2), flow trading (B1.2), prime services (B1.2), risk-weighted assets (B1.2), structured product (B5.19), allocated equity (B16.5), binding capital constraint (B16.5), quantitative investment strategy (B9.27), franchise P\&L (B9.24), central risk book (B9.25), XVA desk (B6.20).
- **Tutorial.** A derived table of markets revenue by bank and year, 2019-2025, from filings (US 10-Ks, European annual reports and results) for about twelve banks, converted at annual average FX rates with sources: firm.bankmix computes each bank's equities share, the dispersion of mixes, their stability across years and their sensitivity to the VIX yearly mean. Banks are ordered by equities share, not by size. End state: a stacked chart of FICC and equities by bank and a table of shares.
- **Build.** `firm.bankmix`: bank segment revenue mapping (each bank's reported lines to FICC and equities, with the filing's own definitions noted), currency conversion from sourced annual averages, mix metrics and their year-to-year stability; Python; reads Book 16's data/desk/vix_annual.csv.
- **Weekend problem.** Where the equity derivatives are -- named result: each bank's equities share of markets revenue as a range over 2021-2025, and the share of the group's equities revenue earned by the three most equity-heavy banks.
- **Facts to verify.** markets revenue lines 2019-2025 of JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Citigroup, Bank of America (Forms 10-K) (dated); BNP Paribas Global Markets (FICC, equity and prime services) and Societe Generale Global Markets (equities, fixed income and currencies) from results and universal registration documents (dated); Barclays, Deutsche Bank, UBS, HSBC, Nomura markets lines (annual reports) (dated); a source for the French banks' equity-derivatives specialisation (AMF or Banque de France publications on structured products; academic studies) (dated); Coalition Greenwich index press releases: what it measures and who it covers (publisher and date only); Euromoney FX survey description (publisher and date only); ECB/Fed H.10 annual average exchange rates.
- **Data.** data/industry/bank_markets_revenue.csv (derived, filing and page per row).
