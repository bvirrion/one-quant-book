# 4. Quantitative Hedge Funds and Systematic Managers — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** The most secretive funds in the business still file one public form: every registered US adviser states in Form ADV its regulatory assets under management, its number of employees and how many of them do investment advisory work, and the gross assets of each private fund it runs. Read side by side, the forms make managers that publish nothing else comparable.
- **Sections.** Three families: research-driven multi-strategy funds, trend followers, statistical-arbitrage houses; What Form ADV and the private-fund statistics reveal; Assets, gross assets and concentration; Secrecy and publication: what these firms put in the public domain.
- **Defines.** systematic manager, discretionary manager, regulatory assets under management, exempt reporting adviser.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** hedge fund (B1.1), assets under management (B1.1), commodity trading advisor (B1.3), trend following (B8.19), statistical arbitrage (B8.1), master--feeder structure (B16.4), general partner (B16.4), Herfindahl--Hirschman index (B16.30), market concentration (B16.30), leverage (B1.6), industry classification code (ch1).
- **Tutorial.** Stream a committed extract of the SEC's monthly Form ADV bulk file with firm.formadv: select advisers with private funds reported as hedge funds, compute RAUM per employee and per advisory employee, gross private-fund assets over RAUM, and the concentration of hedge-fund RAUM (top-10 share, Herfindahl--Hirschman index); highlight a sourced list of systematic managers. End state: a log-log scatter of RAUM against employees and the concentration table.
- **Build.** `firm.formadv`: a reader for the SEC's Form ADV bulk files (Item 5.A employees, 5.B advisory employees, 5.F RAUM, Schedule D 7.B.1 private-fund gross assets and fund type), streamed from the zip, with per-adviser metrics and concentration measures (wraps firm.moats' HHI if its interface fits, otherwise its own); Python; reused in ch. 5, 8 and 12.
- **Weekend problem.** Assets per head -- named result: the interquartile range of RAUM per employee for hedge-fund advisers, the same for the sourced systematic managers, and the top-ten share and HHI of hedge-fund RAUM.
- **Facts to verify.** Form ADV Part 1A instructions: Items 5.A, 5.B, 5.F, Schedule D 7.B.1 (SEC); SEC FOIA bulk data: Information about registered investment advisers and exempt reporting advisers (monthly files; format, size); SEC Private Funds Statistics (latest quarter): qualifying hedge funds, gross and net assets (dated); Form ADV of named systematic managers: Renaissance Technologies, Two Sigma, D. E. Shaw, AQR, Man Group's US adviser, Winton, CFM, Squarepoint, Qube (dated; each only with its ADV row); the firms' published research (AQR papers, Man Institute, CFM arXiv papers) as evidence of what they publish; Form ADV glossary: regulatory assets under management; exempt reporting adviser (Advisers Act 203(l), 203(m)).
- **Data.** data/industry/adv_hedge_advisers.csv (derived from the ADV bulk file); data/industry/systematic_managers.csv (sourced list).

- Classification of named firms as trend followers or statistical-arbitrage houses: no firm's own page uses those words for itself; the three families are described generically.
