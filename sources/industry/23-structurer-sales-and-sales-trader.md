# 23. Structurer, Sales and Sales-Trader — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** The European trade association of structured-products issuers publishes each quarter what its members sold -- billions of euros of notes a year, most of them sold to individuals through banks' networks. Behind each note is a structurer who designed it, a salesperson who placed it, and a margin both are paid from.
- **Sections.** The structurer; Institutional sales and coverage; The sales-trader; Quantitative skills in client-facing roles; The rules for client-facing work.
- **Defines.** structurer, institutional salesperson, product governance.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** structured product (B5.19), structured note (B5.19), structuring margin (B5.19), autocallable (B5.18), sales-trader (B1.2), franchise (B1.2), client tiering (B9.24), high-touch trading (B10.20), labor condition application (ch14), prevailing wage (ch14), wage level (ch14).
- **Tutorial.** The role's pay evidence: LCA base-salary ranges (10th-90th percentile, median) for the role family by employer type and wage level from data/industry/lca_ranges.csv through firm.paydata, cells under ten filings suppressed, beside the OEWS range for the matching occupation in the securities industry. Then the note's margin with firm.roles (wrapping firm.autocall): the structuring margin of a stylised autocallable at two rate levels and two volatility levels, and the revenue per structurer implied by a sourced range of issuance volumes. End state: a margin table and the pay-range chart.
- **Build.** `firm.roles` (increment): the client-facing role cards and the structuring-margin analysis; Python; wraps firm.autocall.
- **Weekend problem.** The margin in the note -- named result: the structuring margin of the chapter's autocallable at 1 and 4 per cent rates, and the revenue per structurer for a desk's issuance in the sourced range.
- **Facts to verify.** EUSIPA quarterly market reports: volumes by product type and country (dated); MiFID II art. 16(3) and 24(2) product governance; ESMA guidelines on product governance (2023); ESMA knowledge and competence guidelines; FINRA Series 7 and 63 (dated); pointer Book 5 ch. 18-19.
- **Data.** data/industry/eusipa_volumes.csv (derived).
