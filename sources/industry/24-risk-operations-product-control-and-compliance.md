# 24. Risk, Operations, Product Control and Compliance — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** European remuneration rules require the pay of staff in control functions to be mostly fixed and independent of the businesses they control; the rule shapes the career as much as the pay. The control functions employ many of the industry's quants, and they are a route into it as often as a destination.
- **Sections.** Risk management; Operations; Product control; Compliance; Control functions as careers and as routes to the front office.
- **Defines.** control function, product controller, compliance officer.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** chief risk officer (B16.12), risk committee (B16.12), four-eyes principle (B16.12), front office (B16.13), middle office (B16.13), back office (B16.13), product control (B6.27), independent price verification (B6.27), market abuse (B16.16), operational risk (B6.28), pay band (ch9), labor condition application (ch14), prevailing wage (ch14), wage level (ch14).
- **Tutorial.** The role's pay evidence: LCA base-salary ranges (10th-90th percentile, median) for the role family by employer type and wage level from data/industry/lca_ranges.csv through firm.paydata, cells under ten filings suppressed, beside the OEWS range for the matching occupation in the securities industry. Then the ratio of control-function to front-office medians by employer type with firm.roles, against the fixed-pay emphasis the EBA guidelines require. End state: a chart of the ratio by employer type.
- **Build.** `firm.roles` (increment): the control-function role cards and the cross-family ratio analysis; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The price of independence -- named result: the ratio of control-function to front-office median offered base at banks and at trading firms, with bootstrap intervals.
- **Facts to verify.** EBA Guidelines on sound remuneration policies (EBA/GL/2021/04): control-function pay (dated); FCA SM&CR SMF16 and SMF17 (dated); GARP FRM and other professional certifications (factual descriptions only; dated); pointer Book 16 ch. 12-16.
- **Data.** data/industry/lca_ranges.csv.
