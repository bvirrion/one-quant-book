# 26. Hours, Rhythm and Intensity — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Measured in UTC, the world's futures markets leave only a few hours of the week with nothing open, around Friday night and Saturday; the crypto markets leave none. A desk's hours are set by the markets it trades before any firm decides anything, and on-call is the price of markets that never close.
- **Sections.** Market hours by product, in one time zone; On-call and markets that never close; Working-time rules; What is documented and what is anecdote.
- **Defines.** coverage requirement, working-time opt-out, exempt employee.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** follow-the-sun trading (B3.29), on-call rotation (B15.28), runbook (B15.28), incident commander (B15.28), round-the-clock trading (B16.30), futures contract (B1.18).
- **Tutorial.** Compute a desk's coverage requirement with firm.workload (wrapping firm.marketmap and firm.calendar): the sourced sessions of the markets it trades in UTC, the weekly staffed hours, the fewest shifts that cover them with overlap, the headcount under a 48-hour week, and the on-call nights per engineer for a rota of given size. End state: a weekly UTC heatmap of open markets and the shift table.
- **Build.** `firm.workload`: coverage requirement from a market set, shift and headcount planning under a working-time limit, on-call load per person, and a documented-against-anecdote tag on every input; Python; wraps firm.marketmap and firm.calendar.
- **Weekend problem.** Who watches Sunday night? -- named result: the smallest headcount that covers a futures-and-crypto book around the clock under a 48-hour average week with two people on each shift, and the on-call nights per engineer per month for a rota of eight.
- **Facts to verify.** CME Globex, Eurex, ICE, SGX session hours (exchange pages; dated); Directive 2003/88/EC arts. 6 and 22; UK Working Time Regulations 1998 regs. 4-5 (the opt-out); US FLSA white-collar exemption, 29 CFR 541, and the 2024 salary rule vacated in November 2024 (dated); bank-published policies on junior hours (firm publications or reputable press; dated; only as documented); Book 3 ch. 29's market registry (pointer).
- **Data.** Sessions from firm.marketmap's sourced registry; synthetic rota.

## EXCLUDED

- Surveys of hours worked by quants, traders or engineers at named firms, and press stories of individual schedules: anecdote; the chapter uses none as data and says so.
- Holiday calendars: firm.calendar has none; the chosen week has no exchange holiday in the five markets (checked against the sessions only, not against holiday lists).
- CME E-mini S&P 500 hours: the product page renders by script and its archived copy carries no hours; the Treasury note's page stands for CME's Globex schedule.
