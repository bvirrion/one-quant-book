# 27. Locations — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** The same gross package, after income tax and the median rent of a two-bedroom flat, leaves amounts that differ by a factor of two or more between the industry's cities, and the ordering changes with the size of the package and the share paid as bonus.
- **Sections.** The trading cities and what is there; Pay, tax and housing; Visas and work permits; Moving: relocation, remote work and the cost of leaving.
- **Defines.** specialty-occupation visa, skilled-worker visa, price level index, disposable pay after housing.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** colocation (B14.9), tax residence (ch15), average tax rate (ch15), expatriate tax regime (ch15), labor condition application (ch14), firm profile (ch2).
- **Tutorial.** Compare cities with firm.locations (wrapping firm.aftertax): official rent statistics for ten cities, after-tax pay for a range of packages, disposable pay after housing, and LCA worksite counts by US metro for quantitative titles. End state: a chart of disposable pay after housing against gross pay by city and the LCA worksite table.
- **Build.** `firm.locations`: city records (tax jurisdiction, rent series with source and date, price level index, visa facts by ledger id), disposable-pay comparisons and break-even packages between two cities; Python; wraps firm.aftertax.
- **Weekend problem.** The same offer in five cities -- named result: disposable pay after tax and median two-bedroom rent for the chapter's package in five cities, and the gross package that equalises London and New York.
- **Facts to verify.** official rent statistics: US Census ACS or HUD fair market rents; ONS private rents; CBS; INSEE/OLAP; Singapore URA; Hong Kong RVD (dated); Eurostat price level indices (licence CC BY 4.0); USCIS H-1B cap, weighted selection and any fee changes in force in 2026 (dated); UK Skilled Worker and Global Talent thresholds after July 2025 (dated); EU Blue Card Directive 2021/1883; Netherlands highly skilled migrant thresholds 2026; Singapore Employment Pass COMPASS and salary floor 2025-26; Hong Kong Top Talent Pass; UAE Golden Visa (dated); firms' published office lists (dated); LCA worksite cities (derived).
- **Data.** data/industry/rents.csv, data/industry/lca_worksites.csv (derived).

- EU Blue Card Directive text, Hong Kong Top Talent Pass and UAE Golden Visa: not fetched; the IND's Blue Card threshold stands for the directive's effect in one member state.
