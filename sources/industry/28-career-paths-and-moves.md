# 28. Career Paths and Moves — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A trader who resigns in January may serve three months of notice, spend six more on garden leave and then sit out a non-compete, and lose on the way the deferred pay that has not yet vested: a move is often a year out of the market and a year's bonus left behind, which is why the new employer's buyout is part of the offer.
- **Sections.** Entry routes: internships, graduate programmes, lateral hires, doctorates; Bank to buy side and back; Non-competes and garden leave in practice; Going independent and starting a firm; Exits.
- **Defines.** internship, return offer, graduate programme, lateral hire, notice period.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** non-compete clause (B16.11), garden leave (B16.11), non-solicitation clause (B16.11), confidentiality agreement (B16.11), trade secret (B16.11), deferral buyout (B16.10), deferred compensation (B16.10), emerging manager (B16.25), seed investor (B16.25), track record portability (B16.25), spin-out (ch3), forfeiture (ch13), good-leaver provision (ch13), sign-on bonus (ch13).
- **Tutorial.** Value a move with firm.careerpath (wrapping firm.payoffer and firm.gardenleave): unvested deferrals forfeited, notice, garden leave and non-compete months, the probability and size of a buyout, and the raise needed to make the move worth it; then a career chain whose transition probabilities are labelled assumptions, with the expected pay path and its spread; and the spin-out edges of chapter 3's lineage graph. End state: the break-even raise surface and the pay-path fan.
- **Build.** `firm.careerpath`: move valuation (forfeiture, time out of market, buyout), a Markov career chain with labelled transition assumptions and pay by state, and path statistics; Python; wraps firm.payoffer, firm.gardenleave and firm.lineage.
- **Weekend problem.** Move now or wait for the vest? -- named result: the raise that makes a move neutral as a function of the month of leaving and the buyout probability, and its value on the day before the largest tranche vests.
- **Facts to verify.** Book 16 ch. 11 pointers for the law; FTC non-compete rule set aside (Ryan LLC v. FTC, N.D. Tex., 20 August 2024) and the FTC's withdrawal of its appeal (2025) (dated); UK government statements on limiting non-competes (2023 onward) and their status (dated); California Business and Professions Code s. 16600-16600.5; Illinois Freedom to Work Act thresholds; New York proposals (dated); UK statutory minimum notice, Employment Rights Act 1996 s. 86; firms' published graduate and internship programmes (dated).
- **Data.** Illustrative parameters (labelled).
