# 12. Revenue and Profit across the Industry — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Put every firm that files side by side and revenue per employee ranges over more than a factor of ten, from bank markets divisions and asset managers at the low end to the most profitable private market makers; and within a firm it can double from a quiet year to a volatile one. The table is the industry's income, and its gaps are the firms that file nothing.
- **Sections.** A sourced table of filed numbers; Per head: what it measures and what it hides; Volatility regimes: how results move with the market; Who is missing: survivorship and disclosure bias.
- **Defines.** revenue per head, profit per head, disclosure bias.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** net trading revenue (B16.1), compensation ratio (B16.1), operating margin (B16.1), non-GAAP measure (B16.1), return on equity (B16.1), survivorship bias (B7.3), staff costs (ch11), average headcount (ch11), statutory accounts (ch11), regulatory assets under management (ch4), firm profile (ch2).
- **Tutorial.** Build the industry table: data/industry/filings_perhead.csv (about twenty filers, 2019-2025, from ch. 11's snapshots, Book 16's derived tables and new filings) read with firm.industrypnl; normalise currencies and inflation; regress log revenue per head on the VIX yearly mean with firm fixed effects, by firm type. End state: a chart of revenue per head by firm type across years (log axis) and a table of elasticities with standard errors.
- **Build.** `firm.industrypnl`: a firm-year panel with per-head metrics, currency and inflation normalisation from sourced series, fixed-effects regression on a volatility measure, ranges by firm type and year; Python; wraps firm.firmecon and firm.filings.
- **Weekend problem.** The good year -- named result: the elasticity of revenue per head to the VIX yearly mean by firm type, with its standard error, and the range of the 2020 to 2023 revenue-per-head ratio across market makers.
- **Facts to verify.** filings per firm: Virtu, Flow Traders, the UK accounts of private market makers (ch. 2), Man Group, exchange groups (ch. 9), bank markets segments (ch. 7), 2019-2025 (dated); Cboe VIX index history as derived annual means (Book 16's table); annual average FX (Fed H.10, ECB) and CPI (BLS via FRED); press reports of private firms' revenues (context only, dated; never in the table).
- **Data.** data/industry/filings_perhead.csv (derived, source per cell).

- Bank markets divisions: no division headcount is filed; banks enter as whole firms only.
- Operating profit per head for the whole panel: only four examples are computed (section 2); the lines differ too much between kinds of firm to pool.
