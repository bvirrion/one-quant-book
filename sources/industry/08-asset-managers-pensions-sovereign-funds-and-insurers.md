# 8. Asset Managers, Pensions, Sovereign Funds and Insurers — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Norway's sovereign fund manages well over a trillion dollars with about seven hundred employees and reports its management costs at a few basis points of the fund; a pension fund of a tenth of the size may employ more people, because it manages more in-house. On the asset-owner side, quants sit where the owner has decided to do the work itself.
- **Sections.** Asset owners and their in-house teams; Where quants sit: allocation, risk, liability matching, factor and index engineering; Long-only quantitative managers; Insurers: asset-liability management and the actuarial quant; Pace, pay and stability on the asset-owner side.
- **Defines.** asset owner, sovereign wealth fund, defined-benefit pension plan, internal management.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** asset manager (B1.1), mandate (B1.3), benchmark (B1.3), passive management (B1.3), active management (B1.3), tracking error (B1.3), liability-driven investment (B2.7), factor product (B8.29), asset--liability management (B6.24), management fee (B1.3), assets under management (B1.1).
- **Tutorial.** A derived table from about eight asset owners' annual reports (assets, employees, operating costs, share managed internally): firm.ownercost computes cost in basis points, staff per $10bn, and a model of internal against external management cost (team size and staff cost per head against external fee rates). End state: a chart of cost in basis points against the share managed internally, and the break-even table.
- **Build.** `firm.ownercost`: asset-owner cost model (internal team cost as headcount times staff cost per head plus systems, against external fees on the same assets), the break-even mandate size, and sensitivity to the fee rate; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Build or hire the team -- named result: the mandate size above which internal management is cheaper than an external fee of 10 to 40 basis points, for staff cost per head in the range the chapter's filings give.
- **Facts to verify.** NBIM (Norges Bank Investment Management) annual report 2025: market value, employees, management cost ratio (dated); CPP Investments annual report FY2026: net assets, employees, operating expenses (dated); Ontario Teachers', APG/ABP, CalPERS, Australia's Future Fund, GPIF annual reports: assets, staff, costs, internal share (dated); Thinking Ahead Institute Global Pension Assets Study (press summary; dated); EIOPA insurance statistics: investments of EU insurers (dated); International Forum of Sovereign Wealth Funds / Santiago Principles definition of a sovereign wealth fund.
- **Data.** data/industry/asset_owners.csv (derived, source per row).
