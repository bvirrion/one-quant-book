# 3. The Options Market-Making Houses — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Three of today's largest global options market makers trace their start to a single trading floor, the Amsterdam options exchange opened in 1978; others grew out of the Chicago Board Options Exchange's floor (1973) and the Philadelphia Stock Exchange's. The floor's lesson -- price every strike, hold the book, hedge the risk -- still shapes how these firms hire and train.
- **Sections.** Three lineages: Amsterdam, Chicago, Philadelphia; Training programmes as the firms describe them; How an options house differs from an electronic delta-one firm; Size and footprint.
- **Defines.** options market-making house, spin-out.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** options market maker (B1.24), quoting obligation (B1.24), market-maker appointment (B1.30), lead market maker (B1.19), mass quote (B11.19), live surface fit (B11.19), open outcry (ch2), floor trader (ch2), firm profile (ch2), proprietary trading firm (B1.1).
- **Tutorial.** Two public views of the business: (i) the lineage graph -- firms and founders' former employers as sourced edges (founded-by, spun-out-of) with firm.lineage, drawn as a directed graph with founding years; (ii) the market-maker share of US listed options volume over time from OCC's volume-by-account-type statistics (fallback: an exchange's own published origin statistics), as derived annual shares. End state: the lineage graph and the share chart.
- **Build.** `firm.lineage`: a sourced genealogy of trading firms (nodes with founding year and place; edges founded-by, spun-out-of, acquired-by, each with a ledger id), queries (ancestors, descendants, founding-decade counts), and a TikZ/CSV exporter; Python; reused in chapter 28.
- **Weekend problem.** Three floors, one screen -- named result: the market makers' share of US listed options contracts in 2010 and 2025 (range across months), and the share of the sampled options houses founded by people from the three floors.
- **Facts to verify.** European Options Exchange founded Amsterdam 1978 (Euronext history); Optiver founded 1986, IMC 1989, Da Vinci 2006 (firms' own sites); Cboe founded 1973; Susquehanna founded 1987 (firm site); Wolverine, Peak6, Group One, Akuna founding years (firms' own sites); the firms' published descriptions of graduate trading programmes (dated); OCC monthly volume by account type (customer, firm, market maker) (theocc.com; may refuse scripts -- fallback Cboe statistics); Cboe and Nasdaq options market-maker quoting obligations (pointer to Book 1 ch. 24); Optiver and IMC headcount and offices from their own publications or Dutch filings (KvK filings are paid: firm publications and press only) (dated).
- **Data.** data/industry/lineage.csv (sourced edges); data/industry/options_mm_share.csv (derived).
