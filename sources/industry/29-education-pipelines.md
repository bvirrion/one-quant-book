# 29. Education Pipelines — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** US universities award each year a few thousand master's degrees in financial mathematics and financial engineering, counted by programme code in the federal completions survey; the number has grown with the industry's hiring, and it is small beside the statisticians, mathematicians and computer scientists the same firms also hire.
- **Sections.** Degrees that feed each kind of firm; The specialised master's programmes: size, cost and placement; Doctorates and the academic route; Competitions and what they signal; Learning outside a programme.
- **Defines.** placement report, Classification of Instructional Programs.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** quantitative researcher (ch17), return offer (ch28), graduate programme (ch28), labor condition application (ch14), survivorship bias (B7.3).
- **Tutorial.** Count the pipeline with firm.edupipe: IPEDS completions by programme code and award level, 2010-2024 (public domain), for financial mathematics and engineering, statistics, mathematics, computer science and physics, beside HESA counts for the United Kingdom where openly licensed; and a sourced table of programmes' own placement reports, read for their response rates. End state: a chart of completions by field (log axis) and the placement-report table with its caveats.
- **Build.** `firm.edupipe`: IPEDS and HESA readers, degree-count series by code and level, and a placement-report record (cohort, response rate, reported median, what the report excludes); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** How many quants does the world train? -- named result: annual US master's completions in the financial programme codes, and their ratio to the yearly count of LCA filings in quantitative titles at finance employers.
- **Facts to verify.** NCES IPEDS completions survey and CIP 2020 codes 27.0305, 27.0501, 27.0101, 11.0701, 30.7104 (dated); HESA open data licence and subject counts (dated); published placement reports of several financial-engineering master's programmes (dated; response rates); International Mathematical Olympiad, ICPC and Putnam formats (official sites).
- **Data.** data/industry/ipeds_completions.csv, data/industry/placement_reports.csv (derived).

- HESA (United Kingdom) counts by subject: the open-data tables were not fetched and their licence not checked in time; UK pipelines are described without numbers.
- Other programmes' placement reports: not collected; two reports stand as examples of how to read them, not as a ranking (no prestige rankings).
- ICPC and Putnam formats: the official pages render by script or refuse scripted access (403); not described in detail.
