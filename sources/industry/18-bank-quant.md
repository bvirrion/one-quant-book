# 18. Bank Quant — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In April 2011 US bank supervisors issued guidance requiring every model to be developed, validated and governed; the United Kingdom followed with its own principles in force from May 2024. Each model now has an owner, a developer and a validator, and a bank's quant job title says which of the three it is.
- **Sections.** Desk strategist; Library quant; Risk quant; Model validator; Valuation-adjustment quant; Who they answer to and what they ship.
- **Defines.** desk strategist, library quant, risk quant, model validator.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** model validation (B6.26), model risk management (B6.26), model inventory (B6.26), model tiering (B6.26), effective challenge (B6.26), three lines of defence (B6.26), XVA desk (B6.20), internal models approach (B6.23), product control (B6.27), model owner (B12.28), labor condition application (ch14), prevailing wage (ch14), wage level (ch14).
- **Tutorial.** The role's pay evidence: LCA base-salary ranges (10th-90th percentile, median) for the role family by employer type and wage level from data/industry/lca_ranges.csv through firm.paydata, cells under ten filings suppressed, beside the OEWS range for the matching occupation in the securities industry. Then the validation workload with firm.roles (wrapping firm.modelval and firm.modelcard): an inventory of models by tier, review frequency and hours per review as labelled parameters anchored to the public guidance, and the validator headcount it implies. End state: the pay-range chart by bank-quant family and the headcount curve.
- **Build.** `firm.roles` (increment): the four bank-quant role cards, a reporting-line graph with an independence check (a validator must not report to the model's owner), and the validation-workload model; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Who validates the validators? -- named result: the validator headcount for an inventory of 1,000 models split by tier under the chapter's review cycle, and its sensitivity to the share of tier-one models.
- **Facts to verify.** Federal Reserve SR 11-7 / OCC 2011-12 (April 2011); PRA SS1/23, model risk management principles, effective 17 May 2024; ECB guide to internal models (dated); Basel FRTB internal models approach (pointer Book 6 ch. 23); banks' own descriptions of strat and quantitative-research groups (firm publications; dated).
- **Data.** data/industry/lca_ranges.csv.

## EXCLUDED

- Any bank's model count, validator headcount or hours per validation: no bank publishes them in a form this chapter could cite; the workload model's inventory, tier probabilities and hours are illustrative parameters, labelled as such.
- Split of the LCA filings into the four bank-quant families (strats, library, risk, validation): would need job titles, which the one LCA pass did not keep (decision 5: the derivation runs once); the chapter uses the 'quant researcher' and 'risk' families at banks and says so.
- FRTB implementation dates by jurisdiction: volatile and outside the role; pointer to Book 6, chapter 23.
- JPMorgan Quantitative Research's own description (careers.jpmorgan.com redirects; no archived copy): not quoted.
