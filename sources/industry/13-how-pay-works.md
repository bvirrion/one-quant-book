# 13. How Pay Works — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** New York's State Comptroller estimates each spring the average bonus paid in the city's securities industry, on top of salaries; the figure has swung by more than a third between consecutive years. An average bonus hides the structure that decides what pay is worth to the person who receives it: how much is fixed, how much is deferred, and what is lost on leaving.
- **Sections.** Base and bonus: the two-part structure and why it exists; Formulaic and discretionary pay from the employee's side; Deferral, vesting, forfeiture and clawback: what deferred pay is worth; Sign-ons, guarantees and buyouts; Partnership, equity and carried interest; Structure by firm type.
- **Defines.** base salary, total compensation, sign-on bonus, guaranteed bonus, forfeiture, good-leaver provision, restricted stock unit, carried interest, partnership share.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** bonus pool (B16.10), formulaic payout (B16.10), discretionary bonus (B16.10), deferred compensation (B16.10), vesting schedule (B16.10), malus (B16.10), clawback (B16.10), material risk taker (B16.10), deferral buyout (B16.10), payout rate (B16.3), high-water mark (B1.3), performance fee (B1.3), compensation ratio (B16.1), limited liability partnership (B16.2), members' capital (B16.2), certainty equivalent (B4.9), relative risk aversion (B4.9), utility function (B4.9), members' remuneration (ch11).
- **Tutorial.** Value three stylised offers with firm.payoffer -- a bank role with a share of variable pay deferred in stock over four years, a market-maker role with base and discretionary bonus, a platform analyst's package with a payout share, a sign-on and a one-year guarantee -- with all parameters labelled illustrative and ranged where a public source exists: expected present value, the 10th-90th percentile range, the value forfeited on leaving in each year, and the certainty equivalent under CRRA utility. End state: box plots of the three distributions and a table of certainty equivalents.
- **Build.** `firm.payoffer`: a pay package as data (base, bonus distribution, deferral fraction and vesting schedule, forfeiture on leaving, good-leaver terms, sign-on, guarantee, clawback trigger, partnership share or carried interest), present value and distribution by simulation with a leaving hazard, certainty equivalent (CRRA), and the buyout needed to make a move neutral; Python; reused in ch. 22, 28 and 30 (and available to Book 18 ch. 7).
- **Weekend problem.** Three offers, one choice -- named result: each offer's certainty equivalent at relative risk aversion 1 and 3, and the deferral buyout that makes moving in year two neutral.
- **Facts to verify.** New York State Comptroller: securities industry bonus estimate for 2025 and its history (dated); EU CRD V deferral and instrument rules and UK PRA/FCA remuneration rules after PS9/23 and the 2025 deferral changes (pointer to Book 16 ch. 10; dated); US bank proxy statements or 10-Ks describing firm-wide deferral policies for employees (firm policy only, never named executives) (dated); UK carried-interest tax reform from April 2025/2026 and US IRC s. 1061 three-year holding period (dated); press ranges of platform payout percentages (dated; context only); US RSU taxation at vesting (IRC s. 83) (dated).
- **Data.** Illustrative parameters (labelled), ranged by the ledger's sources.
