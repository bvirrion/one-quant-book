# 6. Medium-Frequency Proprietary Shops — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Most proprietary trading firms are small. FINRA's annual industry snapshot counts broker-dealers by size and business line, and the firms that trade only for their own account are overwhelmingly firms of a few dozen people or fewer; the few thousand-person firms of chapter 2 are the tail of a long distribution.
- **Sections.** Medium frequency: horizons, capacity and infrastructure; The size distribution of proprietary firms; Newer entrants and firms that grew out of crypto; Profit splits, arcades and funded-trader programmes; What a small firm means for the employee.
- **Defines.** medium-frequency trading, trading arcade, funded-trader programme.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** proprietary trading firm (B1.1), statistical arbitrage (B8.1), strategy capacity (B7.28), members' capital (B16.2), own funds (B16.2), K-factor requirement (B16.2), dealing on own account (B16.17), formulaic payout (B16.10), principal trading firm (ch1).
- **Tutorial.** Fit the size distribution of proprietary firms: FINRA industry snapshot counts by size band and business type, and investment-firm class counts from the EBA/FCA reports where published, as derived tables; fit a Pareto tail with firm.firmsize (maximum likelihood on binned data, with its standard error) and compute the share of employment in firms under fifty staff. End state: a log-log size-distribution chart with the fitted tail.
- **Build.** `firm.firmsize`: size-distribution tools for binned public counts (binned Pareto and lognormal maximum likelihood, tail index with standard error, employment shares, goodness of fit); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The long tail -- named result: the Pareto tail index of proprietary-firm sizes with its standard error, and the share of the category's employment at firms under fifty people.
- **Facts to verify.** FINRA Industry Snapshot 2025/2026: registered firms by size and by business line (proprietary trading) (dated); EBA or FCA statistics on investment firms by class under IFR/MIFIDPRU (dated); CFTC v. Traders Global Group (My Forex Funds), complaint 2023 and outcome (court record) -- neutral, with the outcome; FCA statements on trading arcades or funded-trader schemes, if any (dated; else EXCLUDED); sourced examples of medium-frequency firms that grew out of crypto (firm publications).
- **Data.** data/industry/finra_snapshot.csv, data/industry/investment_firm_classes.csv (derived).
