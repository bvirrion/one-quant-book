# 14. Structural Credit Models — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In May 2005 General Motors' shares rose on a takeover bid while its bonds fell on a downgrade; hedge funds long the debt and short the stock lost on both legs.
- **Sections.** Equity as a call on the firm; Distance to default; First-passage models; Capital-structure signals and arbitrage.
- **Defines.** structural model, Merton model, distance to default, first-passage model, default barrier, capital-structure arbitrage.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** Black--Scholes model (B5.3), equity-to-credit model (B5.21), convertible bond (B5.21), reflection principle (B4.2), hitting time (B4.2), seniority (B2.21), credit spread (B2.21), hazard rate (B2.23), credit default swap (B2.23), implied volatility (B1.25), probability of default (ch13).
- **Tutorial.** Back out asset value and asset volatility from equity price and equity volatility, compute the distance to default and the model spread, and compare with a first-passage model on the same firm.
- **Build.** `firm.structural`: Merton and first-passage models, asset-value inversion, model CDS spread.
- **Weekend problem.** May 2005 — named result: the P&L of a CDS-versus-equity hedge sized by the Merton model when the equity rallies and the spread widens together.
- **Facts to verify.** Merton 1974; Black and Cox 1976 (papers); CreditGrades technical document 2002; GM and Ford downgrades of 5 May 2005 (rating agency releases/press); Kerkorian tender offer for GM shares May 2005 (SEC filing/press); Yu 2006 capital-structure arbitrage (paper).
