# 25. Margin Models — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In March 2020 initial margin at the world's clearing houses rose by roughly three hundred billion dollars in a few weeks, just when every firm needed its cash elsewhere.
- **Sections.** What an initial-margin model must do; Clearing-house models: scenarios, historical and filtered simulation; The standard bilateral model; Procyclicality and its tools; Backtesting margin.
- **Defines.** initial-margin model, standard initial margin model, schedule-based initial margin, initial-margin threshold, anti-procyclicality tool, concentration add-on.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** initial margin (B1.5), variation margin (B1.5), margin period of risk (B1.20), procyclicality (B1.20), portfolio margining (B1.20), scanning range (B1.20), default fund (B1.5), central counterparty (B1.5), uncleared margin rules (B2.10), credit support annex (B2.10), historical simulation (ch21), filtered historical simulation (ch21), VaR exception (ch21), bucketed sensitivity (ch3), margin valuation adjustment (ch19).
- **Tutorial.** Compute the initial margin of a swap portfolio by historical simulation, by a sensitivity-based model with illustrative parameters and by the regulatory schedule; replay a March-2020-type shock with and without anti-procyclicality tools.
- **Build.** `firm.initmargin`: historical and filtered-simulation IM, a sensitivity-based bilateral IM with illustrative weights and correlations, schedule IM, APC buffers.
- **Weekend problem.** The margin calls of March 2020 — named result: the IM increase with and without a 25 per cent buffer and a stressed-period weight, and the cash the member had to find.
- **Facts to verify.** BCBS-CPMI-IOSCO review of margining practices (September 2022): March 2020 IM increase; ISDA SIMM methodology (current version and calibration cycle, dated); UMR phase-in to phase 6 (1 September 2022) and EUR 50m IM threshold; EMIR RTS anti-procyclicality options (25% buffer, 25% stressed weight, 10-year lookback); PFMI coverage 99% (CPMI-IOSCO 2012).
