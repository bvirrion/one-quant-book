# 26. Model Risk and Validation — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In 2012 a bank's new value-at-risk model halved the measured risk of a synthetic-credit portfolio; one of its spreadsheets divided by a sum where it should have divided by an average.
- **Sections.** What model risk is and how it enters; Supervisory expectations; The validation report; Inventories, tiering and limitations; Benchmarking and outcomes analysis.
- **Defines.** model risk, model risk management, model validation, effective challenge, model inventory, model tiering, benchmark model, outcomes analysis, post-model adjustment, three lines of defence.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** value at risk (ch21), VaR exception (ch21), P\&L attribution (B1.7), Hull--White model (ch7), trinomial tree (B5.22), Monte Carlo (B4.26), model reserve (B5.27).
- **Tutorial.** Validate chapter 7's tree pricer against an independent Monte Carlo and the closed form over a grid of parameters, record tolerances and failures, and write the findings in the structure of a validation report.
- **Build.** `firm.modelval`: model inventory records, tiering rules, benchmarking harness with tolerance grids and outcome tests.
- **Weekend problem.** The averaging error — named result: the VaR reduction produced on a synthetic tranche book by dividing a rate change by the sum of two rates instead of their average.
- **Facts to verify.** Fed/OCC SR 11-7 (April 2011); PRA SS1/23 (May 2023, effective 17 May 2024); JPMorgan Management Task Force report on CIO losses (January 2013); ECB guide to internal models (latest revision, dated); any later revision or rescission of US model-risk guidance (search latest).
