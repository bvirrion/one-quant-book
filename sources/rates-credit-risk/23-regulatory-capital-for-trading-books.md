# 23. Regulatory Capital for Trading Books — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Each quarter a bank's trading desk must show that its risk model explains its own P&L; if the test fails, the desk falls back to the standardised approach and its capital can double.
- **Sections.** The trading book and its boundary; The standardised approach: sensitivities, curvature, default and residual risks; The internal models approach: expected shortfall with liquidity horizons; Backtesting and the P&L attribution test; Non-modellable risk factors.
- **Defines.** Fundamental Review of the Trading Book, trading book, banking book, sensitivities-based method, curvature risk charge, default risk charge, residual risk add-on, internal models approach, liquidity horizon, stressed expected shortfall, P\&L attribution test, hypothetical P\&L, risk-theoretical P\&L, non-modellable risk factor, risk-factor eligibility test.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** risk-weighted assets (B1.2), P\&L attribution (B1.7), value at risk (ch21), expected shortfall (ch21), risk factor (ch21), VaR exception (ch21), traffic-light test (ch21), bucketed sensitivity (ch3), Kolmogorov--Smirnov test (B4.12), Spearman correlation (B4.15 or B4.16).
- **Tutorial.** Compute sensitivities-based delta, vega and curvature charges for a small rates and FX book under the three correlation scenarios, then run the P&L attribution test on simulated hypothetical and risk-theoretical P&L.
- **Build.** `firm.frtb`: standardised-approach engine (weighted sensitivities, buckets, correlation scenarios), liquidity-horizon ES, PLA and NMRF eligibility tests.
- **Weekend problem.** The desk that failed attribution — named result: the desk's capital under internal models and under the standardised approach, and the Spearman and Kolmogorov--Smirnov values that put it in the amber zone.
- **Facts to verify.** BCBS minimum capital requirements for market risk (d457, January 2019): liquidity horizons, PLA thresholds, NMRF 24 observations; BCBS implementation date and each jurisdiction's latest date: EU CRR3 postponements, UK PRA, US proposal status (dated; search latest release); trading-book boundary rules (BCBS); Basel 2.5 stressed VaR (2009).
