# 15. Portfolio Credit — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In May 2005 traders long the equity tranche and short the mezzanine, hedged by every model they had, lost on both legs in the same week.
- **Sections.** Default correlation and copulas; The one-factor Gaussian copula and the large pool; Pricing tranches: recursion, compound and base correlation; Beyond the Gaussian: fat tails and tail dependence; May 2005 and 2008.
- **Defines.** default correlation, Gaussian copula, Student-t copula, large homogeneous pool, compound correlation, tranche delta, super senior tranche.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** tranche (B2.24), attachment point (B2.24), detachment point (B2.24), base correlation (B2.24), credit index (B2.24), index series (B2.24), equity tranche (B2.25), collateralised loan obligation (B2.25), hazard rate (B2.23), survival probability (B2.23), copula (B4.15), tail dependence (B4.15), Monte Carlo (B4.26), loss given default (ch13).
- **Tutorial.** Price index tranches by the large-pool formula and by exact recursion, imply compound and base correlations from synthetic quotes, and show where compound correlation has no or two solutions.
- **Build.** `firm.portcredit`: copula default simulation, loss recursion, tranche pricer and correlation implier; imports `firm.tranche` (Book 2).
- **Weekend problem.** Long equity, short mezzanine — named result: the P&L of the delta-hedged equity tranche when idiosyncratic spreads blow out and correlation falls.
- **Facts to verify.** Li 2000 'On default correlation' (paper); Vasicek 2002 loan-portfolio value (paper); May 2005 correlation dislocation (BIS Quarterly Review June 2005 or similar); 2008 super-senior write-downs (bank shareholder reports, public); Andersen, Sidenius, Basu 2003 recursion (paper).
