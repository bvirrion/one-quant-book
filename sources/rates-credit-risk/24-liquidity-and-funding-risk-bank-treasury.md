# 24. Liquidity and Funding Risk; Bank Treasury — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 9 March 2023 depositors withdrew 42 billion dollars from Silicon Valley Bank in one day, and asked for about a hundred billion more the next morning.
- **Sections.** Liquidity ratios; Funds-transfer pricing; Asset-liability management and rate risk in the banking book; Deposits: the behavioural model; The 2023 regional-bank failures.
- **Defines.** liquidity coverage ratio, high-quality liquid assets, net stable funding ratio, funds-transfer pricing, asset--liability management, interest-rate risk in the banking book, economic value of equity, net interest income sensitivity, non-maturity deposit, deposit beta, held-to-maturity portfolio, bank run.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** reserves (B2.1), standing facility (B2.1), repurchase agreement (B1.6), haircut (B1.6), modified duration (B2.3), bail-in (B2.26), liquidity spiral (B1.31), banking book (ch23), trading book (ch23), key-rate duration (ch3).
- **Tutorial.** Compute the economic-value and net-interest-income sensitivities of a stylised bank balance sheet under the supervisory shocks, its LCR, and the deposit outflow that exhausts its liquid assets.
- **Build.** `firm.alm`: balance-sheet cash-flow ladder, EVE and NII under shock scenarios, LCR and NSFR calculators, FTP curve.
- **Weekend problem.** March 2023 — named result: the held-to-maturity unrealised loss as a share of capital and the one-day outflow at which the stylised bank runs out of liquid assets.
- **Facts to verify.** SVB outflows 9-10 March 2023 (Fed Barr review, April 2023); SVB HTM unrealised losses end-2022 (10-K); Signature Bank and First Republic failures (FDIC); BTFP announced 12 March 2023 (Fed); BCBS LCR (2013) and NSFR (2014) standards; BCBS IRRBB standard 2016 and the supervisory outlier test (latest, dated).
