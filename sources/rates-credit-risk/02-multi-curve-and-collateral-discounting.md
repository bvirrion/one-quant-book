# 2. Multi-Curve and Collateral Discounting — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In August 2007 the spread between three-month Euribor and the overnight swap rate for the same three months jumped from under ten basis points to over sixty, and every single-curve pricer on the Street became wrong overnight.
- **Sections.** The 2007 break and the tenor basis; Discount curves and projection curves; Collateral discounting and the credit support annex; Collateral choice and cross-currency collateral; After the interbank rates: the 2020 discounting switches.
- **Defines.** projection curve, discount curve, multi-curve framework, tenor basis, tenor basis swap, collateral rate, CSA discounting, collateral choice option, price alignment interest, discounting switch.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** interbank offered rate (B2.1), overnight index swap (B2.9), credit support annex (B2.10), cross-currency basis (B2.16), cross-currency basis swap (B2.16), variation margin (B1.5), central counterparty (B1.5), fallback spread (B2.1), cheapest-to-deliver (B2.6), numeraire (B4.5), forward measure (B4.5), pillar (ch1), curve calibration (ch1).
- **Tutorial.** Build a six-month Euribor projection curve on an euro short-term rate discount curve; price the same swap under Euribor-flat, OIS and dollar-collateral discounting and explain each difference.
- **Build.** `firm.multicurve`: discount curve per collateral currency and projection curve per index, dual-curve swap pricer; imports `firm.curvebuild`.
- **Weekend problem.** The discounting-switch weekend — named result: the value transfer on a 30-year receiver swap at the October 2020 SOFR switch and the basis-swap hedge that neutralised it.
- **Facts to verify.** Euribor-EONIA basis in 2007-08 (ECB/BIS papers); LCH and CME euro switch to €STR discounting and PAI (July 2020); SOFR discounting switch of 16-17 October 2020, cash compensation and basis-swap auction; EONIA cessation 3 January 2022; Piterbarg 2010 'Funding beyond discounting' (paper).
