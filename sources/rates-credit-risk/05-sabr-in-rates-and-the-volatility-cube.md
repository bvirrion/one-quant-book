# 5. SABR in Rates and the Volatility Cube — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In 2015 a desk asked its SABR calibrator for the volatility of a receiver swaption struck at minus twenty basis points; the formula returned a complex number.
- **Sections.** SABR in rates: normal SABR and the backbone; Negative rates and shifted models; Building the cube smile by smile; Interpolating in expiry, tenor and strike; Arbitrage in the wings.
- **Defines.** swaption matrix, shifted lognormal model, shifted SABR, normal SABR, effective lower bound.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** SABR model (B5.11), backbone (B5.11), static arbitrage (B5.7), butterfly arbitrage (B5.7), risk-neutral density (B5.7), Hagan formula (B5.11), volatility cube (B2.13), normal volatility (B2.13), Bachelier model (B2.13), swaption (B2.13), lognormal volatility (ch4), caplet volatility (ch4).
- **Tutorial.** Calibrate shifted SABR smile by smile to a synthetic euro cube on a 5x5 expiry-tenor grid, interpolate a missing section, and check the implied density for negativity in the low-strike wing.
- **Build.** `firm.sabrcube`: the cube as expiry x tenor x strike sections with shifted-SABR parameters, interpolation and a density check; calls Book 5's SABR formula through the frozen interface if it exposes one.
- **Weekend problem.** The minus-fifty strike — named result: the price of a deep receiver under shifts of 1 and 3 per cent, and the smallest shift for which the density stays positive.
- **Facts to verify.** ECB deposit facility rate low of -0.50% (2019-2022); broker shift conventions for EUR swaption quotes (dated); Hagan et al. 2002 'Managing smile risk'; Antonov, Konikov, Spector 2015 free-boundary SABR; ICE Swap Rate as the settlement rate (administrator).
