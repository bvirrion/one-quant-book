# 19. Funding, Margin and Capital Adjustments — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In January 2014 a large US bank took a charge of about one and a half billion dollars in a single quarter for the cost of funding its uncollateralised derivatives.
- **Sections.** Funding a derivative: the debate; Funding cost and benefit adjustments; Initial margin and MVA; Capital: counterparty and CVA capital, and KVA; The whole stack.
- **Defines.** funding valuation adjustment, funding cost adjustment, funding benefit adjustment, margin valuation adjustment, capital valuation adjustment, standardised approach for counterparty credit risk, exposure at default, CVA risk capital, hurdle rate.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** funding spread (B1.17), initial margin (B1.5), risk-weighted assets (B1.2), uncleared margin rules (B2.10), CCP basis (B2.10), credit valuation adjustment (ch18), debit valuation adjustment (ch18), XVA (ch18), expected exposure (ch17), expected positive exposure (ch17), CSA discounting (ch2).
- **Tutorial.** Compute FCA, FBA, MVA and KVA for the chapter 17 swap with initial margin from a simple sensitivity model, and decompose the full adjustment stack.
- **Build.** `firm.xvafund`: funding, margin and capital adjustments from exposure and margin profiles; reuses `firm.ccpbasis` (MVA) and `firm.exposure`.
- **Weekend problem.** The fourth-quarter charge — named result: the FVA of an uncollateralised ten-year swap for a bank funding at 80 basis points over the overnight rate, and how much a CSA removes.
- **Facts to verify.** JPMorgan FVA charge Q4 2013 (earnings release, January 2014); Hull and White 2012 'The FVA debate' (paper); Burgard and Kjaer 2011 (paper); BCBS SA-CCR standard (March 2014); BCBS revised CVA framework (July 2020); implementation dates of SA-CCR and CVA capital by jurisdiction (dated).
