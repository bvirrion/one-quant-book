# 29. Build: A Risk Engine — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** At six in the morning the overnight run must have repriced fifty thousand trades under five hundred scenarios, aggregated them by desk, and flagged every limit breach before the traders arrive.
- **Sections.** Architecture: market data, scenarios, revaluation, aggregation; Scenario generation; Revaluation: full, grid and sensitivity-based; Aggregation, hierarchies and limits; Testing a risk engine.
- **Defines.** risk engine, full revaluation, revaluation grid, risk hierarchy, risk limit, risk data aggregation.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** pricing engine (B5.28), market-data snapshot (B5.28), reference pricer (B5.28), bump-and-reprice (B5.4), value at risk (ch21), expected shortfall (ch21), historical simulation (ch21), delta--gamma approximation (ch21), Euler allocation (ch20), stress test (ch22), sensitivities-based method (ch23), bucketed sensitivity (ch3), position limit (B1.27).
- **Tutorial.** Run the engine on a thousand-trade book over historical scenarios, produce VaR and ES by node of the hierarchy, and compare full, grid and delta--gamma revaluation for speed and error.
- **Build.** `firm.riskengine`: scenario generation, revaluation through Book 5's pricing library (adapter on the frozen interface), aggregation by hierarchy, limits; Python driver with a C++20 aggregation kernel and a Rust twin.
- **Weekend problem.** The six o'clock deadline — named result: wall-clock time and VaR error of full, grid and delta--gamma revaluation on the test book, and the cheapest method within a 2 per cent error budget.
- **Facts to verify.** BCBS 239 principles for risk data aggregation (January 2013) and adoption status (dated); Book 5 pricing-library interface (code/firm/INTERFACES.md).
