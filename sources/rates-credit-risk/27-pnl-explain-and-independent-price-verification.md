# 27. P\&L Explain and Independent Price Verification — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Every evening a product controller must explain each dollar a desk made or lost; on the day a swaption book shows 400 thousand dollars nobody can account for, the search begins.
- **Sections.** Attribution: risk-based and revaluation-based; Unexplained P&L and what it reveals; Independent price verification; Reserves and the fair-value hierarchy; Prudent valuation.
- **Defines.** risk-based attribution, revaluation-based attribution, unexplained P\&L, product control, independent price verification, fair-value hierarchy, prudent valuation, additional valuation adjustment.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** P\&L attribution (B1.7), realised P\&L (B1.7), unrealised P\&L (B1.7), mark-to-market (B1.7), carry (B1.7), composite price (B2.22), gamma (B1.26), vega (B5.4), theta (B5.4), day-one P\&L (B5.27), valuation reserve (B5.27), bid--offer reserve (B5.27), model reserve (B5.27), parameter bid--offer (B5.27), hypothetical P\&L (ch23), cross-gamma (ch3), delta--gamma approximation (ch21).
- **Tutorial.** Explain a day of a swaption book's P&L by Greeks and by step-by-step revaluation, measure the unexplained part, verify the marks against consensus data and compute the prudent-valuation adjustment.
- **Build.** `firm.pnlexplain`: risk-based and revaluation-based attribution, unexplained-P&L alerts, IPV tolerance checks, AVA calculator; reads `firm.pnl` (Book 1).
- **Weekend problem.** The unexplained 400 thousand — named result: the missing cross term that accounts for the unexplained P&L on a big-move day, and the attribution after it is added.
- **Facts to verify.** EU prudent valuation RTS (Delegated Regulation 2016/101): 90% confidence, core and fallback approaches, EUR 15bn threshold; IFRS 13 / ASC 820 fair-value levels; JPMorgan CIO valuation-control findings (Task Force report 2013); consensus-pricing services (public description).
