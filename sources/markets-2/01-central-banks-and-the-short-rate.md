# 1. Central Banks and the Short Rate — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** At 14:00 New York time a one-paragraph statement moves every interest rate in the world; the overnight rate printed the next morning moved by exactly the amount announced.
- **Sections.** Reserves and the central-bank balance sheet; Corridor and floor systems; Open-market operations and balance-sheet policy; Overnight benchmarks and the end of the interbank offered rates.
- **Defines.** reserves, policy rate, corridor system, floor system, standing facility, open-market operation, quantitative easing, overnight benchmark rate, interbank offered rate, compounding in arrears, fallback spread.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** repurchase agreement, general collateral, settlement.
- **Tutorial.** Compound a month of overnight fixings in arrears with and without a five-day lookback; compare with a simple-interest average.
- **Build.** `firm.rfr`: overnight-rate compounding engine (in arrears, lookback, lockout, observation shift).
- **Weekend problem.** The month the corridor moved — named result: the interest a reserve-rich bank earns versus a reserve-poor one over one maintenance period.
- **Facts to verify.** Fed administered rates (IORB, ON RRP) and target range now; ECB deposit facility / MRO / MLF and the 2024 corridor narrowing; BoE Bank Rate and its reserves remuneration; SOFR, €STR, SONIA, TONA definitions and administrators, publication times; LIBOR cessation dates (FCA 2021 announcement; synthetic end 2024); ISDA fallback spreads (5-year median, fixed March 2021); Fed balance-sheet size and QT programme.
