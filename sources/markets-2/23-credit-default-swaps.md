# 23. Credit Default Swaps — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 10 October 2008 an auction of two rounds set the price of Lehman Brothers' bonds at 8.625, and every protection seller that adhered paid 91.375 per 100 of notional.
- **Sections.** The standard contract; Upfront and running; Credit events and determinations committees; Settlement auctions; The cheapest-to-deliver option.
- **Defines.** credit default swap, protection buyer, protection seller, reference entity, credit event, determinations committee, auction final price, standard coupon, upfront payment, hazard rate, survival probability.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** recovery rate, cheapest-to-deliver, credit spread, annuity, central counterparty.
- **Tutorial.** Convert running spread to upfront with a flat hazard curve and verify against the par spread.
- **Build.** `firm.cds`: CDS pricer, flat hazard (Python, C++20, Rust).
- **Weekend problem.** The auction — named result: the final price from a two-stage auction and each participant's settlement.
- **Facts to verify.** 2009 Big Bang (standard coupons 100/500, auction hardwiring); ISDA DC composition and role; CDS auction results (e.g. Lehman 8.625, GM) from Creditex/ISDA; 2014 Definitions; CDS notional (BIS).
