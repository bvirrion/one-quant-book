# 11. Inflation Markets — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A thirty-year bond whose coupons are adjusted for prices gained 37 percent less than it promised to protect, because its real yield had been minus two.
- **Sections.** Index-linked bonds; Real yields and breakevens; Zero-coupon inflation swaps; Indexation lag and seasonality.
- **Defines.** index-linked bond, reference index, real yield, breakeven inflation rate, indexation lag, zero-coupon inflation swap, inflation seasonality, deflation floor.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** yield to maturity, par swap rate, coupon.
- **Tutorial.** Compute breakevens from a nominal and a linker curve and estimate monthly CPI seasonality from index data.
- **Build.** `firm.breakeven`: breakeven and inflation-carry calculator with seasonality.
- **Weekend problem.** The linker's carry — named result: the monthly carry of a linker position in a high-inflation print.
- **Facts to verify.** TIPS conventions (3-month lag, CPI-U NSA, deflation floor at par); UK index-linked gilts (8-month then 3-month lag); OATi/€i and HICPxT; US CPI release data (BLS; free); real-yield 2020–2022 moves.
