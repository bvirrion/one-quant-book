# 14. The FX Market — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Nine trillion dollars' worth of currency changes hands on an average day, and there is no exchange.
- **Sections.** Currencies, pairs and conventions; Dealers, tiers and the over-the-counter structure; Primary venues and electronic networks; Single-dealer platforms and prime brokerage; What the turnover surveys say.
- **Defines.** currency pair, base currency, quote currency, pip, spot value date, cross rate, primary venue, electronic communication network, single-dealer platform, FX prime brokerage.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** market maker, prime broker, broker.
- **Tutorial.** Compute spot dates across holiday calendars and triangulate crosses with bid and ask.
- **Build.** `firm.fxpairs`: pair conventions, spot dates and cross rates.
- **Weekend problem.** The cross — named result: the widest arbitrage-free bid-ask of a cross given two legs.
- **Facts to verify.** BIS Triennial Survey 2025 turnover numbers; EBS and Refinitiv Matching as primary venues; CLS volumes; T+2 spot, USDCAD T+1; market convention ranking EUR>GBP>AUD>NZD>USD>....
