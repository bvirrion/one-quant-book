# 7. European and Japanese Government Bonds — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 28 September 2022 the Bank of England announced it would buy long gilts; the thirty-year yield fell more than a percentage point in a day.
- **Sections.** The euro area: one currency, many issuers; Spreads, backstops and the electronic dealer market; Japan: yield-curve control and its exit; The United Kingdom and the 2022 gilt crisis.
- **Defines.** sovereign spread, redenomination risk, syndication, Bund, gilt, yield-curve control, liability-driven investment, central-bank backstop.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** primary dealer, margin call, leverage, dealer-to-client platform.
- **Tutorial.** Build a spread monitor across euro-area ten-year yields and decompose one widening episode.
- **Build.** `firm.sovspread`: sovereign spread monitor.
- **Weekend problem.** The collateral spiral — named result: the yield rise at which an LDI fund's collateral buffer is exhausted.
- **Facts to verify.** BoE 28 Sept 2022 intervention size and yield moves (BoE reports); BoE FPC LDI buffer guidance (250 bp); BoJ YCC start (Sept 2016), band changes, end (March 2024); ECB TPI (July 2022), OMT (2012); MTS as EGB interdealer venue; euro-area issuance by syndication.
