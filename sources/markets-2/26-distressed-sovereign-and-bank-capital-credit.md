# 26. Distressed, Sovereign and Bank-Capital Credit — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** On 19 March 2023 a regulator's order wrote about sixteen billion Swiss francs of a bank's bonds down to zero, while the same bank's shareholders were paid three billion francs in the acquirer's shares.
- **Sections.** Restructuring and exchange offers; Sovereign debt: collective-action clauses and holdouts; Bank capital: contingent convertibles; The 2023 write-down.
- **Defines.** distressed debt, exchange offer, collective action clause, holdout creditor, pari passu clause, contingent convertible bond, additional tier 1, point of non-viability, bail-in.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** recovery rate, seniority, credit event.
- **Tutorial.** Value a distressed exchange offer against holding out, with a probability tree over outcomes.
- **Build.** `firm.recovery`: restructuring-offer valuation.
- **Weekend problem.** Hold out or tender — named result: the tender participation above which holding out stops paying.
- **Facts to verify.** Credit Suisse AT1 write-down CHF 16bn (FINMA); Greece 2012 PSI and CAC use; Argentina pari passu litigation (NML v Argentina); AT1 market size; EU single-limb aggregated CACs 2022.
