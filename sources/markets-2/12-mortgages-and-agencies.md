# 12. Mortgages and Agencies — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** When rates fall half a point, a thirty-year mortgage bond gains less than a Treasury, and its holders sell Treasuries to catch up.
- **Sections.** The agency pass-through; Prepayment; The to-be-announced market and dollar rolls; Negative convexity and its hedging flows.
- **Defines.** agency mortgage-backed security, pass-through, prepayment, conditional prepayment rate, PSA benchmark, to-be-announced trade, specified pool, dollar roll, negative convexity.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** convexity, DV01, repurchase agreement, cost of carry.
- **Tutorial.** Project the cash flows of a pass-through under several prepayment speeds and compute its effective duration.
- **Build.** `firm.prepay`: pass-through cash-flow engine.
- **Weekend problem.** The refinancing wave — named result: the effective duration at the money and the hedge a servicer must buy after a 50 bp rally.
- **Facts to verify.** Agency MBS market size (SIFMA); TBA good-delivery rules (SIFMA guidelines); PSA benchmark definition; Fed MBS holdings; 1994 and 2003 convexity events (public accounts).
