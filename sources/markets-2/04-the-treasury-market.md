# 4. The Treasury Market — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** At 13:00:00 the result of a ten-year auction appears on every screen; within a second the futures have moved half a point.
- **Sections.** Auctions and primary dealers; When-issued, on-the-run and off-the-run; Inter-dealer and dealer-to-client; Principal trading firms and the electronic market; 15 October 2014 and March 2020.
- **Defines.** primary dealer, uniform-price auction, bid-to-cover ratio, auction tail, when-issued trading, on-the-run, off-the-run, inter-dealer broker, dealer-to-client platform, flash rally, dash for cash.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** proprietary trading firm, central counterparty, clearing.
- **Tutorial.** Reconstruct an auction's stop-out yield, tail and bid-to-cover from a synthetic bid book.
- **Build.** `firm.tsyauction`: auction analyser (stop-out, tail versus when-issued, allotment).
- **Weekend problem.** Tailing — named result: the tail at which a dealer's auction bid loses money against the when-issued close.
- **Facts to verify.** Treasury auction process (uniform price since 1998), results timing; number of primary dealers (NY Fed list); Joint Staff Report on 15 Oct 2014 (yield range, timing); March 2020 dash-for-cash (FSB/Fed reports; Fed purchases); SEC Treasury clearing rule and compliance dates; on/off-the-run spread evidence; BrokerTec/eSpeed/Tradeweb as venues (public).
