# 27. Crypto Medium-Frequency Strategies — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In 2021 the Bitcoin perpetual paid longs' funding to shorts at thirty per cent a year; a book long spot and short the perpetual collected it, until the exchange it traded on failed.
- **Sections.** Funding and basis carry; Cross-sectional momentum in coins; On-chain data signals; Venue and custody risk.
- **Defines.** funding carry, basis carry, on-chain signal, venue risk.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** perpetual future (B3.17), funding rate (B3.17), cash-and-carry trade (B3.10), funding arbitrage (B3.17), backtest (B7.16), vectorised backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28), fundamental factor model (B7.24).
- **Strategy files.** perpetual funding carry; dated-futures basis carry; coin cross-sectional momentum; on-chain flow signal; funding-rate mean reversion; venue-diversified carry.
- **Tutorial.** Measure funding-carry returns from Book 3's Binance funding statistics, simulate a coin universe with momentum and venue failures, and size a carry book across venues with a failure probability.
- **Build.** `firm.cryptomf`: funding and basis carry accounting, coin cross-sectional momentum, on-chain signal scaffolding and venue-failure risk; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Until the exchange failed — named result: the funding carry's yearly return and the venue-diversified book's loss when one venue fails.
- **Facts to verify.** Binance funding statistics (as B3); Liu and Tsyvinski 2021, Risks and returns of cryptocurrency (RFS); Liu, Tsyvinski, Wu 2022 common risk factors in cryptocurrency (JF); FTX bankruptcy filings (as B3); Schmeling, Schrimpf, Todorov 2023 crypto carry (BIS working paper).
