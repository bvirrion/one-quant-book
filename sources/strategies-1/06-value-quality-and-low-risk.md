# 6. Value, Quality and Low Risk — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** From 2007 to 2020 the US value factor lost about half of its cumulative gains; the same years made profitability and low-risk books look like the only ones that worked.
- **Sections.** Value: book-to-price and its cousins; Quality and profitability; Low risk and betting against beta; Implementation details that decide the result; Combining the canon.
- **Defines.** book-to-price ratio, value strategy, quality factor, profitability factor, low-risk anomaly, betting against beta, factor timing.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** market beta (B7.22), leverage (B1.6), backtest (B7.16), vectorised backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28), fundamental factor model (B7.24).
- **Strategy files.** book-to-price value; composite value; gross profitability; quality minus junk; betting against beta; low-volatility long-only.
- **Tutorial.** Construct value, profitability and low-risk portfolios from point-in-time fundamentals on firm.synthmkt; measure the value factor's 2007--2020 drawdown and the profitability factor in the French data (derived statistics); show how the book-to-price lag and the rebalance date change the result.
- **Build.** `firm.factorlib`: factor construction by characteristic sorts and by characteristic-weighted portfolios (value composites, profitability, low beta with the BAB leverage adjustment) from firm.fundpit; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** The lost decade of value — named result: the value factor's drawdown from its 2007 peak in the French data, and the correlation of value and profitability returns.
- **Facts to verify.** Fama and French 1992 (JF); Novy-Marx 2013, The other side of value (JFE); Asness, Frazzini, Pedersen 2019, Quality minus junk (Review of Accounting Studies); Frazzini and Pedersen 2014, Betting against beta (JFE); Fama and French 2015 five-factor model (JFE); Kenneth French data library HML and RMW.
