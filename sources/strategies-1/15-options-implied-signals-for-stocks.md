# 15. Options-Implied Signals for Stocks — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** When a stock's out-of-the-money puts become expensive relative to its calls, the stock underperforms over the next weeks: someone is buying protection who knows something.
- **Sections.** Skew as a signal; Implied minus realised volatility; Option volume and the put--call ratio; Where informed traders trade.
- **Defines.** implied volatility spread, option-to-stock volume ratio, put--call ratio, informed options trading.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** implied volatility (B1.25), volatility skew (B1.25), backtest (B7.16), vectorised backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28), fundamental factor model (B7.24).
- **Strategy files.** skew signal; implied-volatility spread signal; option volume signal; implied-minus-realised volatility signal.
- **Tutorial.** Add an options layer to firm.synthmkt in which informed traders buy puts or calls ahead of planted news, compute skew, volatility spread and option-volume signals, and measure their predictive power for the stock.
- **Build.** `firm.optsignal`: synthetic option-market layer (implied volatilities with informed demand), skew, put--call volatility spread and volume signals; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Someone is buying protection — named result: the IC of the skew and volatility-spread signals and their decay with horizon.
- **Facts to verify.** Xing, Zhang, Zhao 2010, What does the individual option volatility smirk tell us? (JFQA); Cremers and Weinbaum 2010 deviations from put--call parity (JFQA); Pan and Poteshman 2006 information in option volume (RFS); Johnson and So 2012 option-to-stock volume ratio (JFE).
