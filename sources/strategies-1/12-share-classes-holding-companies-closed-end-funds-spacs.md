# 12. Share Classes, Holding Companies, Closed-End Funds, Blank-Cheque Companies — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A closed-end fund holds a hundred million dollars of listed stocks and trades at eighty-five million: the discount has lasted for years, and closing it requires someone to force the issue.
- **Sections.** Dual share classes; Holding-company discounts; Closed-end fund discounts; Blank-cheque companies and their trust value; Structural relative value, in general.
- **Defines.** dual-class share, holding-company discount, closed-end fund discount, special purpose acquisition company, trust value.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** net asset value (B1.14), mean reversion (B4.4), backtest (B7.16), vectorised backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28), fundamental factor model (B7.24).
- **Strategy files.** share-class spread; holding-company discount trade; closed-end fund discount mean reversion; activist-catalyst discount trade; SPAC trust-value floor.
- **Tutorial.** Model a closed-end fund discount as a mean-reverting process with catalyst jumps and a SPAC's payoff as a trust-value floor plus a call on the deal, and simulate the structural trades' returns and their tails.
- **Build.** `firm.structrv`: structural relative-value tools (class spreads, NAV discounts with mean reversion and catalysts, SPAC trust value with redemption option); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Eighty-five cents on the dollar — named result: the expected return and the time to close of a discount trade with and without a catalyst.
- **Facts to verify.** Lee, Shleifer, Thaler 1991, Investor sentiment and the closed-end fund puzzle (JF); Pontiff 1996 costly arbitrage: closed-end funds (QJE); Klausner, Ohlrogge, Ruan 2022 a sober look at SPACs (Yale J. on Regulation); SEC SPAC rules of January 2024 (dated).
