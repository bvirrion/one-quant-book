# 2. Short-Term Reversal — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Buy yesterday's losers and sell its winners: on the synthetic market the rank IC is 0.04 and the gross Sharpe ratio above 7; after costs in the 500 most liquid names, below zero. The whole strategy lives in how it is traded.
- **Sections.** Why prices reverse: paying for immediacy; Daily reversal: raw, residual, industry-adjusted; Intraday and overnight reversal; Costs: where the strategy lives or dies; The decline of reversal profits.
- **Defines.** short-term reversal, residual reversal, industry-adjusted reversal, overnight reversal.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** residual return (B7.6), break-even cost (B7.27), cost-aware optimisation (B7.27), signal smoothing (B7.27), backtest (B7.16), vectorised backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28), fundamental factor model (B7.24).
- **Strategy files.** daily residual reversal; intraday reversal to the close; overnight reversal; reversal as a liquidity-provision overlay.
- **Tutorial.** Measure raw, residual and industry-adjusted reversal on firm.synthmkt, then trade residual reversal with costs inside the optimiser and a fitted square-root cost model at three fund sizes; compare with the naive book of Book 7 chapter 16.
- **Build.** `firm.reversal`: reversal signal family (raw, residual on firm.riskmodel factors, industry-adjusted, overnight and intraday variants) and a cost-aware daily book on firm.tcost; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Seven before costs — named result: the net Sharpe ratio of residual reversal at $100m and $1bn with costs inside the optimiser, against the naive book's.
- **Facts to verify.** Jegadeesh 1990 (JF); Lehmann 1990 (QJE); Nagel 2012, Evaporating liquidity (RFS); Da, Liu, Schaumburg 2014, A closer look at the short-term return reversal (Management Science); Blitz, Huij, Lansdorp, Verbeek 2013 short-term residual reversal (J. Financial Markets).
