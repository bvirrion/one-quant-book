# 18. Asia-Specific Equity Strategies — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** A Shanghai stock hits its ten per cent limit up at 10:05 and stays there all day; tomorrow it opens higher, because the buyers who could not trade today are still there.
- **Sections.** Price limits and T+1 settlement; Retail-dominated markets; Connect flows.
- **Defines.** limit-hit continuation, magnet effect, northbound flow.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** price limit (B1.12), Stock Connect (B1.12), settlement cycle (B1.5), market-wide circuit breaker (B1.31), backtest (B7.16), vectorised backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28), fundamental factor model (B7.24).
- **Strategy files.** limit-hit continuation; retail-attention reversal; northbound-flow signal.
- **Tutorial.** Simulate a market with price limits and T+1 on firm.synthmkt's shocks, measure continuation after limit hits and the magnet effect near the limit, and study a foreign-flow signal.
- **Build.** `firm.limitmkt`: a price-limit and T+1 market layer (limit locks, delayed trading, magnet effect) and signals built on it; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Locked limit up — named result: the next-day return after a limit-up lock and how it changes with the limit's width.
- **Facts to verify.** Chen, Gao, He, Jiang, Xiong 2019 daily price limits and destructive market behavior (J. Econometrics); Shanghai Stock Exchange trading rules (dated); HKEX Stock Connect documentation (dated); Seasholes and Wu 2007 predictable behavior at price limits (J. Empirical Finance).
