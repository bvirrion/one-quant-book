# 20. Carry Across Asset Classes — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Borrow in yen, lend in Australian dollars: the carry trade paid for years, and gave back three years of gains in the autumn of 2008.
- **Sections.** Carry defined everywhere; Currencies, bonds, commodities, equity indices; Carry crashes; A diversified carry book.
- **Defines.** carry strategy, carry crash, diversified carry.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** carry (B1.7), roll yield (B3.10), forward points (B2.16), backtest (B7.16), vectorised backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28), fundamental factor model (B7.24).
- **Strategy files.** currency carry; bond carry and roll-down; commodity roll-yield carry; equity index dividend carry; diversified carry portfolio.
- **Tutorial.** Define carry as the futures return with prices unchanged in each asset class of firm.synthfut, build carry portfolios per class and combined, and measure crashes and their correlation with the stock crash; compute WTI carry from the EIA curve.
- **Build.** `firm.carrystrat`: carry per asset class from futures curves (forward discount, roll-down, roll yield, dividend carry), carry portfolios and crash diagnostics; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Autumn 2008 — named result: the diversified carry book's Sharpe ratio and its loss in the crash, against each class alone.
- **Facts to verify.** Koijen, Moskowitz, Pedersen, Vrugt 2018, Carry (JFE); Brunnermeier, Nagel, Pedersen 2008, Carry trades and currency crashes (NBER Macro Annual); Lustig, Roussanov, Verdelhan 2011 (RFS); Bank for International Settlements on the 2008 yen carry unwind (dated).
