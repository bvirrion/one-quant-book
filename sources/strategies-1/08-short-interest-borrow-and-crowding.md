# 8. Short Interest, Borrow and Crowding — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** In January 2021 a stock with short interest above its float rose twenty-fold in weeks; the short sellers' crowding became the trade.
- **Sections.** Short interest as a signal; Borrow fees and utilisation; Recalls, squeezes and the lending market's risk; Crowded shorts.
- **Defines.** borrow-fee signal, squeeze risk, short-crowding measure.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** short interest (B1.16), days to cover (B1.16), utilisation (B1.16), recall (B1.16), short squeeze (B1.16), lendable supply (B1.16), borrow fee (B1.6), crowded trade (B7.28), backtest (B7.16), vectorised backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28), fundamental factor model (B7.24).
- **Strategy files.** high short interest avoidance; borrow-fee signal; days-to-cover ranking; crowded-short risk overlay.
- **Tutorial.** Add a lending layer to firm.synthmkt (borrow demand from informed and uninformed shorts, fees rising with utilisation, recalls) and measure the short-interest signal, its cost after borrow fees, and the loss distribution of crowded shorts under a squeeze.
- **Build.** `firm.lendsig`: synthetic stock-lending market (supply, demand, fee as a function of utilisation, recalls) and short-interest and borrow-fee signals; Python.
- **Weekend problem.** January 2021 — named result: the return of the high-short-interest signal before and after borrow fees, and the loss of a crowded short book in a simulated squeeze.
- **Facts to verify.** Asquith, Pathak, Ritter 2005 short interest and institutional ownership (JFE); Boehmer, Jones, Zhang 2008, Which shorts are informed? (JF); Engelberg, Reed, Ringgenberg 2018, Short-selling risk (JF); SEC staff report on equity and options market structure conditions in early 2021 (October 2021); FINRA short interest reporting (dated).
