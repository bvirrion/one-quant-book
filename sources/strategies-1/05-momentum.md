# 5. Momentum — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** From March to May 2009 the US momentum factor lost more than forty per cent, as the losers it was short rebounded from the crisis lows; its long-run Sharpe ratio had looked like the steadiest in equities.
- **Sections.** Cross-sectional momentum; Residual and industry momentum; Intraday and time-of-day momentum; Crashes and their management; Implementation: turnover, skipping a month, costs.
- **Defines.** cross-sectional momentum, residual momentum, industry momentum, momentum crash, volatility-scaled momentum.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** comomentum (B7.28), skip period (B7.7), backtest (B7.16), vectorised backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28), fundamental factor model (B7.24).
- **Strategy files.** 12-1 cross-sectional momentum; residual momentum; industry momentum; intraday momentum in the last half hour; volatility-scaled momentum.
- **Tutorial.** Build momentum portfolios on firm.synthmkt and measure the 2009 crash in the Kenneth French momentum factor (derived statistics); scale momentum by its own recent volatility and compare drawdowns.
- **Build.** `firm.momstrat`: momentum variants (12-1, residual, industry, time-of-day) and crash management (volatility scaling, dynamic weighting on market state); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Spring 2009 — named result: the momentum factor's drawdown in 2009 in the French data and the drawdown of the volatility-scaled version.
- **Facts to verify.** Jegadeesh and Titman 1993 (JF); Daniel and Moskowitz 2016, Momentum crashes (JFE); Barroso and Santa-Clara 2015, Momentum has its moments (JFE); Blitz, Huij, Martens 2011 residual momentum (J. Empirical Finance); Moskowitz and Grinblatt 1999 industry momentum (JF); Gao, Han, Li, Zhou 2018 market intraday momentum (JFE); Kenneth French data library momentum factor.
