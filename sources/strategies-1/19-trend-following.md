# 19. Trend Following — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Over a century of futures data, a simple rule — long what went up over the last year, short what went down, sized by volatility — earned a positive return in almost every decade, and its best years were the worst years of stocks.
- **Sections.** Signal families: moving averages, breakouts, returns; A portfolio of trends; Volatility scaling; The long record and crisis alpha; The recent decade.
- **Defines.** trend following, time-series momentum, breakout rule, crisis alpha, volatility targeting.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** moving-average crossover (B7.7), continuous futures series (B7.2), backtest overfitting (B7.20), cross-sectional momentum (ch5), backtest (B7.16), vectorised backtest (B7.16), information coefficient (B7.6), transaction cost analysis (B7.23), capacity curve (B7.28), fundamental factor model (B7.24).
- **Strategy files.** time-series momentum; moving-average crossover portfolio; breakout system; multi-speed trend blend; volatility-scaled trend portfolio; trend on WTI crude futures.
- **Tutorial.** Build firm.synthfut, a synthetic universe of 40 futures in four asset classes with planted persistent drifts, carry, seasonality, volatility clustering and a stock crash; run trend portfolios of several speeds, volatility-scaled, and measure crisis alpha; run the same rules on the EIA WTI series.
- **Build.** `firm.synthfut` (synthetic futures universe: contracts with expiries and curves, spot and carry, persistent drifts, seasonal commodities, crash episodes; deterministic) and `firm.trendfollow` (signal families, risk parity across markets, volatility scaling, portfolio of speeds); Python.
- **Weekend problem.** Crisis alpha — named result: the trend portfolio's return in the simulated stock crash and its Sharpe ratio across speeds.
- **Facts to verify.** Moskowitz, Ooi, Pedersen 2012, Time series momentum (JFE); Hurst, Ooi, Pedersen 2017, A century of evidence on trend-following investing (JPM); Baltas and Kosowski 2020 demystifying time-series momentum strategies (working paper); EIA WTI futures data (as B3).

## EXCLUDED

- Performance of named trend-following funds or indices (for example the SG Trend Index) in 2008 or the 2010s: not fetched; the recent decade is told through Baltas and Kosowski's abstract only.
- The hook's "best years were the worst years of stocks": Hurst, Ooi and Pedersen's abstract supports good performance in 8 of the 10 largest 60/40 drawdowns, which the chapter states instead.
- The universe, drifts, crash episodes and cost assumptions (2, 1, 2 and 4 basis points by class) are the chapter's own choices.
