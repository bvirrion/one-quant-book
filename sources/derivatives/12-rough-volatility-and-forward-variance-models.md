# 12. Rough Volatility and Forward-Variance Models — brief and source ledger

## Brief

- **Hook.** Plot the logarithm of realised volatility's increments against the lag on log-log axes and the slope comes out near 0.1: volatility is rougher than any diffusion model allows.
- **Sections.** Forward variance curves; Bergomi's variance-curve models; Rough volatility; Path-dependent volatility; Joint calibration to index and volatility-index options.
- **Defines.** forward variance, forward-variance model, Bergomi model, rough volatility, rough Bergomi model, path-dependent volatility model.
- **Uses (defined earlier).** fractional Brownian motion, Hurst exponent (Book 4 ch. 17), volatility index, VIX future (Book 1 ch. 25), stochastic volatility model (ch. 10), Monte Carlo (Book 4 ch. 26).
- **Tutorial.** Estimate the roughness of volatility from simulated and published realised-variance data, simulate the rough Bergomi model with the hybrid scheme, and show the at-the-money skew term structure against a power law.
- **Build.** `firm.fwdvar`: forward-variance curve object (from variance-swap strikes or the log-contract strip) and a rough-Bergomi simulator (hybrid scheme).
- **Weekend problem.** The skew that will not flatten — named result: the power-law exponent of the at-the-money skew term structure fitted on the chapter's surface, and the Hurst exponent it implies.
- **Facts to verify.** Gatheral, Jaisson, Rosenbaum 2018 Quant. Finance 'Volatility is rough' (H near 0.1); Bayer, Friz, Gatheral 2016 rough Bergomi; Bergomi 2005/2008 Risk 'Smile dynamics II, III'; Guyon 2020 Risk, joint SPX/VIX smile calibration; Guyon-Lekeufack 2023 'Volatility is (mostly) path-dependent'; Bennedsen, Lunde, Pakkanen 2017 hybrid scheme; availability and licence of a public realised-variance dataset.
