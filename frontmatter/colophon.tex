\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{This book is education, not advice.} Nothing in it is
investment, legal, tax or regulatory advice, or a recommendation to trade any
instrument or to use any venue, broker or vendor. Trading involves the risk of
losing more than you put in. Strategies are described so that their mechanics
can be understood and tested; most published strategies have decayed, and no
result shown here is a promise of any future result.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Public sources only.} Every external fact in this book comes
from a public document: a rulebook, a regulatory or court record, a company
filing, a published paper, a conference talk or a firm's own publication. A
practice is attributed to a named firm only where such a source says so.
Facts that venues, vendors and regulators change --- fees, tiers, limits,
locations, programme terms --- are printed in boxes headed ``As of'' with
their month of verification: check the current document before relying on one.
