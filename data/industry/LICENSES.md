# data/industry — sources and licences (One Quant Book 17)

Small derived tables only. Raw files (LCA disclosure workbooks, OEWS and Form ADV archives, IPEDS files, filings)
are downloaded to a scratch directory, streamed, and never committed. Figures transcribed from public filings and
reports are facts reported by their publishers; each file names its source, and the chapter's source ledger
(`sources/industry/NN-*.md`) quotes the line. No publisher's text is redistributed. No individual's pay or private
details appear in any file: filings' named-officer and highest-paid-director lines are dropped at extraction, and
pay cells built from fewer than ten filings are suppressed.
