# data/desk — sources and licences (One Quant Book 16)

Small derived tables. Figures transcribed from public filings are facts reported by the companies (US SEC filings
are public records; the UK and EU companies' results announcements are public disclosures); each row names the
filing it comes from, and the chapter's source ledger (`sources/desk/NN-*.md`) quotes the line. No filing text is
redistributed.
